MX17 Micromegas: the detector as we model it

Every layer of one MX17 module, from the front window to the support plate, as the Geant4 model builds it (MX17ModuleGeometry.hh, the as-built spec). Click any part of a drawing to see its numbers, where each one comes from, and what we still need to know. Before we publish we want every number in this stack confirmed, or its uncertainty written down.

Stephan, and anyone who built these chambers: please check this. Every value carries a status. Unknown and Assumed are the ones we most need. The open questions list them in order of how much they matter. You can answer a question, comment on any part, or tell us about something we left out. Comments are saved with your name and everyone with the link can read them.
