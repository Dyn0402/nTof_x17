Part V

From a plane to an event

Which strips to look at, how wide a window to cut, which cluster is the muon, and how the two planes help each other decide.

25 · Seeding: the one place hits are allowed in

The fit needs to be told where to look. That is a detection question, and detection is precisely what the analyzer's hit finder is for. So wft/seed.py uses hits — and returns nothing but sets of channel numbers.

Rule No hit time crosses the boundary into wft. A Seed carries channels, a strip count, an amplitude sum and a count of strips in competing clusters. Nothing else. If a future change makes a hit time useful to the fit, that is the moment to re-read Part II.

The clustering deliberately reproduces the production selection, step for step, so that detection semantics — and therefore efficiency — stay comparable between the two chains.

Measured The significance floor was added on 2026-07-25 and it is the single largest efficiency change in this analysis's history — on the detectors it had never been applied to, within-5 mm efficiency moved det2 80.9 → 92.1 %, det6 44.5 → 71.2 %, det7 18.1 → 52.7 %, with core σ improving from 0.63/0.67/2.72 to 0.43/0.38/0.52 mm. It is a hits-chain fix that the waveform chain simply inherits.

26 · Cutting the window

extract_window() takes the seed's channels, converts them to position order, and extends pad_strips = 3 strips beyond the cluster on each side.

The pad is not padding. The sharing kernel reaches ±2 strips, so charge that belongs to the track is sitting on strips the hit finder never fired. If the window stops at the seed, the model predicts charge on strips that are not in the fit, and the only way to reduce that χ² is to distort the geometry.

27 · Choosing between candidate clusters

Production kept the largest cluster. That rule is wrong for about 5 % of events, and when it is wrong it is catastrophic rather than marginal: the true track sits a median of 37 mm outside the fit window, so the fit converges beautifully onto the wrong charge.

The forward fit can do better, because it can simply try each candidate and judge the results. Two criteria:

plausible
a real track's charge column crosses the whole drift gap, so it must last a few hundred nanoseconds and its transverse speed is bounded: 250 ns ≤ q_uend ≤ 1100 ns and |tan θ| < 0.6. Coherent noise and stray deposits rarely satisfy both.
Δχ²
how much better the model explains the window than "no signal at all": χ²_null − χ²_fit.
Selection rules, measured on the 224 det3 events where the largest cluster is not the muon.
rulemedian |p₀ − ref|within 5 mm
most strips (production)76.6 mm19 %
most charge47.3 mm32 %

best available candidate (the ceiling)0.4 mm95 %

Read the last row carefully: the right cluster is nearly always among the candidates. The current rule finds it about half the time. That is a large net gain over production and it leaves obvious headroom.

Open Figure 29 exposes a structural weakness in the score: Δχ² is computed inside each candidate's own window, so a bright compact cluster scores higher than a dimmer extended one regardless of which looks like a track. A scale-free score — Δχ² per degree of freedom, or a likelihood ratio against a track-free model on a common window — would remove that bias. The originally identified follow-up was a track-compatibility seeder; this is a cheaper piece of the same problem.

28 · Using the fact that the muon fired both planes

Single-plane selection cannot use the strongest piece of evidence available: a muon's X and Y charge arrive at the same time. Coherent noise or a stray deposit in one plane has no reason to be time-coincident with the track in the other.

select_pair() therefore scores every (X candidate, Y candidate) combination on whether t0x − t0y sits within 120 ns of the measured electronics offset, then on plausibility, then on summed Δχ².

Trap Pair selection is behind an environment variable — WFT_PAIR_SELECT=1 — and is off by default. Every number in this document was produced with it off. It is implemented and tested; it has not been through a full-fleet A/B, so it should not be switched on for a result without one.

29 · Where v_drift enters, and why that is the whole ballgame

Look back at the model. Nothing in it uses the drift velocity: the geometry is p₀ + w·u with u in nanoseconds, and the fit measures w in mm/ns. v appears in exactly one line:

tan θ = w · 10³ / v_drift    wft/reco.py, fit_plane

This is a good property and a dangerous one. Good, because the fit itself cannot be biased by a wrong v — w is a real measurement of the charge column's transverse speed either way. Dangerous, because a 20 % error in v becomes a 20 % error in every angle, with no other symptom.

Measured On det4, three calibrations differing essentially only in v: a free fit at v = 39.6 gave σθ 4.20°/3.36°; the same kernel with v pinned to 34.0 gave 2.63°/2.44°. The entire gap was v. Meanwhile the reconstruction's own implied velocity read 30–36 µm/ns in every angle bin, for every bundle — the data were saying so the whole time.
Rule A fitted per-detector v is a nuisance parameter unless it has been checked against an independent measurement — a drift scan, a Magboltz curve for the known gas and field, or the reconstruction's own implied-velocity plateau. §35 shows why the calibration χ² cannot be trusted to find it on its own.

One row per event, one block of columns per plane, written to Parquet with a sidecar .meta.json recording the calibration bundle, the run, and the selection parameters. That provenance is not decoration — a number from this chain is only interpretable if you know which bundle produced it.

Trap quality_ok is off by default in compat.load_table() and should stay off for anything the hits chain is compared against — the hits chain applies no equivalent cut. det3's median χ²/dof is ~156 on X and ~161 on Y; that is normal, not broken. What is applied by default is n_dropped ≤ 2, the cluster-quality cut, because unlike the hits chain's line fit — which simply failed on junk clusters and quietly removed them — the forward fit converges happily on whatever charge you give it.
