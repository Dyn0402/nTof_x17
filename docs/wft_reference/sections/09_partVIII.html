What the reconstruction is for

A large body of analysis machinery already existed for alignment, efficiency and resolution maps. It was written for the hits chain, but it acts on positions and never on hit times — so reusing it is not reusing the hits basis. wft/compat.py builds the objects it expects out of the event table.

47 · Alignment

Identical geometry procedure to the hits chain — iterate z, then in-plane rotation, then translation, on the clean-cluster subset — with a better input position. det3 lands at z = 714 mm, θ = 89.45°, and residual σ of 0.65/0.60 mm per plane.

48 · Efficiency

Rule Detection stays hits-defined on purpose. Whether the detector saw the muon is a property of the analyzer's trigger, not of the fit. Keeping that decision on hits is what makes this efficiency directly comparable with the old chain's. What the waveform fit changes is only where the point goes — which moves reco_near against reco_far, never has_any. An event rejected by the cluster-quality cut is not a detection failure; it is counted as "hit, no reco".

49 · Drift velocity and the gas

Because the fit measures w without needing v, a reference-pinned χ²(v) scan measures the drift velocity directly, at any field — including fields where the column takes longer than the DAQ window and every gap-based estimator breaks.

The forward series is smooth and monotonic and lies between the Magboltz curves for Ar/isobutane 95/5 with 0.3 % and 1 % water. Fitting the water fraction gives 0.80 ± 0.05 % with an RMS of 0.63 µm/ns — and the Saturday run was the driest of that week, on a bottle humidity trajectory falling from 3 % to 1 %. Two independent stories agreeing.

Measured v(1000 V) = 36.7 ± 0.3 (fit) ± 0.9 (model) µm/ns. The old geometry estimate of 34.3 was identified as floor-subtraction-biased; the old gap-filling 42 and duration-based 28–30 numbers were already understood. A toy study generated with a deliberately different sharing truth at v = 34.0 and run through the standard calibration recovered 33.1 — under strong model mismatch the calibration deflates v by ~2.6 %. It cannot inflate 34 into 36.6.

50 · The charge column, and the shape of the cathode

The deconvolved charge profile is an output nobody designed for. Its integral says how much charge arrived; its endpoint says how deep the gas was still delivering charge — which is a measurement of the drift column at that (x, y).

The careful version needs the reference-pinned profiles and a sparsity-aware estimator (§19.1's trap), and it produced a small saga worth recording, because it is a good example of a measurement correcting itself:

  1. Free-fit profiles with a per-bin median gave a 24.7 mm column against a nominal 29 mm gap. A "missing 4–5 mm" became the single dominant unknown, and disassembling a chamber was on the table.
  2. Two stacked estimator biases were found — the median truncating sparse profiles, and free fits trading slope against profile support. Fixing both moved the answer to 27.9 mm, stable for basis sizes K = 18…27.
  3. Closure toys with known step-columns recovered them to ≤5 ns, including toys built with a 2× deeper template undershoot. The chain cannot shrink a 29 mm column.
  4. The endpoint varies with position: det3's column runs 26.5–29.5 mm across the active area, a ~2.7 mm monotonic tilt along y plus a slight bow in x.
  5. A third chamber settled it. det2 reads 30.5 mm and its map is flat (29.9–31.5 mm). The method reads the full mechanical gap on a flat chamber.

All of it measured in situ, without opening anything.

Trap Depth-resolved analyses — attachment, efficiency against z — must use the local column, not a global 29 mm. Every gap-based velocity estimator inherits a −3 to −7 % bias from the same assumption.

51 · The beam campaign, and what transfers

The H4 beam test put det4 at normal incidence, which removes the degeneracy cosmics cannot break, and measured the kernel directly. Three results transfer to every chamber:

  • The kernel is a layer property. Invariant across a 6 % gain step, across drift fields of 92–233 V/cm, across gas (Ar/isobutane on the bench against a wet Ar/CF₄/isobutane mix in the beam), and across cosmics versus beam delivery. That invariance is the premise every calibration bundle rests on, and it now has three independent legs.
  • The structure is RC dispersion, not delay (§14.2) — ported into wft/model.py as share_mode='lp' and promoted to canonical for det4.
  • The measurement recipe is bench-portable. It needs decoded waveforms and a handful of near-normal reference tracks. No beam required. This is what breaks the det7-class degeneracy for chambers that will never see one.
Open

What does not transfer:

  • v_drift. The beam gas was 4–5× slowed by percent-level water. v must come from each run's own data.
  • The RC time constants, which are window-dependent (§14.2's trap).
  • kY. The beam's det4 value of 1.12 was flagged "indicative only" because the X view carried a tilt systematic — and that flag was correct. With sigma_p0 pinned physical, the bench data demand 2.1–2.2 on its own.

51.1 An unresolved observation

The ±1 sharing on the X view is asymmetric — the −1 side is consistently stronger — and the effect has the same sign in three independent datasets: det4 in the H4 beam, det4 on the bench, and det3 on the bench. Three different mounts on two continents. A mechanical-tilt explanation would require all three mounts to lean the same way.

Open Part of the X-view asymmetry is probably internal to the detectors — the X strips' position under the resistive stack, or anisotropic coupling in the layer. The consequence is concrete: the beam's tan θ_X = −0.015 ± 0.002 for det4 should not be read as a pure mount constant until this is understood, and det3's cathode-tilt story deserves the same second look.
