Part I

The raw material

What the chamber does, what the DAQ writes down, and the three corrections that turn a block of integers into something a model can be fitted to.

1 · The chamber, and why time means depth

An MX17 detector is a resistive Micromegas with a drift gap of roughly 29 mm above the mesh. A charged particle crossing the gap ionises the gas all along its path. The liberated electrons drift down to the mesh at a velocity v_drift set by the gas and the drift field — about 36.6 µm/ns for this run — pass through the mesh into a 150 µm amplification gap, avalanche there, and induce signal on a plane of strips at 0.78 mm pitch. Two orthogonal strip planes, X and Y, each read out by their own 512-channel front-end unit, give two projections of the same track.

The essential point is the one that gives the micro-TPC its name: because the electrons all drift at the same speed, arrival time is a proxy for the depth at which the ionisation happened. Charge from near the mesh arrives first; charge from near the cathode arrives about 800 ns later. A single detector therefore measures not just where the track crossed, but its angle — from one plane, in one event.

2 · What the DAQ writes down

Each front-end unit digitises all 512 channels simultaneously on a common clock. For every trigger it writes one block: 512 channels × 32 samples, one sample every 60 ns, so the window is 1.92 µs long. Each sample is a 12-bit ADC count — full scale 4096 — sitting on a large per-channel pedestal, and the model treats anything at 3550 counts above pedestal as clipped. The block is stored flat in a ROOT tree branch called amplitude, together with the event id and a fine timestamp ftst.

Trap The window length is a property of the event, not of the file. det4's 6-24 run mixes 32- and 37-sample events inside a single file. 2.1 Pedestal

Each channel has its own DC offset. It is stable over a run but differs channel to channel by tens of ADC, with visible steps at the boundaries of the 64-channel Dream chips. The recipe is the simplest robust one: the per-channel median over the first 300 events. A median rather than a mean because those 300 events contain real tracks, and a mean would be pulled by them.

2.2 Common-mode

After the pedestal is removed, what remains is not independent per channel. All 64 channels of a Dream chip move together, sample by sample, by an amount that changes from event to event — pickup, supply ripple, and on beam runs the beam itself. This is coherent noise, and coherence is what makes it dangerous: sixty-four channels stepping up together at the same sample looks exactly like a cluster of hits.

The correction is the per-sample median over each 64-channel block, subtracted from that block. A median again, so that the handful of channels carrying a real track do not drag the estimate.

2.3 Noise

What is left is the per-channel noise σ, estimated as 1.4826 × MAD over the pedestal events after common-mode subtraction. Every χ² in this document weights each sample by 1/σ of its strip, and σ is floored at 3 ADC so that a suspiciously quiet channel cannot acquire infinite weight.

Measured Post-CNS noise, det3: median 7.4 ADC on FEU 7. A muon's brightest strip in this run is typically 600–2500 ADC, so the core of a cluster carries a signal-to-noise of order 100:1. The reconstruction is not noise-limited, and Part VII §42 shows exactly what it is limited by.

3 · Channels, strips and positions

Channel number is not position. The mapping from FEU channel to strip position in millimetres is a property of the cabling and the readout board, and lives in the run's strip map (common/Mx17StripMap.py, driven by a per-campaign CSV). wft.io.strip_position_map() turns it into a plain array of 512 positions per FEU, with NaN for channels that are not connected to a strip of that plane.

Everything downstream works in position order, not channel order. This matters more than it sounds: the resistive sharing kernel couples physically adjacent strips, so "the ±1 neighbour" is defined by the position map, and a window cut in channel order would scatter the neighbours arbitrarily.

4 · The fit's actual input

The object the reconstruction consumes is small: a handful of strips, sorted by position, each with 32 samples and a noise σ. It is called a PlaneWindow, and it is the last point at which anything is detector-specific.
