\section{Gas: drift velocity and attachment}\label{sec:gas}
\sectionstate{partial}{\texttt{sps\_beam\_test\_26/analysis/cloud\_basics} \S16--27 (FINDINGS); notes \texttt{mx17-beam-attachment}, \texttt{mx17-sps-to-bench}}
\intent{The gas the chambers actually ran with, measured from the waveforms with one physics model and no free
loss rate: water sets the drift velocity, O$_2$ sets the attachment.}

\tbw{\textbf{Two velocities and the gap} (\cref{fig:twov}). The angle needs only the drift velocity ($\tan\theta=w/v$,
$w$ the fitted transverse charge-drift rate); the gap enters only through the duration of the drift column, $T=g/v$. So
the M3 ladder ($w$ against the M3 slope, no gap) and the timing velocity $g/T$ are independent, and their ratio measures
the gap: on chamber~A, 28.2\,$\pm$\,0.5\,mm over four fields ($\chi^2$ 4.1/3), against 28.19\,$\pm$\,0.19\,mm from the strip
extent of inclined tracks.}
\tbw{\textbf{Drift velocity against field} (\cref{fig:vE}). Chamber~A's 6-27 drift scan: timing velocity 17.7--37.2\,\textmu m/ns at
177--390\,V/cm and angle-scale velocity 2.55--38.9\,\textmu m/ns at seven fields, on one Magboltz curve for Ar/iC$_4$H$_{10}$
95/5 with 0.97\,\% [0.95, 0.99] water ($\chi^2$ 2.5/3); the angle-scale points sit within $-1.7$ to $+1.5$\,\% of it.
Magboltz~\cite{Biagi1999}.}
\tbw{\textbf{Water on the bench dries out over hours} (\cref{fig:drying}). Golden runs: B 0.69, A 0.97, E 0.42, C
1.00, D 0.48\,\%. Through the night of 6-26 chamber~C went from 1.47 to 0.76\,\% and chamber~D from 0.68 to 0.35\,\%, with one
time shape (excess over plateau, $r=0.975$); chamber~C also showed attachment in its first ${\sim}4$\,h (${\sim}600\to370$\,ppm
O$_2$-equivalent, $\times2$ model factor). No gas log exists; the velocity is the gas monitor.}
\tbw{\textbf{At SPS} (cross-check, \cref{fig:attach}): Chamber~E ran wetter, 1.68--2.13\,\% water, with 129--443\,ppm O$_2$
attachment, and its readout constants survive the attachment.}
