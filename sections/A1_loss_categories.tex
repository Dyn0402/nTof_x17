The 7.71\,\% of interior reference tracks that chamber~A does not detect (7.36\,\% in Run~1) fall into five groups.
The classes here count every interior reference track, including those with a second particle in the chamber, so
their fractions are larger than the single-track losses of \cref{sec:single}. Six of the classes are shown together
in \cref{fig:lossgallery}, and each has a display of its own below.

\textbf{The chamber itself: noise bands and discharges (0.72\,\%).} A noise band is a whole-window baseline offset on a
contiguous patch of strips, seen in the X and Y views together. The track itself is clean, but the view fails the
competing-cluster cut (\cref{fig:loss_band}). The pass censors the band hits, which brought the class in Run~1 from
1.43\,\% to 0.22\,\%. A discharge (0.50\,\%) lights up whole chips, which ring (\cref{fig:loss_spark_discharge}): a
real discharge has 113 strips over threshold and 220--230 at SNR\,$>$\,6, beyond the 50-strip veto that stops the
fit. It is not recoverable.

\textbf{A second particle or deposit in the chamber (3.68\,\%).} The largest group. When chamber~A alone sees two particle-like clusters in a view, three things happen. The reference
particle is fitted but the view fails the competing-cluster cut (1.15\,\%, \cref{fig:loss_multi_m3picked}), which
keeping views with two tracks (multi-track credit) recovers. The reconstruction fits the other, brighter particle
(1.51\,\%, \cref{fig:loss_multi_otherpicked}), which needs a rule to choose the triggering track and so cannot be
recovered without the reference. Or the two lie within the 12\,mm seed gap in one projection and are merged, with the
fit between them (0.46\,\%, \cref{fig:loss_multi_merged}); the joint two-track fit with charge corroboration recovers
a reference-matched track in 25 of the 146 pass-1 events of this class. A clean muon with an unrelated deposit
elsewhere in the plane can also trip the discharge veto (0.35\,\%, \cref{fig:loss_spark_muonother}), partly
recoverable by turning the veto off and relaxing the competing-cluster cut. A second deposit that is not
particle-like (0.21\,\%, \cref{fig:loss_multi_deposit}) is not recovered.

\textbf{$\delta$-rays made by the muon (2.61\,\%).} Three classes, one population of increasing size
(\cref{sec:single}): a $\delta$-ray along the track pulls the straight-line fit beyond 5\,mm (0.87\,\%,
\cref{fig:loss_delta}); a compact saturated blob tilts it (0.93\,\%, \cref{fig:loss_satblob}); and a shower at the
crossing (0.81\,\%, \cref{fig:loss_spark_shower}) exceeds the discharge veto, so that no fit is attempted. None is
recovered. A straight-line model cannot absorb a $\delta$ track, and for the saturated blobs neither censoring at
each channel's own rail nor a track plus a compact deposit in the forward model helps: on 350 saturated views of
Run~1, agreement with the reference moves from 71.4 to at most 72.9\,\%, because the deposit is a short $\delta$
track over 5--10 strips, not a point.

\textbf{The reconstruction (0.30\,\%).} A fit off with no second particle-like cluster or saturation flagged
(0.28\,\%, \cref{fig:loss_fit}), and a fit that failed in one view (0.02\,\%, \cref{fig:loss_headon}). The latter
was the head-on class before the pass (0.26\,\% in Run~1, 17 tracks: two-strip X clusters whose third strip falls
below the 10\,\% relative floor and are never seeded); the pass's head-on seeding recovers them, and the one track
left in Run~1 is not head-on.

\textbf{The reference and the geometry (0.37\,\%).} A position tail where the fit agrees with the charge but not with
the reference (0.17\,\%, \cref{fig:loss_postail}; about two thirds are tracks bent between the reference doublets),
and tracks at the edge of the other coordinate (0.20\,\%, \cref{fig:loss_edge}). These are not losses of the
chamber. The remaining 0.03\,\% (cuts, charge elsewhere) is not classed further.

Each display (\crefrange{fig:loss_band}{fig:loss_multi_deposit}) shows one real chamber~A event of the class in both
views: strip position against drift time, the amplitude of every sample above 4\,\% of the view's maximum, the reference
track extrapolated through the drift gap (dashed) and the production fit (solid). The event is the one used in the
loss-category note where it has one, otherwise picked by eye from six random events of the class.
