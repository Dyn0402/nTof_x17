## The amplitude-floor thread (Problem 1, 8-09 late) — coordination record

The floor (~×0.6, angle-independent) got its own campaign the same evening.
Full analysis record: `hv_slope/HV_SLOPE_2026-08-09.md` (+ the three
report.html under `~/x17/response_sim/hv_slope/`). Sequence and verdicts:

1. **Electronics scale ELIMINATED by config read-back.** The DAQ saves
   `CosmicTb_MX17.cfg` with each subrun's `raw_daq_data/` (a fact worth
   remembering — the practice lapsed for late-June runs, but 44 copies
   survive, Jan → 6-16-26, det1/3/4). All 44 identical:
   `Feu * Dream * 1 = 0x081F 0xD023` → peaking code 2 = 180 ns (FEU 1 = the
   M3 trigger is the lone code-4 exception), `Dream 6/7 = 0xAAAA` → 200 fC
   range = 10 mV/fC, with the 100 fC option commented out. The saturday scan
   archived no .cfg of its own (its `run_config.json` points at the DAQ-side
   template path), so this is inference from 44 agreeing copies ending 11
   days before the target, not a read-back — but a one-step gain-range error
   (the only discrete ×~2 on the data side) is dead.
2. **The floor is NOT one constant — HV-slope test.** Data d lnA/dV =
   0.449 ± 0.009 per 10 V vs Garfield 0.296 ± 0.008 (×1.52, ≈12 σ). Gain
   doubles every 15.4 V on the bench, 23.5 V in the sim. A single fitted gain
   factor is **not defensible**; the sim's α(E) is wrong. imon can never give
   absolute gain (46 pA of signal vs 1.126 µA standing current; the May-scan
   imon rise is leakage masquerading as a gain curve).
3. **The isobutane axis is closed** (gain wants ~4 % iso, slope caps at
   ~0.317/10 V for 1–12 % — opposite pulls; v(E) cannot constrain the
   fraction once the dished gap is profiled; and no June gas record exists —
   the MFC mixer postdates the run by ten days).
4. **Literature anchor (Penning is the live knob).** Bulk-MM Ar/iso Garfield
   comparisons from the Saha/Saclay group needed Penning transfer **80 %**
   (Bhattacharya et al., arXiv:1304.6228, 128/192 µm, Ar/iso 90:10) later
   revised to **40–50 %** with a better two-step method (arXiv:1605.02921),
   both above Şahin's ~40 % estimate (JINST 5 P05002) — and their Fig. 4
   shows rP fanning the *slope*, not just the scale. So our calib's
   "rP = 0.40 upper bracket" is really the literature's lower end, and rP is
   the only single knob that moves slope and gain the same direction. Even
   tuned-rP Garfield undershoots absolute bulk-MM gain (their fallback: the
   as-built gap is smaller than nominal — which raises gain but *shallows*
   slope, so it cannot fix ours). Their measured 90/10 slope ≈0.33/10 V sits
   between our data and our sim; their max stable Ar/iso gain 3–4×10⁴ matches
   det3's data-implied gain at 490 V (consistent with sparking >500 V).
5. **The slope hunt** (iso confirmation point, Penning ladder extended to
   rP {0.3…0.8}, meshfield-vs-uniform A/B) runs on the MX17_Geant side
   (T7 slope-hunt runner, commits c62dab0 → 9cb700d).

The same thread audited the T14 data legs and corrected the record
(`hv_slope/HV_SLOPE_2026-08-09.md` §4–5, all withdrawals stated loudly):

- **Both data legs are biased subsamples**: the θ window drives the widths;
  the reco-quality cut silently drops railed waveforms (data-Y saturation
  0.327 → 0.110 vs sim acceptance 0.954/0.989 — one-sided). **Rule: apply
  the θ window to both legs, never the rail depletion. Corrected targets:
  undershoot X −3.5 % / Y −9.8 % (never quote −12.0 % again); FWHM X 524 /
  Y 402 ns; rise p50 X 274 / Y 247 ns; frac <240 ns X 0.41 / Y 0.48.**
  Unselected, the detector's X/Y amplitudes are IDENTICAL (2606.5/2607.0
  ADC) — the X/Y amplitude asymmetry is sim-side. "Y unreachable by any β"
  *survives strengthened*; the Y-FWHM ×1.39 contrast and the Y demand-curve
  tension do not survive (window artifacts).
- **CNS excluded** for undershoot (exact software A/B — this run's
  decoded_root is RAW, FeuReader applies ped+CNS on read; ≤0.3 pp effect),
  and the sim's missing CNS emulation is not a material bias. An
  "amplitude-limited cap at −290 ADC" was **withdrawn** (railed-bin
  artifact); what stands: the undershoot fraction is amplitude-dependent
  (non-monotonic in Y), so **any β fitted to an undershoot median must carry
  the amplitude distribution it was fitted on**.
- **The deep Y undershoot is DETECTOR-side (resistive layer), not
  electronics**: the 6-26 overnight run breaks the layer/FEU confound —
  X/Y = −90/−291 (det3, FEU 7/8), −86/−436 (det6, FEU 3/4), −39/−314
  (det7, FEU 6/8). Every resistive side 3–8× deeper across three detectors
  and three FEUs. A response-model item (filed with mx17-geant-6b), not a
  calibration.

## Method notes that saved us repeatedly

Both legs through `t13_reco.py` at the same σ (seeding common-mode);
angle-match with `--theta-halfwidth 3.0` for inclined points (p5/p95 windows
collapse at 20°); never compare FeuReader noise re-characterisations across
legs (occupancy-inflated); rise metric is 10–90 % — never mix with 5→100 %;
pre-state falsifiers before looking; a claim measured in the wrong metric or
scoped to one angle gets withdrawn loudly, not quietly.
