## Controls

- **detn <-> L/R map**: **CONFIRMED** (17/17 illuminated bars are the hot channel under detn 1 = left, 2 = right). The README carried this as "per Dylan", unverified; the campaign lights one bar at a time, so it is now measured.
- **AR repeatability** (Y-88 on AR in 224588 and again in 224596): {'699': -0.54, '1612': -1.23} % per edge.
- **Far-source leakage**: rate ratio 0.882, shape ratio flat to 4.1 % — no spectral leak: the ratio is flat, the difference is overall rate (source re-placement between runs).
- **Background templates are not source-free**: the ring order is A-D-C-B and the sources always sit on opposite arms, so each unlit arm is adjacent to BOTH lit ones. Arm C's dark runs read [110.1, 114.1] % above the Y-only control on its two plastics. On a source bar the background is ~3 % of the spectrum, so this moves an edge by <~1 %; it matters for the weak same-arm and wall channels.
- **Saturation / pileup**: 122 channels flagged (sat_frac > 1e-3 or pileup_frac > 0.30); max saturated fraction 6.00e-05 — nothing is clipping. Worst pileup: run224595:WALD2 66%, run224590:WALB8 63%, run224590:WALB7 63%, run224592:WALC7 61%, run224592:WALC8 61%.
