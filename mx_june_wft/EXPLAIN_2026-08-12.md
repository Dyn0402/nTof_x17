## vd_estimators.png (F7)

One inclined X-plane event (event 27, tan θ_ref = −0.23) with the corpus's
"drift velocities" drawn where they actually act:

- **red** — per-strip rising-edge hit times and their fitted slope: the
  ladder estimators (1–3). On this event the ladder spans visibly *less* time
  than the fitted charge column — that gap IS the 20–30 % compression, and it
  reads v ≈ 45 µm/ns against the fit's 36.6.
- **orange bracket** — the column duration on the time axis: estimators 4–5
  convert it to a velocity only by *assuming* it spans a known gap.
- **blue** — the forward fit's track line. The fit never uses v until the
  final `tan θ = w / v_drift` line; v itself comes from the ref-pinned χ²(v)
  scan (6), cross-checked by Magboltz (7).
- Side panel: the seven estimators with det3 values and status, grouped by
  what they measure, plus the implied-v validator and the
  don't-draw-one-arrow caveat (the sim mismatch is angle-dependent,
  `ANGLED_LADDER_2026-08-09.md`).

## deconv_transfer.png (F8a)

Why per-event inversion is hopeless, in one spectrum: a typical central strip
(872 ADC) stays above the per-mode noise floor everywhere, but the thing
un-sharing must resolve is the **neighbour copy** — coeff 0.05 (X) / 0.15 (Y)
and RC-low-passed, so its spectrum crosses the floor at ~2.3 / ~3 MHz:
**12/17 (X) and 11/17 (Y) of the window's frequency modes carry no copy
information at all.** A naive inverse amplifies noise ×30 in exactly those
modes (right panel). Regularisation can suppress the blow-up but cannot
manufacture the missing information — hence "stable enough to display, too
unstable to measure from".

## deconv_scatter.png (F8b)

The same statement measured: 250 events, X plane, the 2-D Tikhonov
deconvolution (threading-display estimator, ported to the shipped model
tensors) with only t0 borrowed from the fit.

    per-event σ68 vs reference:  forward fit 1.0°   deconvolved ladder 2.8°
    median bias:                 fit −0.06°          deconvolved −0.58°

The ensemble medians track the diagonal (slightly compressed at large angle —
estimator 3's "biased low"), while per event the deconvolved angle scatters
~3× wider, with a bimodal residual. Ensemble fine, per event not — the honest
form of "deconvolution doesn't work".

## Talk-ready one-liners

  squeezed ladder or assume a gap; the forward fit is the only one that never
  divides by an assumption."
- "The neighbour copy the deconvolution must isolate is a 5 % amplitude,
  RC-filtered signal — two-thirds of its frequency content is below the noise
  floor of a single event. You can display the inversion; you cannot measure
  with it."
