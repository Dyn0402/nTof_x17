# RETIRE_C2GTC1_2026-08-21 — every product built on an inverted kernel is gone

**Decision (Dylan, 2026-08-21):** c2 > c1 is retired everywhere. No deliverable
may show a result from it again, and the campaign was re-run rather than
re-labelled.

This supersedes the recommendation in `R06_GATE_2026-08-19.md` §3, which was
"do not re-freeze on r06, do a per-plane ratio instead". That recommendation
was made on resolution grounds and it still stands **as physics** — see §4,
which is the cost, stated plainly, and §5, which is what should happen next.
The decision to re-freeze anyway is a decision about what we are willing to
publish: an amplitude ordering that cannot exist is not a fit result, and the
H4 beam measures the ratio directly at 0.45 ± 0.02.

## 1 · The gate — why it cannot come back

`wft/calib.py::check_kernel_ordering` refuses a bundle with c2 > c1 at three
points: `CalibrationBundle.load`, `CalibrationBundle.save` (so a refit cannot
write one either) and `model.use_calibration` (so no path reaches the forward
model). It judges the **effective** c2 — on a `c2_over_c1` bundle the stored
`c2` is 0.0 and the ratio carries it — which is the rule `build_matrix`
applies.

`WFT_ALLOW_INVERTED_KERNEL=1` downgrades it to a warning. Exactly two
legitimate users: `19_ratio_recal.py` and `20_make_ratio_bundle.py`, which read
the defective bundle as the seed they fit away from and now set the variable
themselves. Covered by `wft/tests/test_c2_gate.py` (6 tests).

## 3 · Two bundles that did not exist yet

`g_det3` and `g_det3_wknd` are mx17_3 keys with their **own** per-key bundles,
both inverted, and neither had an r06 sibling. Refit with
`19_ratio_recal.py --ratios 0.6` and written with `20_make_ratio_bundle.py`:

| key | c1 | kY | tau_s | sigma_s | sigma_p0 | Dp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| g_det3_wknd | 0.0509 → **0.0929** | 2.88 → 2.51 | 146 → 141 | 12 → 177 | 0.409 → 0.322 | 0.0134 → 0.0134 |
| g_det3 | 0.0502 → **0.0897** | 3.11 → **5.75** | 143 → 241 | 12 → **398** | 0.356 → 0.381 | 0.0004 → 0.0163 |

⚠️ **g_det3's refit railed on two bounds** — kY at 5.75 against a bound of 6.0
and sigma_s at 398 against 400 — and Dp moved by a factor of 38. Treat that
bundle as provisional: the fit is telling us the seed was far away and the
basin is not well determined. g_det3_wknd's is unremarkable and can be trusted
on the same footing as sat_det3's.

The general pattern across all of them: pinning c2 down pushes c1 **up** by
about 1.8× and sigma_s up by an order of magnitude. The model compensates for
the smaller ±2 copy with a wider, later ±1 copy. That is the trade R06_GATE
measured on resolution, seen in the hypers.

## 4 · The cost, stated plainly

From `R06_GATE_2026-08-19.md` §2–3 (paired bootstrap, same events, same
reference), frozen → r06:

| | s68 delta [deg] | significance |
|---|---|---|
| det3 x | −0.001 ± 0.007 | flat |
| det3 y | **+0.061 ± 0.013** | 4.7σ worse |
| det7 x | **+0.076 ± 0.033** | 2.3σ worse |
| det7 y | **+0.131 ± 0.019** | 6.9σ worse |

**The angular resolution on the deck gets worse, on three of four planes.** The
training χ² agrees: pinning the ratio anywhere in 0.45–0.8 costs 2.8–3.3 % of
χ² relative to the free fit. Inside this model, the bench data want c2 ≥ c1.

Two things to hold at once, and both are true:

* the data prefer the inverted kernel **inside this model**, and
* the inverted kernel cannot be a physical resistive film, so the preference is
  evidence that the *model* is wrong in that direction, not that the film is.

r06 is better physics and worse fit. Nothing here resolves that; §5 is what
would.

## 5 · What actually fixes it

R06_GATE §3: the failure is **plane-specific**. r06 improves implied-v flatness
on X on both detectors (det7 x 1.048 → 0.254) and degrades Y on both — and Y is
the plane the kernel acts on (kY = 2.9 det3, 5.6 det7). A single global c2/c1 is
what fails.

The identified next experiment is a **per-plane ratio, pinned on X and free
(but bounded < 1) on Y**. It needs a new hyper, because `build_matrix` applies
`c2_over_c1` to the base hypers deliberately before the per-plane kY scaling —
i.e. a model change, validated on its own, not a refit. Until then r06 is what
ships.

  (channels 448–511, FEU 3) was electrically dead through run_79, and the
  bundle's `dead` dict is empty. The kernel re-run deliberately changes one
  thing at a time, so this was left alone — it is a separate defect and it is
  still there.

  fixed by `set_w0.py --write` + `apply_w0.py --write` with no reco. The r06
  arms carry freshly measured w0/kw; the re-run campaign rows do not yet.
