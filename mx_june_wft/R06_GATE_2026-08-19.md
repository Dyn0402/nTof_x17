# R06_GATE_2026-08-19 — w0/kw re-measured, and the c2-slaved kernel gated

**Question asked:** re-measure w0/kw and re-freeze on `calib_bundle_r06` (the
physically ordered kernel, c2 = 0.6 x c1).

**Answer:** w0/kw are re-measured and written (det3 + det7). **The re-freeze on
r06 is NOT recommended** — scored the same way the t0-prior gate was, r06 fails
on angular resolution on three of four planes and undoes, on Y, the implied-v
flattening that the w0/kw refresh buys. A separate and cheaper defect was found
on the way: **the frozen bundles' own w0/kw are stale against the frozen reco**,
and that one is worth fixing whatever happens to the kernel.

## 1 · What was run

Golden keys only, locally, nothing overwritten (`events_r06.parquet`,
`alignment_r06/`, `angles_r06/` alongside the frozen products):

```
wft reco <key> --bundle <W>/calib_bundle_r06 --out <W>/events_r06.parquet --matched-only
bash mx_june_wft/21_r06_gate.sh <key> <W>          # set_w0 -> apply_w0 -> 01 -> 03
.venv/bin/python mx_june_wft/22_r06_compare.py sat_det3 g_det7_long
```

det3 7,093 events (11 min), det7 8,082 (20 min), 7 jobs.

**One reco pass is enough.** w0/kw enter reconstruction only as a post-hoc map
`tan = (w*1e3 - w0) / (kw * v)`; the stored `<plane>_w` does not see them. New
`bench/apply_w0.py` recomputes `tan_theta`, `theta_deg` and `slope_reliable`
from that column. `slope_reliable` is in the list because it is a cut on |tan| —
it moves with w0/kw, and the sigma-theta *population* moves with it (FREEZE §7).

## 3 · Reading it

**The r06 kernel costs resolution.** det3 y +0.061 ± 0.013 deg (4.7 sigma),
det7 y +0.131 ± 0.019 (6.9 sigma), det7 x +0.076 ± 0.033 (2.3 sigma); det3 x
flat. Decomposed against the middle arm, det3 y is +0.011 refresh + **+0.050
kernel**, while det7 y is +0.128 refresh + **+0.003 kernel** — i.e. det7's
apparent kernel cost is almost entirely the w0/kw refresh, det3's is not.

**The training chi2 agrees.** `kernel_arms/ratio_recal.json`, 180 events:

| detector | free ratio (frozen) | ratio 0.45 | 0.6 | 0.8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mx17_3 | 106,734,684 | 110,256,803 | 110,272,024 | 110,459,207 |
| mx17_7 | 107,412,668 | 110,918,154 | 110,384,879 | 110,104,605 |

Pinning the ratio anywhere in 0.45–0.8 costs **2.8–3.3 %** of chi2 relative to
the free fit that landed at c2/c1 = 1.14 (det3) / 1.75 (det7); within the pinned
range chi2 is nearly flat (0.2 % det3) and det7 even prefers the *highest*
ratio. So the data, inside this model, want c2 >= c1 — which is the whole
tension: the H4 beam measures c2/c1 = 0.45 ± 0.02 by cross-relation, with no
deconvolution and no regulariser, and c2 > c1 is not a physical RC cascade.

**The pattern is plane-specific.** On implied-v flatness (ungated, the judge
that catches a chain which has quietly reacquired the compression bias) r06 is
better on X on both detectors — det7 x 1.048 → 0.254, a residual rise of the
implied velocity with angle that r06 removes — and worse on Y on both. Y is the
plane the kernel actually acts on (kY = 2.9 det3, 5.6 det7). **A single global
c2/c1 is what fails; the next experiment is a per-plane ratio, pinned on X and
free on Y**, not a re-freeze. This is the same open item FREEZE §2 parked as
"the Y kernel shape (F6/F12)".

## 4 · The separable defect: the frozen bundles' w0/kw are stale

Measured with `set_w0.py` (no `--write`) on each detector's **own frozen**
`events.parquet`:

| detector | bundle carries | its own reco measures |
|---|---|---|
| mx17_3 | w0 (−0.004, −0.170), kw (1.006, **1.024**) | w0 (−0.038, −0.165), kw (1.001, **0.981**) |
| mx17_7 | w0 (0.033, −0.341), kw (1.060, **1.089**) | w0 (−0.010, −0.239), kw (1.016, **0.994**) |

With the frozen code the w→angle map needs essentially **no** correction (kw
within 2 % of 1 on both detectors). The frozen bundles apply up to **9 %**,
inherited from the `1292d91` reco generation that FREEZE §7 records the frozen
code does not reproduce. Consequences, both visible in the table above:

- a real median angle bias survives into the products — **+0.20 deg on det7 y**,
  −0.06 deg on det3 x;
- the Y angle scale is compressed by kw, and with it the residual spread, so
  **the quoted sigma_theta is optimistic**: det3 y 1.165 → 1.176 (+1 %), det7 y
  1.830 → 1.958 (**+7 %**).

This is fixable with **no reco at all** — `set_w0.py --write` then
`apply_w0.py --write` over the promoted parquets — and it is independent of the
kernel question. It is not free: it trades a bias for spread, and the deck
quotes the spread. That is Dylan's call, flagged, not taken.

## 5 · Tooling changed

- `wft/calib.py` — `summary()` printed the **stored** `c2` (0.0 on a slaved
  bundle), i.e. every reco log line for r06 announced "no ±2 copy" about a model
  that draws one. Now prints `c2=0.030 (=0.6xc1)`.
- `bench/set_w0.py` — `--events` (was hardcoded `events.parquet`, so
  re-measuring meant overwriting the live table); clears `w0_kw_stale` on
  `--write`, since that is exactly the condition being resolved.
- `bench/apply_w0.py` — new; post-hoc re-application, dry-run by default.
- `20_make_ratio_bundle.py` — provenance inherited the **source's** `chi2` and
  `code_commit`, so both r06 bundles claimed a chi2 they never achieved
  (106,734,684 rather than 110,272,024) under a commit not in this repo. Now
  carries the refit's own chi2 plus `chi2_source`. Both existing bundles were
  corrected in place.
- `condor/make_reco_package.py` — `--bundle-override DET=BUNDLE`, so a
  re-freeze is an explicit per-detector act that shows up as `--bundle-name` on
  each job line, leaving the manifest as the record of what was frozen. It
  refuses a bundle still stamped `w0_kw_stale`.
- `21_r06_gate.sh`, `22_r06_compare.py` — the gate and its table.

## 8 · Three detectors, one pattern: the constraint is right for X and wrong for Y

With det2 added, the golden-key gate reads the same way on every chamber that
was inverted. Frozen → r06, full coverage, identical event sets:

| key | X s68 | Y s68 |
|---|---|---|
| sat_det3 | 1.186 → 1.185 (−0.001 ± 0.007) | 1.165 → **1.226** (+0.061 ± 0.013) |
| o22_long_det2 | 1.311 → 1.294 (−0.017) | 1.574 → **1.696** (+0.122) |
| g_det7_long | 2.467 → 2.543 (+0.076 ± 0.033) | 1.830 → **1.961** (+0.131 ± 0.019) |

**X is flat or better on two of three and Y is worse on three of three**, and on
det3 the damage is concentrated head-on (|θ| < 5°: X 1.275 → 1.294, +1.5 %;
Y 1.225 → **1.432**, +17 %). The implied-v flatness splits the same way — on the
ungated paired comparison r06 is flatter on X (det7 1.048 → 0.254) and worse on Y.

Y is the plane the resistive film's own sheet resistance acts along (strips run
along y; kY = 2.9 det3, 5.4 det2, 5.6 det7). So a **single global c2/c1 is what
fails**: the beam's 0.45 was measured on det4's X view, which the angled-mount
work showed is at normal incidence and therefore isolates the kernel, and it is
being applied to a plane whose copy is *slower*, not just stronger (the
+28 ± 7 ns X vs +67 ± 15 ns Y shift already measured on the fleet — "a timing
difference booked as an amplitude").

**The next experiment is a per-plane ratio: pin X at the beam value, leave Y
free.** That is one extra hyper, it is testable on the same golden keys with the
machinery now in place (`19_ratio_recal.py` + `21_r06_gate.sh` + `22_r06_compare.py`),
and it is the concrete form of the "Y kernel shape" item FREEZE §2 parked. What
is shipped today is the ordering — defensible, and honest about its cost — not
the last word on the kernel.

### Found and fixed

1. **`wft_gallery.png` and `wft_candidates.png` were rendered mid-flight.**
   Re-rendered all ten `wft_*` doc figures under r06 and byte-compared: eight
   bit-identical, these two not — and they match a frozen-bundle render either.
   They were made on 08-18 with r06 **before w0/kw were re-measured**, so they
   carried the inherited stale constants. `f_gallery` is deterministic (two
   renders bit-identical), so the difference is real: 32 % of gallery's pixels,
   0.009 % of candidates'. Replaced. **This is why the check has to be a
   re-render and a byte-compare and not a reading of the log.**

2. **`maps/Plot_Data/efficiency_r2mm_cut.csv` was stale on ALL FIVE keys since
   before the campaign** (08-12 21:38, predating even the 08-13 recos). It is
   written by `report/make_maps_2mm.py`, **not** by `04_maps.py` — so re-running
   the chain never refreshes it, and `mpgd26/make_efficiency_breakdown.py`
   redraws `efficiency_map_2mm.png` from it, producing a fresh-looking figure
   over old data. Regenerated for all five; effect small (mean efficiency 0.2-0.4
   points, median bin unchanged, max bin 0.2-0.4).

3. **The deck's efficiency map is on a different det3 dataset.**
   `make_efficiency_map.py` uses `g_det3_wknd` (22,095 events, "highest
   statistics det3"), which is manifest row 59 — tier A, identical conditions to
   sat_det3 (490 V, 1000 V, Ar/Iso 95/5) — and was still on
   `calib_bundle_lp2_t0p`. Re-reconstructed on det3's r06 bundle. (No `set_w0`
   here: w0/kw are per DETECTOR and were measured on the golden key; measuring
   them again on this run would overwrite the shared bundle.)

4. **Diagnostic leftovers removed**: `events_w0fix.parquet` / `angles_w0fix/` on
