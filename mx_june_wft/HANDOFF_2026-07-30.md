## What happened (2026-07-29/30, this arc)

The waveform fit was benchmarked exhaustively (harness: `bench/build_cache.py`
+ `bench/run_bench.py`, bit-validated against production), improved in four
validated steps, and the improved reconstruction resolved the drift-gap
puzzle. Detection semantics (has_any 100 %, spark_frac 8.22 % on det3) never
moved — as designed.

### The four adopted reconstruction changes

1. **`MODEL_FRAC = 0.03`** (env `WFT_MODEL_FRAC`): 3 % fractional model-error
   term in the chi2 weights. The chi2 was model-error dominated (chi2/dof
   ~110-180); this recovers ~40 % of the reco_far tail (giant-charge delta
   blobs) and tightens angles. NOTE: chi2/dof scale collapses (median 20-60);
   `WFT_CHI2DOF_BAD=250` is the matching quality-flag threshold.
2. **`w0` / `kw` per-plane angle-mapping constants** in the bundle:
   `tan = (w*1e3 - w0) / (kw * v_drift)`. w0 = transverse-speed offset of
   reference-vertical tracks (explained every angle bias in the fleet digest,
   det6-Y's -0.95 deg included); kw = slope scale. Measured by
   `wft.calibrate.measure_w0` (corridor, pass 1) and REFINED by
   `bench/set_w0.py --bundle <b> --write` from a production reco (pass 2 —
   the corridor values are biased ~0.06 um/ns; always do pass 2 + re-reco).
   Under the RC-ladder kernel kw comes out ~1 (its physics is solved).
3. **`share_lp` RC-ladder sharing kernel** (bundle hyper `share_lp: 1`,
   `kTauY ~ 1.78`): the +-1 neighbour copy is a LOW-PASSED template
   (template x exp kernel, tau ~ 230 ns X / 410 ns Y — measured directly on
   near-vertical tracks, 14x/54x better shape than the old delay+smear), +-2
   = two cascaded stages. Removes the -3 % Y slope compression physically,
   collapses the Y deep-charge pile-up (67 -> 18 %), best position numbers,
   fastest. Calibrate with `--share-lp`.
4. **`PRESCAN_COARSE`** (env `WFT_PRESCAN=1`): global scan on a K=9 x 120 ns
   basis. Accuracy parity, ~1.5-2x faster. Whole det3 reco = 13 min @ 8 jobs.

`bench/gap_study.py`: stacked NNLS charge-arrival profiles of M3-contained
tracks, erfc endpoint, mm via geometric v (kw*v — NOT timing chi2(v), which
is kernel-degenerate; fixed-hyper scans run to ~40 um/ns on both kernels).

apparent tilt = Y-response systematic -> always quote X for column physics.**

## Gotchas for the fresh session

- A bundle used outside its conditions is a silent error (CLAUDE.md rule) —
  and now bundles also differ by KERNEL (`share_lp` in hyper). Never mix.
- chi2/dof scales differ per config (unweighted ~110/180, mf3 ~20/60): all
  chi2 thresholds are config-relative.
- Warm-vs-cold t0 grids differ by ~20 % in total chi2 — never compare
  kernels by single-evaluation chi2.
- The w0/kw two-pass: corridor values are ~0.06 um/ns biased; production
  retrofit is mandatory before quoting angles.
- det7: 700 V drift but v ~ 36.6 (chamber-specific fast gas, hardware
  question); don't quote det7 absolute v/depth.
- `/tmp/wft_logs/` can be cleaned by the OS mid-session; recreate.
- The June hits caches all carry the 2026-07-25 significance floor now;
  `02_efficiency.py --source hits` is the only fair hits-chain comparison.
