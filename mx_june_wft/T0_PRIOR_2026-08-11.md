# T0_PRIOR_2026-08-11 — the absolute-t0 gate (T1.1), and what it actually found

**Status: gate measured, prior implemented, A/B in flight (§6 pending).**
Handoff item T1.1 (`docs/wft_reference/HANDOFF_FABLE_2026-08-11.md`, F26).

## 1 · Verdict of the gate

The gate passes — the fit's t0 carries far less information than the trigger
provides — but **not in the shape F26 predicted**. The within-ftst-class spread
of the fitted t0 is not "trigger jitter ⊕ fit noise". It is three fit
pathologies stacked, each larger than the ~4 ns trigger term:

| effect | size | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| production t0 is grid-locked | 59 % (X) / 80 % (Y) of production t0 sit *exactly* on a 10 ns lattice | `events.parquet` t0 mod 5/10; teeth in every per-class histogram are delta functions |
| one-depth-bin tooth ambiguity | minima 60 ns apart, comparable chi2; only ~34 % of planes land in the physical tooth | pooled (t0 + 10·ftst): teeth at exactly 60 ns spacing, fractions 0.35/0.23/0.21 (X); `q_uend` medians 780/720/660 ns across teeth −1/0/+1 — the whole fitted column slides one bin |
| per-event drag along the p0–t0 valley | ±60–100 ns (X), worse on Y | chi2(t0) at ref-pinned geometry: single sharp minimum per event whose *location* scatters ±100 ns; free-refit vs production t0 moved > 30 ns for 53–58 % of events |

Meanwhile the fit's reported `t0_err` is ~1.4–2.0 ns — it is the fixed-(p0,w)
curvature, and the valley direction makes it fiction (the F25 caveat, now
measured). The within-class spread is 60–75 ns per plane, independent between
planes (t0x−t0y rsig 92 ns), so it is not a common DAQ-latency jitter.

**Physical tooth identified.** The central tooth's `q_uend` ≈ 720 ns matches
det3's measured column span; the −1 tooth reads 780 (empty leading bin) and the
+1 tooth 660 (leading charge cut). The mode is the physical solution. Tooth
also correlates with angle (X median |tan| 0.149/0.123/0.079 for −1/0/+1) —
inclined tracks preferentially mis-tooth early, the §21.1 connection.

**Consequence for geometry.** A 60 ns tooth error moves p0 by w·60 ≈ 0.3–0.9 mm
on inclined tracks — *larger* than the 0.33 mm floor. F26's "will not move
position" assumed δt0 ≈ 5 ns; the real ambiguity is 60 ns, so the expectation
is revised: the prior can move position tails (and `q_uend`-based maps).

## 2 · Why the fit's t0 is this weak

chi2(t0) profiles at reference-pinned geometry (8 inclined events,
`scratchpad/chi2_t0_profiles.png`, regenerable from §7): each plane shows a
*single* well-formed minimum — no comb at pinned geometry — but its location
wanders ±100 ns event to event, and Y's valleys are shallow and often
one-sided (Y median chi2/dof ~2× X's: the kernel mis-model, F6/F12 again).

Mechanism: the p0–t0 valley (δp0 = w·δt0, doc §20). With p0 free, chi2 is soft
along the valley, so any per-event model-time mismatch (template shape vs the
event's pulses, δ-rays, saturation) drags t0 by tens of ns and p0 follows by
w·δt0. At *pinned* geometry the reference's own ~0.4 mm transverse error enters
the same way: δt0 = δp0/w ≈ 70 ns at |tan| ~ 0.2. Both measured numbers match.

So the trigger prior is not a small correction to an already-good t0 — it cuts
across the soft valley direction and converts external timing into position
information. Its realistic targets: tooth selection (the 65 % mis-tooth
population), the near-vertical slope wander, and the §21.1 wrong-basin rate.

A σ = 10 ns prior does NOT move a fit whose minimum sits 45 ns away (measured,
evt 27): the data-side chi2 preference at the wrong tooth is tens-to-hundreds
of units. Pulling those events demands σ ≈ 5 ns (exposing real model tension in
chi2, which is honest — the trigger knows the time base better than the model)
or the prior does nothing (σ ≳ 25). Hence the A/B scans σ ∈ {5, 10, 25}.

## 3 · The prediction is calibratable to ~4 ns — and the ftst ladder is real

`08_t0_abs_calib.py`: ref-pinned t0 per plane per ftst class on the 7,093
bench-cache events (n ≈ 1150/class; per-event rsig ~125 ns → class-median SE
3.4–3.9 ns). The −10 ns/step ftst ladder emerges cleanly on Y
(steps −11.3/−9.9/−10.3/−10.1/−6.2) and in total span on X (54 ns over 5
steps), which is the direct validation that ftst → t0 prediction works.

Products (det3 saturday run): `<OUT_BASE>/wft/t0_prior/t0_abs.json` (+ the
bare `t0_abs_table.json` for `run_bench --t0-abs`), per-class histograms in
`t0_abs_classes.png`.

Storage: `CalibrationBundle.t0_abs` {plane: {ftst: t0_pred}} +
`t0_prior_sigma` (0 = disabled), saved/loaded with the bundle. Per run
condition, like everything else in a bundle.

## 4 · What was implemented

- `wft/model.py` — `chi2_plane(..., t0_prior=(t0_pred, sigma))` Gaussian
  penalty; threaded through `fit_plane_raw` (both paths) and the NM objective.
- `wft/reco.py` — `t0_prior_for(cal, plane, ftst)`; `fit_plane`,
  `fit_plane_candidates`, `_global_start` take `t0_prior` (the prior collapses
  the stage-1 t0 scan to one point); `_errors` extended to t0 → `t0_err` on
  `PlaneFit` and in the parquet; per-plane ftst carried through
  `_stream_windows` payloads and written as `x_ftst`/`y_ftst` columns;
  module globals `T0_PRIOR_SIGMA` / `T0_ABS` for bench overrides.
- `wft/calibrate.py` — `measure_t0_abs()` (ref-pinned, mirrors
  `measure_dt_xy`).
- `mx_june_wft/bench/` — `build_cache.py` stores per-plane ftst
  (`bench_cache_ftst.pkl`); `run_bench.py` `--t0-abs`/`--t0-sigma`, near-
  vertical score columns (`signv_x/y`: reference-selected |tan_ref| < 0.08).
- `mx_june_wft/07_t0_prior_gate.py` — the gate diagnostic (t0-by-ftst combs,
  within-class spread vs `t0_err`, angle/run controls).
- `mx_june_wft/08_t0_abs_calib.py` — the calibration above.

## 5 · Traps found on the way (each cost real time)

1. **The bench variant switches largely no-op against the shipped `wft/`.**
   `MODEL_FRAC`, `PRESCAN_COARSE`, `ROBUST_REFIT`, `SCORE_MODE`, `ITER_SCAN`
   lived in the RC-ladder line (commit 9dd7d6e); the 2026-08-06 merge kept the
   other line and archived them. `run_bench.py` still sets them via
   `reco_globals`/`model_globals` — silently doing nothing. The validated
   production configuration (mf3 + prescan + w0) is therefore NOT in the
   shipped code, and `events.parquet` (built pre-merge, commit 1292d91) is not
   reproducible by it. Any bench result quoting those variants post-merge is a
   baseline re-run. Worse: per `ANALYSIS_STATE §2.1` the production
   reconstruction *used* `kTauY = 1.78` (the bundle's fitted value) — the
   shipped model carries it in the hyper dict and never reads it, so the merge
   silently dropped part of the validated production *model*, not just tuning
   knobs. T2.1 (merge the per-plane kernel) is a restoration, and a
   shipped-code refit differs from the reference parquet by kTauY, model_frac,
   prescan and w0/kw all at once.
2. **dt_xy has three inconsistent generations**: −18.8 ns (the R&D default
   from `forward_model2.py`, f60f585 — still the fallback in `reco.py:316` and
   `model.py:360`), −7.65/−16.01 (doc §36), +5.43/+30.23 (the shipped
   `calib_bundle_lp2`). t0's registration depends on the kernel form, so dt_xy
   is bundle-specific — a bundle-mismatched dt_xy silently degrades
   `select_pair` and `fit_joint`. Also: "dt_xy by ftst *difference*" is an
   approximation — the t0_abs table gives dt for diff = +1 ranging −7.8 to
   −18.8 depending on which (fx, fy) pair realises the difference.
3. **Ref-pinned t0 needs the cached tan sign convention checked** — the cache
   stores tan in the fit frame (verified: chi2/dof 62 vs 1161 sign-flipped).
4. The model-free leading-edge (earliest half-max) spread within a class is
   ~50 ns (X and Y), consistent with PLAN_42's absolute σ68 = 37.7 ns — the
   per-event absolute-timing floor of *edge estimators* is set by detector
   terms, not by the trigger. The trigger's ~4 ns lives only in the class
   medians (n^-1/2) and in the prior.

## 6 · A/B — scan half (even events, n = 3,547)

`bench_cache_ftst.pkl`, `calib_bundle_lp2`, variant `baseline` vs
`--t0-abs t0_abs_table.json --t0-sigma {25, 10, 5}`:

| arm | within5 | far | core σ [mm] | med |r| | σθ X/Y [°] | vsp X/Y | sNV X/Y [°] | s/fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | 92.78 | 2.48 | 0.452 | 0.726 | 1.15/1.15 | 1.7/1.3 | 1.16/1.25 | 1.06 |
| σ = 25 | 92.58 | 2.68 | 0.448 | 0.710 | 1.14/1.14 | 1.0/1.5 | 1.16/1.24 | 0.98 |
| σ = 10 | 92.58 | 2.68 | 0.443 | 0.707 | 1.14/1.14 | 1.0/1.5 | 1.15/1.23 | 1.05 |
| σ = 5  | 92.58 | 2.68 | **0.437** | **0.698** | 1.14/1.14 | 1.0/1.5 | 1.15/1.23 | 1.06 |

Reading: core position and median |r| improve **monotonically** with prior
tightness (−3.3 % / −3.9 % at σ = 5) — the valley-clamp mechanism (δp0 =
w·δt0), exactly where the gate said the information was. Angles and
near-vertical σ essentially unchanged (as the corrected F26 predicted);
X's implied-v spread improves 1.7 → 1.0; Y's ticks 1.3 → 1.5 (watch it);
efficiency flat (the ±0.2 % within5/far moves are ~7 events). The §21.1
catastrophic failures are NOT touched by the prior alone — they are p0-scan
window misses, which is T2.4 (the collapsed t0 scan pays for the widening).

### Validation — held-out odd half (n = 3,546), baseline vs σ = 5

| arm | within5 | far | core σ | med |r| | σθ X/Y | vsp X/Y | s14 X/Y | sNV X/Y | s/fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | 93.27 | 2.66 | 0.482 | 0.743 | 1.17/1.11 | 1.1/1.1 | 1.14/1.14 | 1.23/1.27 | 1.08 |
| σ = 5 | **93.71** | **2.22** | **0.460** | **0.714** | 1.15/1.10 | **0.8/0.8** | 1.11/1.10 | 1.20/1.25 | 0.93 |

**The winner survives out of sample, and more cleanly than on the scan half**:
within5 +0.44, reco_far −0.44, core σ −4.6 %, implied-v spread better on BOTH
planes (the scan half's Y-vsp tick was noise), every angle metric equal or
better, 14 % faster. No metric regresses.

### How it actually works — the tooth-population check

Per-event rows on the validation half, deviation of fitted t0 from the class
prediction:

| | central tooth (|dev| < 30 ns) | core rsig | med q_uend Y |
|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | 27 % (X) / 28 % (Y) | 22/24 ns | 900 ns |
| σ = 5 | 33 % (X) / 34 % (Y) | 21/19 ns | 840 ns |

So even at σ = 5 the prior does **not** pin the time base — for two thirds of
planes the model's chi2 preference for a displaced t0 beats a
((60/5)² = 144)-unit penalty. The measured gains come from **starting the fit
at the physical t0** (the collapsed scan) and tipping near-degenerate basins,
not from forcing t0. The model-side time drag — worst on Y, where the kernel
is known-wrong (F6/F12, and see `SHARING_DEPTH_2026-08-11.md`) — is what caps
how tight the prior can usefully be. Merging the per-plane kernel back (T2.1)
should shrink that drag and let the prior bite harder; re-run this A/B after.

## 8 · Recommendation

Adopt for det3: `calib_bundle_lp2` + `t0_abs` (from `08_t0_abs_calib.py`) +
`t0_prior_sigma = 5.0` in the bundle. Costs nothing (it is *faster*), improves
core position ~5 % and implied-v flatness, and gives every event a t0 pinned
well enough for `select_pair`/`fit_joint` work downstream. Fleet rollout needs
the per-run t0_abs calibration (it is a run-condition quantity like everything
else in a bundle) — the 08 script needs only a bench cache with ftst. Re-tune
σ after T2.1.

**DONE (2026-08-12): `calib_bundle_lp2_t0p`** — a copy of `calib_bundle_lp2`
with `t0_abs` (from `t0_prior/t0_abs_table.json`) and `t0_prior_sigma = 5.0`
patched into `bundle.json` (numerics otherwise identical; provenance notes the
parent). Verified: a 400-event bench through the bare bundle (no `--t0-abs`
flags) reproduces the CLI-flag run metric-for-metric — the prior activates
from the bundle fields alone via `t0_prior_for`'s fallback. This is the
production configuration for det3 sat-scan conditions.
