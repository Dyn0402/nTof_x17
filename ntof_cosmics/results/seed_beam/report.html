The 3-strip beam seeder on beam data: more real tracks, at lower purity

Verdict: min 3 is a net gain on beam data, but it should not ship alone. Background-subtracted scintillator-confirmed tracks rise by A +45 %, C +52 %, near-normal confirmed tracks by A ×3.6, C ×5.1. Tracks only min 3 finds confirm at A 56 %, C 90 % of production's rate, so they are mostly real but dirtier. Nothing is lost as a particle. About 13 % (A) / 5 % (C) of production's x/y pairings are re-paired in busy events, where neither choice is clearly better on timing or charge. That is the x/y pairing problem the two-track work's xy_pairing fix addresses, so the seeder change and that fix must be validated together (sept26_prelim_analysis/SAME_CHAMBER_PAIRS.md).

What was compared

run_145 stat090_0000, all seven file tags, every trigger (the September full pass had no allowlist). The production reco (reco_fullpass, seeder minimum 5) against a re-run that changes only MIN_STRIPS_BEAM to 3: the full pass's own saved bundle per arm, the same fit, the same environment. A local re-run at min 5 reproduces production on one tag to 99.8 % of fits (the rest are the known laptop-vs-condor flips between near-degenerate minima). Tracks are built and gated by today's build_tracks with stage 3's k, which reproduces the stage-3 gated counts exactly. Purity is external: each gated track is extrapolated to its arm's SiPM wall and plastic (det_a_scint.match_run), and the same test in an equal-width off-time window is the accidental floor. Chamber B has no angle calibration and cannot be extrapolated.

What this does not rule out

  • One sub-run (run_145 stat090_0000), post-23-July noisy configuration. Quiet runs have more strips over threshold and less to gain.
  • Confirmation is a lower bound on reality: out-of-time particles and tracks that miss the wall never confirm, so "unconfirmed" is not "junk". The gained sample is more out of time (t0 > 300 ns) than production, which accounts for part of its lower rate.
  • Near-normal confirmation is low in both seeders (A 8–10 %). Either near-normal reconstructed tracks are mostly mis-measured, or few of them point at a fired group. Not resolved here.
  • Re-paired tracks: which x/y pairing is right is undecided. Per-plane t0 is unconstrained in the free fit (89 ns scatter), so timing cannot arbitrate.
  • Angles still come from the v = 42.6 bundles and the old C kernel: this tests detection, not angle accuracy.
