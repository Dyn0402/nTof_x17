What this does not rule out

  • Which k is right on A is not settled by the wall: it is coarse, and the same-track preference is a few per mille of the tracks. The A–C cosmic truth is the stronger argument.
  • The same-track test covers only tracks both chains find in the same place; it says nothing about the tracks only the re-pass finds.
  • Wall confirmation is a purity proxy with its own angle-dependent extrapolation error, and confirmed-per-gated also carries the wall's acceptance.
  • Nothing here tests |true tan| 0.5–1 on data except the wall; the cosmic truth stops near 0.5.
  • B and D are not in the re-pass (no in-situ bundle).
