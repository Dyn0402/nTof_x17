Arm-to-arm time resolution of the SiPM walls, from cosmic muons (A–C)

Verdict. With no beam, the SiPM walls of arms A and C give σ_t ≈ 0.52–0.64 ns per arm (σ(t_A − t_C) = 0.90 ± 0.01 ns from the two-peak fit of 2579 muons; direction-tagged peak cores 0.52, 0.52 ns per arm), as read out at n_TOF (official pulse fit, 1 GS/s). The plastics give 0.95 ns per arm (one PMT per bar, 477 muons). The muon's own time of flight between the walls (±2.3–2.5 ns) is resolved: the distribution has two clean peaks. During beam the same muons give ~6 ns (the pulse fit's floor under beam), so only the beam-off data measure the detector.

1. What was measured

Muons crossing the SiPM walls of A and C, with both ends of a wall group firing in each arm, unsaturated, within ±25 ns of each other, while n_TOF recorded with no protons (run_149, n_TOF 224678–224687). Wall time = mean of the two ends (cancels the light path along the vertical bars).

Only 17 of these muons also have an A–C Micromegas line, so each muon's own TOF cannot be subtracted. Instead t_A − t_C is fitted as a mixture of ±T (T = L/c from the geometry of the beam-run A–C muons that do have a line: median 2.32 ns), a Gaussian of width σ, per-group offsets, a time walk in log(amplitude) per arm, the direction probability from the bars' top−bottom differences, and a flat accidental fraction.

3. Checks

  • Toy closure: the fit returns its input (input 0.902, refit 0.899 ns).
  • Stable in time (two halves of the n_TOF runs), with amplitude (both above median), and with the T template scaled ±10 % (0.92–0.96 ns).
  • Peak separation 5.0 ns, i.e. T ≈ 2.5 ns, against the template's 2.3: the beam-off muons are somewhat steeper.

4. Beam runs (ac_tof.py): why they do not measure σ_t

A–C muons at 20–80 ms after the flash with a Micromegas line (the TOF subtracted per muon) give t_A − t_C cores of ~6 ns whatever is corrected. Injected test pulses show the n_TOF pulse fit's wall timing degrades to 2.5–4.5 ns per hit under beam (0.4 ns without), which accounts for it.

5. What this does not rule out

  • It is the n_TOF readout. The LNL plan (V1742 at 5 GS/s for the walls, a CFD digitiser for the PMTs) could do better or worse; this is the as-built detector with this electronics, not a prediction for LNL.
  • Arms assumed equal; A and C only. The per-arm split cannot be separated with two arms.
  • The plastic number rests on 477 muons and a crude position correction (wall group, bar height).
  • Offsets were fitted on the same sample (8 of 2,579 degrees of freedom).
