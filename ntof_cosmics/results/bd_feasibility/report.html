Chambers B and D: can they get an in-situ angle calibration?

run_149 beam-off cosmics (87 sub-runs; hit-level checks on the 14 sub-runs whose combined hits are local), 2026-10-08. Generated by ntof_cosmics/bd_feasibility.py report.

D: yes, in x, from its own SiPM wall. Cosmics at D's wall read true tan = 1.525 × production raw (sub-run bootstrap 1.50–1.56, 3,391 single x-plane tracks). The same estimator gave A 1.15, which the A–C line confirmed (1.11). D's implied geometric drift speed is ≈ 27.9 µm/ns, far below its bench bundle's 36.6, so D's error is about twice the v substitution: D needs its own in-situ v, as A and C did. D's y view has no truth from the wall.

B: not as D's partner on cosmics. B lights (≥ 3 hits in both planes) on only 13 % of the straight cosmics that D's track says crossed it, against 65 % for C on A's (accidental baseline subtracted), and its reconstruction keeps a track on 3.1 % (C 51 %). On beam, scintillator-tagged, B's hit efficiency is comparable to the other chambers; on cosmics it is nearly blind. B–D lines therefore cannot give D a y-view truth at useful statistics without a dedicated B hit-mode reconstruction, and even then the crossing efficiency caps it.

5 · What it would take

  1. D in x (small): build an in-situ D bundle with v ≈ 28 (and the r06 det7 kernel it already has), reconstruct the clock-matched run_149 D tracks with seeder 3, and iterate the wall fit to s = 1. Needs D's waveforms (FEU 01/02) for those sub-runs from EOS. The per-run gas ratio comes from D's capsule band, as for A/C. Per-track truth (and kw per plane) is not available: one scale for x.
  2. D in y: no truth. Options: carry x's scale with a systematic (A's kw differ 3 % between views, C's 4.5 %), or the along-bar wall coordinate (corr −0.72 between bar halves; needs a slope fitted against tracks, i.e. the transfer B already rests on).
  3. D cross-checks: corner-cutting cosmics (A–D, C–D) at |tan| ≈ 1 test D (and A, C) above the 0.6 plausibility cut: the same sample answers the re-pass's TAN_MAX question.
  4. B: keep B as the hit-mode tagging chamber it is in the analysis (no angle). If B–D lines are still wanted: a hit-mode B position from the waveform charge centroid (no drift model), seeding at 3, and all 41 cosmic runs (57.6 h, ~3.4× run_149). At B's ~13 % crossing efficiency that is O(3,183) B–D lines campaign-wide, before any cleanliness cut.

What this does not rule out

  • A D wall scale biased by D's one-sided dead channels or hot strips (unmasked fit).
  • An angle-dependent D response: the per-bin wall fits are not stable enough to measure it.
  • B's cosmic blindness being a run_149 condition (HV, gas) rather than the chamber: B's HV for run_149 was not checked here.
  • That D's tracks used to predict B's crossings include junk: the A→C control uses the same method and gives C 60 %, so a D-side purity loss would have to be large to explain B's 11 %.
