Beam-off cosmics through the campaign reco: run_149/cosbounce_cos_0000

The chain runs on cosmics unchanged, and the first sub-run already says two things about the beam-on analysis. (1) The 170° back-to-back cut keeps only 74–77 % of clean through-goers (opposing pairs whose lines meet within 20 mm; 43 of them): their opening angle has a median of 173.9° but a 16 % quantile of 162–167°. (2) A capsule-free angle-scale check disagrees with the borrowed k: chamber A's track slopes run ~+8 % and chamber C's ~-12 % against the line through both chambers, under either neighbour's k. Both results come from ~40 events and are the reason to run the rest of run_149, not conclusions.

What was run

  • Reco: the campaign full pass exactly — `make_stage2_campaign.py --full-pass` with a new --tags-json (cosmic runs have no n_TOF slim, so no stage 1 to take tags from), the campaign bundles (A/B/D r06, C lp), v_drift pinned at 42.6 µm/ns, no hot-channel mask. Condor cluster 4354841, 12 jobs, none held; output in /eos/user/d/dneff/x17/cosmics_fullpass, apart from the campaign's. Code changed since the campaign pass only by the opt-in two-track switches, which are off.
  • Tracks: build_tracks.build, no stage 1 or allowlist, k borrowed from the neighbouring beam runs on each side (run_147, run_150) — k_arm assumes capsule tracks and cannot run on cosmics. run_147 has no certified k for B, so B pairs exist only under run_150's.
  • HV: no trips in hv_monitor.csv; drift at set-point by 14:51:09, the first minute of the first file.
  • Pairs: every pair of gated, calibrated tracks in different arms of one trigger. open_deg is source_imaging's definition (the track directions all point inward), so the 170° cut means the same thing as on the beam-on sample.

What this does not establish

  • ~40 clean events. Every percentage above has a ±7 % binomial error or worse. The other 86 sub-runs of run_149 (cluster 4355060) take this to ~3 500.
  • The 170° efficiency includes the angle-scale error. If A is ~10 % too shallow and C ~10 % too steep, the opening angle of a straight line is smeared by that alone; once k is fixed the fraction above 170° should rise. Re-measure with a cosmic-derived k before moving the cut.
  • "Clean" is a selection on line agreement, so it favours pairs where the two chambers agree and pulls the slope ratios toward 1. The departures above are therefore if anything understated. The unclean tail (lines missing by centimetres) is not yet understood: two particles, a δ-ray, or one chamber mis-reconstructed.
  • The track yield is low: only 10.1 % of triggers have a gated track, and only 136 have two arms. Part of it is the gate (arm A y-plausibility 55 %, D quality 76–79 %, against ~85 % on B/C), which was tuned on capsule tracks near the normal; the rest is scintillator coverage outside the chambers. Not yet split.
  • The clock join is thin here: n_TOF sees ~16 % of the time, so only 20 two-arm triggers are matched. That the n_TOF arm is one of the two tracked chambers 95 % of the time is a consistency check of both the clock match and the reco, not a Δt result.
  • No time-since-flash (there is no flash); the t_since_flash columns are empty by construction.
