# M3 per-track pointing (2026-11-26)

The pointing of the M3 reference telescope depends on the track: on the χ² of its fit, on its angle and on where it
crosses the telescope. Paper I used one pointing per slot. This package measures the pointing of each track, tests it,
and tries a matching rule "the chamber's hit lies within n σ_i of this track's own expected residual".

Report: `$WORK_DATA/nTof_x17/m3_pointing/report/report.html` (generated by `make_report.py`). A copy of the report, the
fit products and the χ² > 1 reconstruction of chamber A Run 1 is on EOS: `/eos/experiment/ntof/data/x17/cosmic_bench/x17rerun/m3_pointing/`.

## The idea in four steps

1. **The χ² has two parts** (`decompose.py`). For Gaussian plane errors the χ² of a four-point line fit is an
   exponential with mean 2σ², and it carries no information on the pointing. The measured χ² is an *ordinary* part
   (an exponential whose mean μ(t) follows the plane resolution, which grows five-fold with the track angle) plus an
   *anomalous* part (kinks, δ-rays, wrong clusters) with the same distribution at every angle. Each track gets the
   posterior means E[k | χ², t] and E[g | χ², t] = χ² − E[k] in closed form.
2. **Two chambers measure the telescope** (`fit.py`, `crosscov.py`). On the same track, two chambers share only the
   M3 error, so the covariance of their residuals is the M3 error alone (a three-cornered hat). Three June pair runs
   (6-22, 6-23, 6-26) give a bivariate likelihood fit of
   `σ_i² = d0 + d1 t² + P_s² [a0 + a1 (μ/μ0 − 1)] + κ_s E[k] + γ_s E[g]`, with the cross term pinning the floor.
3. **Position** (`posmap.py`). The same covariance in 4 × 4 cells of the M3 position gives a width factor on the
   M3 term, and the common median shift of the two chambers (a position-dependent M3 bias, reported, not applied).
4. **Tests** (`validate.py`, `efficiency.py`, `extend.py`). Pull closure on every row (three rows held out),
   matching efficiency against the fixed 5 mm at equal accidental rate, and chamber A Run 1 reconstructed at every χ²
   (the pass only reconstructed χ² < 1) to test the model where it was not fitted.

## Model in code

`model.v_m3(D, glob, view, posmap)` is the M3 variance of each ray, given the `add_m3` columns of `fit.py`
(`mu_v*`, `k_v*`, `g_v*`, `P2_v*`) and the slot from the alignment z. `model.sigma_track` adds a row's chamber term.
View x of a chamber is read against M3 Y and view y against M3 X (all chambers sit at θ ≈ 90° to M3).
