Every metric: better or worse, and by how much

"Change" is relative for counts and rates, absolute for numbers already in % or mm. The verdict is the direction that helps the physics, not the sign of the change.

1. Capsule focus: does the angle scale point the tracks at the capsule?

Each gated A/C track's x-view line is extended to the beam axis with its tan multiplied by a trial scale s; the median distance by which the lines miss the axis is plotted against s. If the angles are right, the curve is sharpest at s = 1. Before = the is2_v2 re-pass (in-situ bundles, two-track off, cut 0.6). The nominal axis is used here, so the minimum also carries the source's 9 mm offset; the population split below is what separates scale from geometry.

3. The 3D pair-vertex image

Two tracks of one trigger in different chambers are intersected; the vertices are histogrammed in 3D and the no-source null (each track's direction swapped with another track of the same chamber, keeping every marginal) is subtracted. A real source makes a compact blob at the capsule; the chambers and the selection alone make the null. Before = the production full pass (stage3_fullpass, the Athens images); after = pass 1. Maps are data − null, summed over the third coordinate, each normalised to its own maximum. Cross = the capsule from single-track pointing.

What Geant4 through each reconstruction says

The data's close-pair excess (real − mixed under 40 mm: 0.047 % of tracked events on A, 0.028 % on C) matches the rate at which pass 1 splits a single Geant4 lepton into two close tracks (0.053 % / 0.042 %): it is expected splitting, not physics. The correlated pair fraction is ~10× below Geant4 on both chambers (before: ~8×); with the pass-1 response the reconstruction is not what loses them.

What is worse, or not settled

  • The capsule focus is less sharp (median miss up 1–4 mm at fixed |tan|): pass 1 keeps ~40 % more tracks and the added ones point at the capsule less often. The early (< 10 ms) window gains 2.5–5× tracks with almost no capsule content.
  • An antisymmetric A − C focus difference of 4.6 % is now the largest scale effect: a distance or axis effect between the two chambers, not an angle-scale one.
  • The same-chamber correlated fraction is 10× below Geant4 (was 8×), and the pair-fraction-vs-rate fit is poor (χ²/ndf 67 on A). The open item is the Geant4 capsule / source model.
  • The capsule pose fits got worse (best x χ² 12 → 25 over 10 dof, z 1 → 19 over 5): with more tracks and smaller errors the toy no longer fits well, and x and z still prefer different poses.
  • B has no angle on pass 1, so the B–D through-goer and B pointing studies lose B; D's z crossing moved by 2 mm with its new latency pin.
