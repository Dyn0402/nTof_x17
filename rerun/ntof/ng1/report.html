What this does not rule out

  • One beam run (run_145) and one cosmic run (run_149). Other periods are not scored here; the pre-freeze checks (plan §6 11-06) covered efficiency and pointing on runs 79, 81, 116, 162.
  • B and D have no is2_v2 reference (is2_v2 reconstructed A and C only), so N-G1 cannot pass or fail them; their absolute numbers are given. D's beam numbers are on the L2 pin under rescan.
  • Wall confirmation says a track is a real particle that reached the wall, not that its angle is right (G6: electrons scatter). The extra pass tracks are ~half confirmed: some of the rest may be real particles that miss the wall acceptance, some not.
  • The cosmic efficiency comparison is not like-for-like (relative quality rule on an allowlist table); only the fit level is.
  • C3's reference values carry a ±3 mm systematic of the estimator (efficiency tilts, chamber-to-chamber); which of the pass's −33.2 or is2_v2's −35.8 is closer to the truth is not decided here.
  • G5: the focus estimator is unbiased to ±3 % in Geant4 with the simulated source; a source model that differs from the data (vertex distribution, electron angle response) would move s. The remaining 8 % on A is not explained.
