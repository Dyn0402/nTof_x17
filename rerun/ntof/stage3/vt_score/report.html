n_TOF stage 3: the v(t) angle correction

Verdict. The angle scale follows the timing v(t) in time (slope ≈ 1 on all four views over ~240 beam-off sub-runs), but sits a constant 2–3 % below it. The table correction is therefore tan_vt = tan · v_bundle / (κ · v_t) with one κ per chamber and view; without κ it overshoots (run_149 C: 1.04 steep → 0.98 shallow). With κ the A–C line reads 1.000–1.004 on run_149 and the beam source y is unchanged.

What this does not rule out

  • κ is measured on cosmic triggers and applied to beam rows; a beam/cosmic difference in the timing–angle relation would not show here (the class offset in v_t itself is ~1 %, opposite sign in A and C).
  • κ drifts by 0.3–0.4 % between the campaign's two halves (~3σ): a sub-percent systematic on the angle scale.
  • The ~1 % candidate reshuffle a v(t) re-reconstruction would make at the fastest times is not undone by a table correction (A/B 4677861).
  • D: no angle truth, κ_D = 1; D beam v_t is predicted from D's cosmic sub-runs.
