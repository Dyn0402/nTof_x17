# n_TOF pass 1: where the tracking can still improve (started 2026-11-20)

Report: `report/report.html` (generated by `make_report.py`; the live copy is `$WORK_DATA/ntof_pass1/ana/track_quality/`).
Everything reads the pass-1 analysis view (v(t) angles, gated = true |tan| < 0.5). Existing data only.

## Findings

1. **Cosmic-line angle tails are mostly impure truth, not failed fits.** 13–14 % of views miss the joined A–C line by
   more than 0.15 in tan (46,171 lines, all 72 beam-off runs). A and C are both wrong 3× more often than chance, and when
   the other three views agree with the line within 0.05 a view's own tail is 1.0–2.1 %. Flagged views carry ~half the
   charge, ~1/5 of the Δχ² and (A) 5–9 competing-cluster strips: extra particles. Not worth a reco change.
2. **Bench bow block (plan §5 check, never done before): keep it.** Offline bow-off = constant mapping from the stored
   fit speed w. On C the bow lowers σ 8–11 % and the cell-median bias by a third; on A it is neutral (`bow_check.csv`).
3. **A position-dependent angle distortion remains**: rms 0.008–0.016 in tan per 50 mm cell, up to 0.04–0.05 at edges
   (A x: tilt −0.03 / +0.04 at x ≈ ∓175 mm; C: a scale gradient along y in both views, ≈ +0.1 → −0.15).
   Run-parity halves agree at r = 0.94–0.97; early/late campaign tilts 0.70–0.97, C's scale 0.84–0.92.
4. **An in-situ map fixes most of it** (`angle_map.py`: r = a(x,y) + s(x,y)·j per 50 mm cell, bilinear;
   corrected slope = (slope − a)/(1 + s)). Held-out (train one run parity, test the other) clean single-view σ:
   A x 0.031 → 0.026, A y 0.029 → 0.025, C x 0.035 → 0.028, C y 0.039 → 0.032 (11–19 %). On the independent beam
   capsule tracks (≥ 20 ms): pointing excess < 15 mm A 0.445 → 0.461, C 0.417 → 0.436; core 12.6 → 12.1 / 12.9 → 12.5 mm;
   sharpest focus A 1.035 → 0.990, C 0.985 → 0.990.
5. **So the A−C focus asymmetry was not geometry.** PASS1_DOWNSTREAM read the antisymmetric 4.6 % as a distance or axis
   offset (it would be a 5.4 mm source offset toward C, the same as D's −5.55 mm z crossing). The cosmic map, which
   knows nothing about the source, removes it, so most of it is A's edge tilt. D's −5.55 mm is not confirmed by A/C.
6. **B–D cosmic lines did not close** (explained by 7–8 below): D's line misses B's point by a median +117 mm in both z and y (65 k B–D pairs,
   3.4 k within 60 mm). Until that is understood B cannot be D's truth, and D has no map.

7. **D's fake tracks: censored fit windows** (`rerun/ntof/stage3/censor_join.py`). A fit window made of the bundle's
   dead/hot wildcard strips carries no charge measurement; the NNLS puts unbounded charge there (q_sum to 1e22),
   chi2 ~ 0, and the candidate still wins the plane's ranking.  D x (154 dead, many hot channels on connectors 1, 2, 8)
   is hit hardest: 21 % of D's gated beam tracks in run_145 have an all-censored x window, at the hot connector's edge
   (x_local ~ -196 mm, tan x ~ 0, 3-strip seed); in the beam-off runs 69 % of D's gated tracks are censored-window fakes
   (371,692 -> 115,706).  Wall chance level is 0.6 %, real tracks confirm ~90 %.  Rule (chosen on D's late-window
   wall purity): an all-censored x window, or a y window >= 50 % censored.  Late D: keeps 96 % of wall-confirmed
   tracks, removes 16 %, purity 0.69 -> 0.79.  On A it removes early (< 12 ms) tracks on A's dead y edge, which the
   wall confirms less than A's others in every ToF bin; >= 12 ms it removes 87 of A's tracks.  B, C: ~0.
8. **With D's fakes gone the B-D cosmic lines close** (median miss 7 / 4 mm, was 73 / 64; 45 % within 60 mm, was 5 %),
   so finding 6 was D's fakes.  D gets its first external angle anchor: slope against the B-D line z view 0.996,
   y view 0.973 (~2k lines each, +-0.004), residual MAD 0.036-0.037.  The "D ~10 % off its bundle" note in vt_join was
   likely the fakes; D y reads 2.7 % shallow, like A/C's kappa (0.97-0.98).
9. **Same-chamber rate fit** (`same_chamber_compare.FIT_MS`, default 25 ms).  The pair fraction is flat from 25 to 75 ms
   (A 0.41 %, chi2 3.6/7 with a + b rho) while 12-20 ms carries a different population (pointing at the capsule 0.12 vs
   0.30, worst-leg chi2/dof 11.9 vs 2.8, wider separations) that is not proportional to the tracked rate.  Fitting
   12-75 ms forced it into b rho: a was 0.23 %, chi2/ndf 67.  From 25 ms: a = 0.384 +- 0.018 % (A), 0.312 +- 0.019 % (C);
   data/G4 0.10 -> 0.18 (A) / 0.14 (C); the shape tables use real pairs >= 25 ms too.  The correlated opening-angle
   shape is close to the event-mixed one, not Geant4's one-photon e+e-: two independent particles from one capture
   (e.g. a gamma cascade) rather than conversions.  That is the source-model question, not tracking.
10. **The three views side by side** (2026-11-21, `compare_chains.py` -> `ana/track_quality/chains.csv`, all 106 rows
   filled).  The views are no map (`ana_nomap`), map only (`ana_map`), and map + censored-window cut (`ana`, the
   analysis view).  The map + cut view is better than the no-map view on almost every metric:
   - The run_149 resolution is 20-30 % better (A x 0.028 -> 0.020).  Run_149 was in the map's training data, so the
     held-out gains of finding 4 are the honest number.
   - The A-C focus asymmetry falls from 0.046 to 0.008.  The common angle scale moves from 0.997 to 0.977.
   - C's source height moves from -32.9 to -30.0 mm, next to A's -30.7 mm.
   - The scintillator wall confirms the cut.  D's along-bar correlation rises from 0.21 to 0.37, and the fraction of
     D tracks pointing at the fired segment rises from 0.51 to 0.59.  A's along-bar correlation rises from 0.55 to 0.64.
   - The capsule pose fit improves in z (chi2 -40 to -50 %).  However, it worsens in x by 5-20 % (e.g. 34 -> 41 for
     xb-5).  We have not explained the x-view rise yet.
   - The same-chamber pairs barely change (data/G4 0.17 for A and 0.14 for C).
   - The z band excess seen through D halves with the cut (A-D 0.040 -> 0.021).  The data band fraction hardly moves
     (0.089 -> 0.084).  Instead, the band fraction of the null rises from 0.049 to 0.063, and the fitted width narrows
     from 44 to 32 mm.  So the earlier D z excess appears to have been inflated by D's fake tracks, which pulled the
     null down.
   **The pose-fit x chi2 rise is the crossings term, not the shape.**  The x-view chi2 is a crossings part (A and C
   late x crossings vs the toy, 1 mm each) plus a shape part (x crossing vs height).  The shape part improves (best over
   poses 19.5 -> 17.9 for xb-10).  In the toy, x_A - x_C is +2.3..2.7 mm for all 287 poses, so no pose can change it;
   in the data it was +0.67 mm without the map and is +0.11 mm with it (A's late and mid windows also agree now:
   -10.87 / -10.96 mm, were 0.8 mm apart).  So the data became more consistent and the gap to the toy grew.  Which side
   carries the 2.3-2.7 mm (A-C relative x alignment, or the toy geometry) is open.  `compare_chains.py` now reports the
   two parts and the data's A - C difference separately.
   The before/after page (`rerun/ntof/compare_page/make_page.py`, notes/ntof-pass1-before-after) now shows the
   analysis view as "after", reads every after number from the products, and has a section 7 with the three views.
   The chain first stopped at `scint_ana`, because `PASS1_DOWNSTREAM.sh` ran it on arms ABCD and pass 1 gives B no
   angles.  It now passes `--arms ACD`.  `pv_vertex3d` also needed `plotly` in the venv.  `run_map_tail.sh` reruns
   the steps from `scint_ana` on (or the steps in `$TAIL`) on both views, one after the other.
