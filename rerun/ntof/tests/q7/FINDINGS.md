## Method
- **Fit once, score every configuration.** `q7lib.fit_multi` fits each candidate window once, then per configuration
  (tan_max 0.5/0.6/0.7/0.8/1.0/column window × split count/charge × select gate/cost) redoes plausibility, ranking, the
  joint two-track stage and the selection. The joint fit is memoised; only its plausibility and acceptance flags are
  recomputed.
- **Checked against production:** bit-identical to `wft.reco._worker_fit` on bundle copies carrying each configuration,
  10 (configuration, arm) cases, 30–40 multi-candidate events each (`check_A.json`, `check_C.json`).
- **Real beam:** condor (production `ntof_reco.py`, bundle copies, clusters 4617009 and 4616913) ran all 24
  configurations on run_145 stat090_0000, 7 acquisitions × 2 arms, and 7 configurations on run_149 cos_0000–0011.
- **Overlay bench:** `q7_overlay.py`, a copy of `intra_bench` on the ntof_reco path (rr1 hits, min strips 3, dead
  padding, 'replace' overlay; same acquisition and trigger phase, |Δt0| < 60 ns; steep donors, max |tan| ≥ 0.6,
  included on purpose and stratified by tan class SS/SX/XX and separation).

## What this does not rule out
- An overlay is not an IPC pair: no common vertex, t0 agrees only within 60 ns.
- Truth is each donor's own fit, so a mis-fitted donor is scored against its mis-fit.
- XX pairs (both members steep) are thin on A: 5–40 overlays per separation bin.
- Pointing is a statistical truth; differences of a few tens of tracks are 1–2σ.
- One beam sub-run, chambers A and C only, t0 free (the pass pins t0: Q2).
- `rerun/pairing_sim/sim_overlay.py` was not used: its donors are 0–14° muons, with no steep tracks, so it cannot see
  this failure.
