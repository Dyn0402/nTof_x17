5. What this does not rule out

  • Overlay donors are real single beam tracks, but a sum of two triggers is not an IPC pair: no common vertex, the two members' t0 agree only to |Δt0| < 60 ns rather than exactly, and the steep donors are whatever run_145 provides (steep beam tracks as a population show no capsule-pointing excess, table 2). Truth is each donor's own fit, so a donor that is itself mis-fitted is scored against its mis-fit.
  • The XX class (both members steep) is thin on A (5–40 overlays per separation bin).
  • Capsule pointing is a statistical truth (a sideband-subtracted excess); differences of a few tens of tracks between configurations are at the 1–2σ level even though the samples are shared.
  • Chambers A and C only; B and D carry no angle and were not tested.
  • One beam sub-run (run_145 stat090_0000) and run_149 cosmics; other run conditions (run_79 connector 8, late runs) are not covered. t0 is free (v1k); a pinned-t0 bundle could shift the two-track numbers.
  • Condor CPU numbers are ±30 % (mixed nodes).
  • rerun/pairing_sim/sim_overlay.py was not used: its simulated donors are muons at 0–14° with det3 bench conditions, so it contains no steep donor and cannot see the failure tested here.
