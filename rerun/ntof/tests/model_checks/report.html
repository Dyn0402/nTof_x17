n_TOF pass 1: the three model checks before the bundles freeze

Verdicts

  • 1 · Template: holds on A/C; D holds in time, not at the peak. Head-on n_TOF cosmics against the bench head-on cache of the same chamber, same code, centre-strip residual over k = −1…+6: A max |Δ| 0.017 of the peak (late side k ≥ +1: 0.017), C max |Δ| 0.018 of the peak (late side k ≥ +1: 0.018), D max |Δ| 0.130 of the peak (late side k ≥ +1: 0.046) (tolerance 0.03). The ion tail in the template is short (0.14 of the peak 300 ns after it, an undershoot from 450 ns), and nothing is seen growing after the column end (post-column residual ≤ 0.02 of the peak, A/C). D's excess sits at the peak only (centre strip +0.15 vs bench +0.02): a lateral footprint mismatch (it falls to +0.11 with the Magboltz Dp, §3), not the template's time shape. D beam planes are dominated by D's oscillating channels and are not a template test. Toy: a ±5 % tail or ±3 % time-stretch mismatch moves angles ≤ 0.08° and p0 ≤ 0.15 mm. Stated systematic: 0.1° on angles, 0.15 mm on positions (all arms).
  • 2 · Saturation: keep 3550. 3700 lets 1.5–5× as many truly clipped samples through as exact data (cosmics: 31→52 % on A x, 6→32 % on C x, 27→50 % on D x), and buys back only ~25 % of the real samples that 3550 censors on beam, where the CM of a block carrying a big signal sits at −300 ADC and the true rail is ~4080. On the events where the rule can matter (a sample ≥ 3500: ~2 % of beam, ~6 % of cosmic seeded events) 3–11 % of A/C planes and 19–27 % of D planes move, all of them already in the saturated, high-χ² class; the A–C line does not move, capsule pointing does not move on A/C and is 3 tracks better at 3550 on D (30 vs 27 of ~95 within 15 mm). Neither number is the rail; the per-sample rail (SAT_RAIL) is the correct censor and needs a two-line change in ntof_tracking/wft_beam.py (FINDINGS.md).
  • 3 · Dp for 90/10: change C and D, A stays measured. A: Magboltz 0.0199 (H₂O 0.24 %), n_TOF stack 0.0244/0.0250, χ² minimum 0.0232 → 0.0245 ± 0.0014 (now 0.023); C: Magboltz 0.0136 (H₂O 0.67 %), n_TOF stack not a measurement, χ² minimum 0.0137 → 0.0136 ± 0.0008 (now 0.018); D: Magboltz 0.0157 (H₂O 0.49 %), n_TOF stack not a measurement, χ² minimum 0.0180 → 0.0157 ± 0.0009 (now 0.0225). A wrong Dp by 15–30 % moves angles by ≤ 0.15° (toy).

What these checks do not rule out

  • The template is tested on what n_TOF has: 20 samples, a column that starts in the first samples, and the tail only for A (C and D columns end 0–250 ns before the window does). A tail change that shows only beyond ~500 ns after the column end is not seen here, and does not enter the n_TOF fit either.
  • The beam samples are low-amplitude (median peak 300–360 ADC) and carry n_TOF noise sources (oscillating channel pairs, second deposits) that the selection removes by rule, not by a channel census (P2 is not built).
  • The saturation reco comparison covers the high-ADC events of one cosmic sub-run (cos_0001) and two beam acquisitions; the A–C line sample inside it is 7 events, so "the line does not move" rests on the fact that the clip changes only already-saturated planes, not on line statistics.
  • Dp for C and D is Magboltz at the water fraction implied by the in-situ timing v and the bench gap; it carries the gap error (D ±3.8 %, C has none: adopted 28 mm) and assumes water is the only contaminant. The bench det6/det7 stacks agreed with Magboltz (ratio 1.03), det3 did not (1.26) and A does not at n_TOF (1.2).
  • A trigger-placed and a threshold-placed stack differ by ~4 % on the bench (det3) and at n_TOF (A); this is in the quoted A error.
