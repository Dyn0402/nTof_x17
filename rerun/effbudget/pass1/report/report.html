What this does not rule out

  • Efficiencies use |r| <= 5 mm at the mesh, which does not depend on v; the interior_good angle s68 in the tables DO depend on v(t) for g_det4, g_det6_long, g_det7_long and the other VT_PLAN rows.
  • The M3 reference is not perfect: far:POS_TAIL (mostly tracks bent between the M3 doublets, C22) is counted as a chamber loss here.
  • det4's dead / low-gain band map (interior_live) is the pre-pass C22 live-ratio map on the same raw events: hardware, not reconstruction, but not re-derived on the pass tables.
  • C22 classes are rebuilt on the pass tables without the M3 cluster trees (m3feat): only the POS_TAIL / M3_ELSEWHERE explanation text is missing, not the class.
  • The row-level noise-band census reads at most 4000 triggers per decoded file (about 30 % of a long run); the golden rows were re-read in full for A1.
  • Skeptical rows (det3 6-22, det3 6-23, det2 6-17) are listed, not quoted. On det3 6-23 the broken angle scale (folded v 1.7-3.5 um/ns) moves right-position fits to fit_failed through the |tan| <= 3 sanity cut: the position-only column is the efficiency there.
  • The spark veto (> 50 strips above the floor) removes 17-21 % of the det6/det7 golden interior tracks, almost all classified as discharges from their waveforms: a property of those chambers at 495 V.
