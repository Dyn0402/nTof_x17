## Verdict

- **Gain (d ln Q / dV), fitted 425–495 V on the scan sub-runs:**

  | chamber | slope (ln Q per 10 V) | ×2 every |
  |---|---|---|
  | det2, 6-22 | 0.418 ± 0.005 | 16.6 V |
  | det3, 6-27 | 0.414 ± 0.002 | 16.7 V |
  | det6, 6-26, drift 700 V | 0.364 ± 0.005 | 19.1 V |
  | det7, 6-26, drift 700 V | 0.388 ± 0.012 | 17.9 V |

  - det3 reproduces r06's 0.4185 ± 0.0023 (`det3-hv-charge` study).
  - **The ladder is curved.** The local slope is 0.38 → 0.48 on det3 and 0.39 → 0.46 on det2, from the low half of the
    range to the high half. Never carry one slope outside its range.
  - det3 6-22 (registry class `skeptical`) scatters (χ² 186/8; 0.456).
- **Using only unsaturated events is not enough for a median.** A median over the unsaturated events alone stops rising
  once about a third of the events saturate. It then measures the 3550 rule, not the chamber.
  - Every number therefore uses the unsaturated events for values and counts each saturated event only by its rank, as
    "larger" (rank-censored quantiles).
  - The gain is fitted to the 25th percentile, on rows with f_sat < 0.6.
  - The rule is exact if every saturated event lies above that percentile. A saturated event's own fitted charge falls
    below it in a few per cent of cases.
- **det6/det7 at their operating point:** f_sat is 0.92 and 0.86, so no quantile of either golden row is measurable.
  The table's values at 495 V are extrapolations of the 400–465 V curves.
- **Reproducible across nights.** det3 at 490/1000 V gives a 25th percentile of 14 800 (6-22), 15 400 and 16 000
  (6-27) and 16 500 (6-27 P2 run): ±6 % across nights.
- **Drift field (det3, 490 V mesh):**
  - the charge reaches its plateau at ~180 V/cm;
  - it peaks at 250 V/cm (+12 %), then falls ~10 % by 390 V/cm (mesh transparency);
  - it is 0.56 at 107 V/cm and 0.19 at 36 V/cm. That drop mixes window truncation, attachment and transparency and is
    not decomposed.
  - The long run at the 1000 V setting reads 8 % above the scan sub-run at the same setting: the sub-run-to-sub-run
    gain stability.
- **X/Y charge balance** (golden rows, unsaturated). This is the rebuilt `charge_balance` figure.

  | chamber | f = Q_X/(Q_X+Q_Y) median | s68 |
  |---|---|---|
  | det2 | 0.513 | 0.016 |
  | det3 | 0.485 | 0.014 |
  | det4 | 0.509 | 0.026 |
  | det6 | 0.504 | 0.032 |
  | det7 | 0.485 | 0.116 |

  - Paper I §5's placeholder text (det3 0.487, det2 0.531, σ68 0.07) is the hits proxy. The waveform charge is 4–5×
    narrower.
  - det7's tail: 26 % of its unsaturated events differ by more than e^0.5. det7 Y has the known cross-card faults, and
    its unsaturated sample is only 14 % of its events.
- **Bench tan sign:** tan = +d(strip coordinate)/dz_up in both views.
  - On 245 of 247 (row, view) pairs with ≥ 200 M3-matched planes, w rises with the M3 tangent (kw > 0 on all 247).
    The two exceptions are det2 6-17 (half-dead X, broken angles, excluded).
  - External facts: M3 z points up (ray files Z_Up 1302 mm on the top plane, cosmic muons go down); the alignment maps
    positions and tangents with one rotation and handedness.
  - So electrons drift down onto the mesh. This is written next to the constant in `wft.reco.tan_of`,
    `docs/PARAMETERS.md` §3.1 and `COORDINATES.md` §4b.
- **SPS tan sign: not determinable from existing data.**
  - At normal incidence the positions carry no depth.
  - On the rotated runs the yaw enters the position alignment only through cos φ (`DET4_URW_MAPPING_2026-08-01.md`:
    yaw = arccos of a scale ratio).
  - No SPS number depends on the sign: only |tan| is quoted. This is a stated limitation.

## Excluded

- **6-23 det3/det4 night:**
  - The M3 reference is degraded (~4 % clean tracks).
  - The charge is flat over 465–525 V in both chambers, although `hv_monitor.csv` shows the resist HV and current
    following the scan.
  - So det4 has **no bench gain curve**; its curve must come from SPS.
  - The sub-runs ran in descending HV through the night, so a gain drift with time could also cancel the scan. The two
    are not separable.
- **det2 6-17:** half-dead X, 1–115 interior events per row.
- **det6's 6-26 night rows:** its gain rose ×10 between short_run and long_run at the same 495 V (the early
  contaminant the v(t) study saw). Only the afternoon HV scan enters det6's curve.

## Method

`charge_rows.py` runs on lxplus and reads `Analysis_pass1` without writing to it. Per bench row of
`rerun/pass_registry.csv` (cls ≠ excluded), it keeps:
- single-track, spark-free events with both views ok, M3-matched in both views (`angles/per_event.npz`);
- the mid-gap position (p0 + 15 mm · tan) at least 30 mm inside `TRUE_ACTIVE_BY_DET`, with det7 X at ≥ 204.7 mm
  (ch 256 + 5 mm) at both column ends.

There is no χ² gate, because a gate fails preferentially on bright events. The charge is (q_x + q_y) · cos θ, with cos θ
taken from the M3 tangents. An event is saturated if either view has a strip at the 3550 rule.

`make_report.py` makes the estimators, the fits, the figures and `report.html`. For the gain fits it requires
n ≥ 100 interior events per row and at least 4 HV points per series.

## Limitations

- Charge is in fit ADC units. There is no electronics fC calibration, so no absolute gain is quoted.
- The 3550 rule misses 5–46 % of truly clipped samples (K4): det6 and det4 the most. Those events count as unsaturated
  and read low.
- The low-field drift points are confounded with the readout window.
- The gain varies inside a row with time. This is not corrected.
- v(t) enters only through the fit window (u_window = 0.30–1.60 × 30 mm / v): q_sum is a linear solve in time bins. So, to first order, these numbers do not wait for the
  v(t) cross-check.
