Verdict

Gain. On every usable scan the charge rises exponentially with the mesh HV. Fitted to the 25th percentile of the interior charge, with saturated events counted by rank only: det2 6-22-26: 0.418 ± 0.005 in ln Q per 10 V (×2 every 16.6 V, 450–495 V); det3 6-22-26: 0.456 ± 0.009 in ln Q per 10 V (×2 every 15.2 V, 450–495 V); det3 6-27-26: 0.414 ± 0.002 in ln Q per 10 V (×2 every 16.7 V, 425–495 V); det6 6-26-26: 0.364 ± 0.005 in ln Q per 10 V (×2 every 19.1 V, 400–465 V); det7 6-26-26: 0.388 ± 0.012 in ln Q per 10 V (×2 every 17.9 V, 400–465 V). The ladder is curved (the local slope rises with HV, columns low/high in the table), as found on r06; do not carry one slope outside its range.

Unsaturated only is not enough by itself. A median over the unsaturated events stops rising once a third of the events saturate (figure 2): above ~500 V on det2/det3 and at the det6/det7 operating points (f_sat 0.86–0.92) it measures the 3550-ADC rule, not the chamber. Every number here therefore comes from the unsaturated events but treats a saturated event as 'large' (rank-censored quantiles), which is exact as long as the saturated events lie above the quantile; their own fitted charge falls below the 25th percentile in 0.0 % of cases (median over rows), so the assumption holds to a few per cent.

Reproducible across nights. det3 at 490 V / 1000 V drift: det2_det3_overnight_6-22-26/resist_490V_drift_1000V 14810 ± 610, det3_p2_det1_overnight_6-27-26/long_run_p2_det1_sanity_check 16539 ± 68, det3_saturday_scan_6-27-26/hv_scan2_resist_490V_drift_1000V 15366 ± 499, det3_saturday_scan_6-27-26/long_run_resist_490V_drift_1000V 15967 ± 115.

What this does not rule out

  • The charge is in fit ADC units. There is no electronics charge calibration of the bench DREAM gain setting in this analysis, so no absolute gain (electrons) is quoted.
  • The 3550 rule misses 5–46 % of truly clipped samples (K4); those events count as unsaturated with a charge biased low. Strongest on det6 (46 %) and det4 (37 %).
  • Rank censoring assumes saturated events are above the quantile; a head-on track concentrates charge and can saturate at a lower total, so a small low-side leak remains (the 'sat below' column of rows.csv).
  • At the lowest drift fields the column can exceed the readout window and drift slowly through the gas; the charge drop there mixes window truncation, attachment and transparency, and is not decomposed.
  • det4 has no usable bench HV scan (the 6-23 night is excluded); its gain curve must come from SPS.
  • det6's gain rose ×10 through the 6-26 night (short_run → long_run at the same 495 V; the v(t) study's early contaminant). Only the 6-26 afternoon HV scan, after it settled, enters the curve.
  • Time dependence of the gain inside a row is not corrected.
