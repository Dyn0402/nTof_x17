Drift velocity within a row: det6 / det7, 6-26 night

Within-row v(t) is real and post-correctable. det6's ladder v rises 17.3 → 31.5 µm/ns (+82 %) from 01:00 to the 10:30 knee on 6-26, +20 % inside the golden long_run; det7 rises with the same time-shape, 31.5 → 39.1 µm/ns (+24 %), and both stop at the same clock time. det6's deficit is 2.80 ± 0.07 × det7's at every time (r = 0.95): one cause common to both chambers, the gas. The correction, one smooth curve per chamber applied as v → v·r(t) in the angle mapping, wins the held-out test (block χ² 24 against 322 for one v per row, ~40 blocks) and moves det6 angles by up to 2.2° at 30°.

Method

  • Clock: acq_tag stem (minute) + DREAM timestamp × 10 ns, from the pass-1 event tables (Analysis_pass1, code 6a0bb1f, read-only).
  • Estimator: the one that gives every row its kw (rerun/w12/set_w0kw.py) on the row's own 03_angles per-event table, after the tan-form bow (t′ = (tan_ref + a)·S): w0 = median(w − v t′) over |t′| < 0.30, kw = median((w − w0)/(v t′)) over 0.10 < |t′| < 0.40. Run on the whole row it reproduces the applied kw to ≤ 1 σ on all 48 rows (golden rows: their unfolded 1.08). Per time block it gives v_lad(t) = v_drift × kw(t).
  • Correction: tan = (w − w0)/(kw · v · r(t) · S) − a, i.e. wft.reco.v_rel_t's v_map['t'] (t_h, v_rel) per row; w is independent of v (GC), so nothing is refitted. Mid-gap position p0 + 15 mm·tan and depth z = v r(t) u move with it.
  • Models: one v per row (pass 1), a line per row, a piecewise-linear through the row's blocks, one exponential per chamber over the night. Held-out: built on even event_id, scored on odd by the residual scale of the odd half's own blocks.

What this does not rule out / limitations

  • The cause inside "the gas" is not identified. The common time-shape and simultaneous knee point to the gas supply, not to the chambers; that det6's deficit is ×2.8 det7's fits a larger dose of the same contaminant in det6 (e.g. its place in the gas chain or its own outgassing), but the bench gas order and flow on 6-26 are not recorded, and the bench gas composition is known only as a fit (beam-gas-attachment study: bench 0.95 % H₂O, no O₂). Water drying out would raise v in both; det6's early attachment and gain loss (clearing by ~4 h) is an additional electronegative component that det7 did not see. No new data can be taken.
  • The two plateaus differ (det6 31.5, det7 39.1 µm/ns at the same 700 V drift): a chamber-side difference (field, gap, method scale), not addressed here.
  • The column-end depth does not confirm the ladder. z_end = v·u_end in det6's long_run falls slightly with time under one v and rises after the correction: the column duration is gain- and attachment-confounded (GC found the ladder moving 1.68× faster than 1/T on det6). The ladder is gain-independent on the plateau rows (table above), so it is the measure of v here; the absolute depth scale carries this disagreement.
  • One exponential does not describe det6's knee at ~10.5 h (χ² 82/38); inside each row it is as good as a line (held-out). It is a smooth interpolator, not a gas model; its τ is not a gas time constant.
  • The angle resolution improves modestly (det6 long_run x 1.50 → 1.46°); most of the gain is in the angle scale at the row ends (up to ~2° at 30° on det6), which the s68 around the median only partly shows.
  • Rows shorter than ~2 blocks (HV-scan rows, 20 min) get the night curve; their within-row change is ≤ 1–2 %.
  • Only det6/det7 6-26. det4's golden (6-24) and its 6-23 rows, and det2 6-23, are the other GC candidates; not done here.
