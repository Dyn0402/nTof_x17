x↔y pairing of two tracks by charge: the simulation study

MX17 reconstruction paper, section "x↔y pairing" (nTof_x17 rerun/pairing_sim/PLAN.md). 2026-10-26. Data: the five bench chambers' golden rows, production final_phys tables. Simulation: MX17_Geant response chain, v4c (det3 conditions: wet gas, σ0 420 µm, bG 0.48, 26.5 mm column), reconstructed by the production wft with det3's golden-row phys bundle.

Verdict. The data side holds on every chamber: pairing by charge is 87–97 % right with charge alone and 90–98 % with the depth profile, and it gets worse where a chamber's x/y charge identity is looser (det6 σ = 0.18, det7 0.14 against det3 0.055). The response simulation fails the gate that matters: its single-track σ ln(q_x/q_y) is {{sim_lq}} against det3's {{d3_lq}} (×{{ratio_lq}}), and its σ(u50_x − u50_y) is {{sim_u50}} ns against {{d3_u50}} ns (×{{ratio_u50}}). Two causes, both in the simulation. (1) Stage B lands each avalanche, and all its feedback secondaries, at one point, so the x/y split samples the sub-pad split function once per primary electron: already at the induced-charge level (no electronics) the sim's split is {{truth_lq}} = {{truth_a}}/√N; displacing the feedback secondaries (a registered diagnostic) brings it to 0.035, as predicted. (2) The sim's waveforms carry more x/y scatter than the data's (model-free, same strips: {{mf_sim_pos}}–{{mf_sim_sum}} against {{mf_data_pos}}–{{mf_data_sum}}), and production wft fits them worse than it fits data (fit {{sim0_lq}} against {{ref0_lq}}; it stays at 0.095–0.108 when (1) is removed). Per PLAN §4 the sim goes back to MX17_Geant before S3–S5 numbers enter the paper. What the sim does give: the same mechanism (pairing fails only for near-equal charges), a both-found vs separation curve close to the data's, and a pessimistic bound — with twice the data's x/y spread the simulated overlay still pairs {{nom_ge24_q}} right (charge) and {{nom_ge24_qu}} (charge + profile) at ≥ 24 mm, and {{n2_ge24_q}} / {{n2_ge24_qu}} with the n_TOF noise floor (×2).

3 · Where the sim's x/y spread comes from

{{fig_mech}}

Left: the truth-level split (charge induced on all X vs all Y channels, before shaping and noise) narrows exactly as 1/√N with the number of electrons N reaching the mesh: each avalanche lands its whole charge (and, at H = 0, its feedback secondaries) at one point, so it contributes one random draw of the split. Right: the same strips (production footprint ± 3 mm), summed the same way, on data and sim: the data are 2× tighter at every level. The table follows the split through the chain: induced → waveform (model-free) → wft fit. Removing the per-avalanche coin (displaced secondaries) fixes the first step and part of the second, not the third: the fit adds ~0.08 in quadrature on the sim's waveforms against ~0.04 on the data's, the same direction as RECO_TRUTH's unexplained wft nonlinearity on simulated waveforms.

6 · What this does not rule out, and what is left

  • The sim is not validated for pairing. Its x/y split is ~1.9× too wide and its u50/u90 spread ~1.5× too wide, so its pairing numbers are a lower bound, not a prediction. The Landau width passes once the data's own gain map is taken out (the sim muons cross one 31 mm spot).
  • n_TOF conditions are emulated by noise only: the beam gas (1.5 % H2O, ~160 ppm O2 attachment) needs a Stage B gas table with attachment that does not exist yet; attachment removes late charge from both views alike, so it should change the profile features more than the charge.
  • S3 (X17 / IPC e+e− pairs from a common vertex) and S5 (three tracks) are not run. The Stage A gun is ready (mm_sim --primaries-table, MX17_Geant), smoke-tested with 2- and 3-primary events; what is missing is a fixed sim (above) and the pair table from ipc_born.py with the n_TOF chamber geometry.
  • The data toy is at infinite separation; the overlay is the only measurement with merging and shared strips, and it is det3 only.
  • det6 / det7's wider x/y spread is measured, not explained.
