## C8 · The calibration's reference position biases v — CONFIRMED on det3 (fleet running)

First C7 results (K = 18, σ0_x pinned 0.25): v = 41.2 (det2), **42.1 (det3)**, 25.3 (det6), 37.7 (det7) µm/ns. det3's
42.1 is above dry Magboltz at its field (38.5) and 20 % above what free fits read under the same kernel (§12: slope
0.96 at 36.6 → 35 µm/ns); det6 stays 33 % below det7 on the same night and field.

Cause: the ref-pinned objective fixes p0 at `ref_mesh`, the M3 track at the plane the **hits-chain alignment**
(`alignment_tpc_veto50`, `wft.calibrate` line ~63) placed the detector. Hit times are compressed 20–30 %
(RECONSTRUCTION_BASIS.md), so that plane is biased, and a depth offset is absorbed as dv ≈ dz / ⟨drift time⟩.

Test (`rerun/precheck_vscan/v_scan.py`, det3, 60 calibration events, RC constants of the rcv25 fit fixed, recal.py's
cold objective):

| p0 | χ² at the minimum | v at the minimum |
|---|---|---|
| pinned at ref_mesh (calibration as run) | 4.60e7 | **39.6** |
| profiled ±1 mm (recal.py --p0-profile) | 3.07e7 (−34 %) | **37.0** |

→ W1 changes: the reference geometry used for calibration must not come from the hits alignment. Calibrate with p0
profiled, run the reco, align on the waveform positions (`mx_june_wft/01_alignment.py`), and if a pinned calibration
is wanted, rebuild `ref_mesh` from that alignment and iterate until v is stable. Every bundle made so far, r06
included, was calibrated against the hits-based reference. Fleet: condor 4538813 (det2, det3 K24, det6 and det7 at
K 18 and 24).

## C9 · The reconstruction's common mode moves with the signal — fix written (opt-in), validation pending

`wft.io` subtracts the plain per-sample median of each 64-channel block. On det7 (6-26, 495 V, FEU 08, 4000 events)
the track clusters are ~17 strips wide, and against a median that masks the cluster ±3 strips:

| strips of the block masked | baseline error at the pulse peak, median / p90 | largest error in the window, median |
|---|---|---|
| 1–8 | 0 / 3.5 ADC | 3.5 ADC |
| 8–20 | 2.5 / 15 ADC | 9–10 ADC |
| 20–40 | 4 / 51 ADC | 104 ADC |

Under 1 % of a ~4000 ADC peak, but applied to every strip of the block: 10–25 % of a neighbour's tail, which is what
the RC kernel's σ0 and D_rc, and the D_T stacks, are measured from. cloud_basics' stacks used a masked common mode;
the reconstruction never did. Fix: `wft.io.masked_block_median`, opt-in `WFT_CM_MASK=<half-width>` (default 0 =
bit-identical), reference-free two-pass median; on a toy (30-strip track in one block) the baseline error on the quiet
strips goes from −16 to −1.3 ADC. → W11: rebuild the golden calibration caches with it and gate it (resolution, χ²,
σ0, D_rc) before the freeze.
