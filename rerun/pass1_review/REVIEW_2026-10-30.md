# Pass 1: independent review of the products, 2026-10-30

I read the products on EOS (`Analysis_pass1`) directly, not the ledger: every row's meta, angles, efficiency, alignment,
w0kw and bundle, plus each events table (bench and SPS). Per-row table: `pass1_rows.csv` (`collect.py`). Alignment z of
every row: `alignment_z_all.txt`. Fit sanity counts: `wild.py`. SPS per-row table: `sps.py`. All of them run on lxplus with the lock venv.

## Verdict

**The reconstruction tables are sound, but four rows' positions and efficiencies are wrong.** Their own alignment failed:
the z scan ran to the edge of its window. The cause is a few absurd fits that the position step does not guard
against. The fix needs no reconstruction, only 01/02/04 re-run.

## What checks out (independently confirmed)
- 156/156 non-excluded bench rows have events, angles, efficiency, alignment and maps. All STAGES are on `6a0bb1f`, with 0
  overrides. 71/71 SPS rows are present (68 on `6a0bb1f`, 3 on `d34cf25`).
- Every bench bundle carries the bow block and a dead list. D_rc,y is one value per chamber (the CAL4 value; PASS1_STATUS quotes
  the it0 value, which differs by 2–3 %, within the 5 % convergence criterion).
- Golden-row s68 and detection agree with the G1 table (the event sets differ slightly: the full row against the 8000-event RECO3).
- Consistency rows behave physically. det3: v rises monotonically with drift field (2.6 → 38.8 µm/ns from 100 to 1100 V);
  kw stays 1.00–1.05 on the 1000 V HV scans; s68 is 0.84–1.20° up to 505 V and degrades at 515–525 V with saturation. det2 HV
  scan: kw 0.95–0.99, efficiency falls above 510 V. The χ²/dof median grows ×1000 across the HV scans, so the
  relative (5 × median) quality rule is the right one.
- Skeptical rows look exactly as recorded: det3 6-22 has v 10–15 and s68 4–5°, and det3 6-23 has v 1.7/3.5 and s68 15–60°. The
  6-23 det3 v failure is **not** an alignment failure, because its vrefit alignment0 is fine (z 243).

## Problems found

### P1 · Four rows: alignment z railed, positions and efficiency wrong
| row | z_x / z_y (mm) | within 5 mm | far | events |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| det7 6-26 overnight longer_run | 642 / 642 | 6.5 % | 59.4 % | 4466 |
| det7 6-26 hv_scan 435 V | 643 / 642 | 9.8 % | 70.7 % | 820 |
| det3 6-27 hv_scan2 510 V | 760 / 642 | 47.0 % | 41.7 % | 782 |
| det3 6-27 hv_scan 525 V | 642 / 762 | 20.9 % | 28.5 % | 451 |

The same night's other rows sit at 711–715 mm. The vrefit alignment0 of these rows railed the same way. Angles and w0/kw do
not depend on z (M3 matching is by event id), so their angle s68 is usable.

Mechanism:
- A handful of events per row have `ok` set (some also `quality_ok`) with |tan θ| of 1e3–6e8.
- `wft.reco.bow_h` evaluates the bow polynomial at the absurd position, which gives h up to 2e11 mm.
- `wft.compat.mesh_position` = p0 − tan·h then returns absurd positions.
- `_robust_sigma` (`cosmic_micro_tpc_analysis.py:774`): the peak fit fails, and it falls back to the **plain RMS**, so σ ≈ 6e16 mm at every z.
  `nanargmin` of a flat curve returns the window edge.

The golden det7 row's Y scan shows the same fragility: 228 mm spikes, with the minimum found only by luck.

Fleet scale (|tan| > 3 with ok): bench 868 X / 1779 Y of 215,831 events (0.4 / 0.8 %), present in 112 of 156 rows; 246 X / 381 Y
events with |h| > 10 mm. Most rows survive because the peak fit converges.

**Fix (no reconstruction):**
1. `mesh_position` returns NaN when |tan| > a physical bound (bench M3 acceptance |tan| ≲ 1.5; a bound of 3 is safe), or when |h| > a few mm.
2. `_robust_sigma` falls back to 1.4826 × MAD instead of the RMS.
3. Add a `tan_sane` flag to the quality flags (|tan| ≤ 3), so downstream cuts see it.
4. Re-run 01/02/04 on all bench rows (minutes per row). Then check that every row's z is within ±5 mm of its night's golden row.
5. Extend `pass1_ledger.py` with that z check and a "within_R not collapsed against the night" check.

The ledger and G1 cannot see this failure: the ledger only checks presence, and G1 looks at golden rows only.

### P2 · SPS RAW rows (run71 raw275/450/700) unusable as they are
15–19 % of events have |tan| > 3 and still pass quality_ok, and the position residual s68 to the reference is 9–15 mm (the ZS rows give 0.3–0.8 mm).
This is consistent with the known RAW packet loss (~24 % of sample groups). Exclude them from SPS results or treat them separately.

### P3 · SPS angle scale is uncalibrated on rows without v_span
`v_drift` is null in the meta of 34 rows (run55, run56, run57, run59, run60, run61 d070–d280, run62 d200, run70 d100/d200, raw275). On the inclined
CO2 rows (run57/59/60, mount 25.64°) the reconstructed Y angle reads −6…−10°, against −20…−25° on the rows that have v_span.
Use these rows for positions and efficiency only, or give them a v first.

### P4 · Smaller items
- The excluded `det3 quick 6-26 530V/1200V` row still has a product directory in `Analysis_pass1`. Downstream globbing must
  go through the registry, or the directory must be removed.
- det4 6-23 485 V: z_x 719 against 713. It is the calib/align key for five other det4 rows. The effect is small (within_R is
  in line with its neighbours), but check it after the P1 fix.
- SPS run60 flush ov07: x_ok 65 % against 83–86 % on the other plateaus, and y_ok 98 %. Probably a mixed plateau; look before using it.
- Gas drift is large on the 6-26 night. The folded v (v·kw) of det6 falls from 31 (HV scan, daytime) to 26 (golden), then 20 (longer_run)
  and 18 µm/ns (short_run). det7 falls 39 → 37 → 34 → 32. The v(t) correction (GC) is a prerequisite for any det6/det7 angle or
  depth result from those rows.
