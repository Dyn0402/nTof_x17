## Verdict

**Every SPS epoch carried water and O₂.** Every group is quoted at one drift gap, taken from the clean RAW run71 stacks:
27.7 ± 0.1 mm, quoted at the profiled 27.9 mm.

- **CF₄ epoch** (Sun 22:00 → Mon 05:50): water **1.68–1.85 %**, O₂ **130–260 ppm**.
- **CO₂ epoch** (Sat 14:25 → 20:30): O₂ **320–440 ppm**, water **1.9–2.1 %**. The CO₂ water is the least certain number:
  ±0.2–0.5 %, on a shaper fitted in CF₄.
- **The O₂ is required.** Without air, at the same water, a stack's χ² is 1.5–117× worse (median 33×).
- **The O₂ is higher in the CO₂ epoch, and falls from run55 to run59.** The baseline and all 10 systematic arms keep that
  ordering.
- **Individual O₂ values carry ±90–130 ppm**, almost all of it systematic.
- **The water is degenerate with the drift gap and the shaper.** It is quoted to ±0.15–0.5 %, not better.

This agrees with the earlier, pre-pass fit (cloud_basics FINDINGS §25, 2026-10-10): run71 1.51 % / 163 ppm, run63 flat
147 ppm, CO₂ ~290 ppm.
- **Water:** the difference comes from the gap. The earlier fit labelled the fields with 30 mm, and closure.py shows a 30 mm

- **run71 O₂:** the earlier fit kept saturated events. With them kept here (arm `sat`), run71 reads 161 ppm.

How the errors are built:
- **stat:** the 68 % half-width at the quoted gap, with χ² scaled by χ²/ndf; never less than half a grid step.
- **gap:** half the spread over the gaps 27.0 / 27.9 / 28.8 mm.
- **arms:** the systematic arms below, added in quadrature (for a ± pair, the larger shift).
- No quoted value sits on a grid edge.
- The run71 χ² against gap is 456 / 444 / 406 / 511 / 523 at 26.2 / 27.0 / 27.9 / 28.8 / 30.0 mm.

## Method

1. **Stacks** (`gas_stacks.py`, condor cluster 4602763, outputs in `/eos/user/d/dneff/x17rerun/sps_gasfit/stacks/`).
   - **Builder and selection:** the final SPS pass's own cache builder, `sps_build_cache.build_cache`, with the seed fix,
     the per-plateau hot probe and the RAW common mode. 4000 events per row, on all 43 64-sample rows.
   - **No alignment:** stacks are placed by DREAM sample index, with no alignment to the pulse.
   - **Strip offsets** are counted from the seed strip.
   - **RAW drops** are treated as missing. **ZS zeros** are measured zeros: a window strip with no kept sample counts as
     zero.
   - **Saturated events** (any sample ≥ 3550) are kept apart.
   - **RAW rows** also get the FEU zero suppression emulated at 4 and 5σ of each channel's pre-trigger noise.
   - **Errors:** jackknife over 20 time-ordered blocks.
   - **run60 ov07:** events before the beam returned (91170 s) are dropped.
2. **Head-on views only.** X uses ±2 strips and Y ±8. On the 25.64° mount only the narrower view is used. The inclined
   view's strip sum truncates with depth inside the ±16-strip window, which would fake a gradient.
3. **Model** (`gasfit.py`), the physics of cloud_basics FINDINGS §25:
   - `predict.current_field`: uniform ionisation over the gap, with Magboltz v, η and D_L on the (water, air) grid
     `air_merged`. Air means N₂/O₂/Ar 78.08/20.95/0.93 replacing argon, so O₂ = air × 2095 ppm.
   - η and D_L are smoothed in log E (the stack-artefact trap 4).
   - A parametric shaper, fitted on RAW run71 (three fields, X and Y) together with its composition, then fixed.
   - Free per stack: t0 (coarse scan, then least squares) and amplitude.
   - ZS stacks get the additive ZS distortion (RAW-emulated − RAW) of run71 at the nearest field, same view, strip sum and
     threshold. It is additive, not a ratio (trap 3).
4. **Fitting:** a χ² grid per group over (water, air) at each profiled gap (26.2, 27.0, 27.9, 28.8, 30.0 mm). The field is
   HV / gap. `verdict.py` takes the gap from the RAW group (a parabola through its χ²) and quotes every group there.
5. **Closure** (`closure.py`, `results/closure.json`).
   - Toys from the same model come back at the grid node nearest the truth.
   - A 30 mm truth fitted at 28.8 mm moves water by +0.12–0.18 % and leaves the air unchanged.

## Why the Magboltz grid had to grow (`make_mb_ext.py`, merged with `mb_fine_collect.py` into `air_merged`)

The cloud_basics `air_hs` grid was built around the old first-pass solutions:
- beam: water 1.4–1.7 %, air 0–0.13 %;
- CO₂: water 1.4–1.8 %, air 0–0.25 %, at 150–275 V/cm only.

The first fits hit its edges in three places:
- **Water.** Profiling the gap needs a slower gas at short gap. The CF₄ ladders went to the 1.7 % edge. The CO₂ groups
  wanted up to 2.2 %, then up to the 2.2 % edge of round 1.
- **Field.** The CO₂ rows reach 139 V/cm (run55 d400), below the CO₂ grid's lowest field, 150 V/cm. Holding the transport
  at the grid edge instead of extrapolating it also mattered for the k = ±0.1 field-profile arm at 27 mm. Its fields reach
  ~280 V/cm against the grid's 275.
- **O₂.** At 27 mm both CF₄ ladders sat on the beam grid's 0.13 % air edge (272 ppm).

The fix came in two rounds, 3e8 collisions per point, on condor clusters 4602764 and 4605185:
- **Round 1, 649 points:** beam water 1.2–2.0 %; CO₂ water 1.0–2.2 % at 100–275 V/cm.
- **Round 2, 316 points:** beam air to 0.20 %; CO₂ water to 3.0 %.

Both grids are complete in `air_merged`. The midpoint check (cloud_basics `results/mb_mid_check.json`, 24 points) puts
the interpolation error at:
- **beam:** ≤ 0.003 % water and ≤ 16 ppm O₂;
- **CO₂:** ≤ 0.011 % water, and ≤ 11 ppm O₂ except one point (w 1.9, a 0.175, 243 V/cm: 53 ppm, the Magboltz η scatter).
Both are small against the quoted errors.

## Degeneracies

- **Water ↔ drift gap.** Both act through v = gap / T: 1.2 mm of gap is worth 0.12–0.18 % water. The air does not move.
- **Gap ↔ shaper.** The shaper fitted at each gap differs.
  - At 26.2–27.9 mm it rails at the CR-RC order bound (60), a near-Gaussian.
  - At 28.8–30 mm it is order 1.4–1.8, with a large fast undershoot.
  - Statistically the RAW χ² picks the gap to ~0.1 mm. Because of the shaper freedom, one profile step (0.9 mm) is the
    systematic that is carried.
  - The bench-template arm, with no free shaper, moves the results by ≤ 0.06 % water and ≤ 41 ppm O₂.
  - **Checked against a geometric gap (2026-11-01, `rerun/det4_gap/README.md`).** det4's gap from the strip extent of
    inclined tracks, with no v and no pulse model, is 28.6 ± 0.5 mm. That comes from the bench Y view, validated on det3 and
    det2; the SPS rot25 plateau at the ZS floor is 28.1–28.9 mm, a lower bound.
    - The 27.7–27.9 mm used here is within one profile step of it.
    - At the nearest profiled gap (28.8 mm) the water is 0.10–0.19 % lower (CF₄ 1.57–1.70 %, CO₂ 1.75–1.94 %) and the O₂
      ~30–40 ppm lower: inside the carried gap term.
    - The ordering of the O₂ between epochs is unchanged.
    - The bench input 30.48 mm would be outside the RAW χ² profile (Δχ² ≈ 117 at 30.0 mm). The geometric gap removes that tension.
- **Air ↔ electronics droop.** Attachment loses charge at a rate per time similar at every field, as a slow droop would.
  - The RAW fit breaks this with the post-drift-end shape: no undershoot at the end, three fields, a fixed shaper.
  - On ZS stacks the post-end region relies on the RAW emulation. Arm `zswin`, which fits ZS stacks only up to 2.9 µs, is
    the largest single O₂ arm, at 81 ppm.
- **Water ↔ ZS distortion.** ZS removes small and negative samples, which shapes the plateau slope and the tail. The ZS
  groups read 0.1–0.2 % wetter than RAW run71 in the same CF₄ gas, and their χ²/ndf is 3–12, against 1.3 for RAW.
- **The absolute ppm scale** rests on Magboltz's three-body O₂ attachment (O₂ + e + M, with M = H₂O or isobutane). It is
  uncertain to a factor of a few. The ordering between epochs does not depend on it.

Systematic arms (baseline → arm, at 27.9 mm; full table in the report):

| arm | what changes |

| `zs05`, `zs15` | the ZS distortion ×0.5 / ×1.5 |

## What it does not rule out (limitations)

- **The flush is only indicative.** run60, the CO₂ → CF₄ flush, is fitted in each pure base gas: the grid has no mixed
  base. Its values are indicative only (`report/figures/transition.png`). From early to late both bases give 290 → 226 ppm
  (CF₄) and 322 → 242 ppm (CO₂). The CF₄ base fits badly at the start (χ² 492/50) and well at the end (90/50), as the flush
  model expects.
- **The 32-sample rows are not used.** run61, run66 and run70 have a column that overruns the 1.92 µs window.
- **run59 and run63 flat** are single-field groups: their water rests on one v.
- **The CO₂ water is the least certain number.** It uses a shaper fitted in CF₄, and the ion tail depends on the gas.
- **The ZS groups fit at χ²/ndf 3–12.** The model with the run71 ZS correction does not describe them to their statistical
  error, so their errors are χ²-scaled and the arms cover what the scaling cannot.
- **The O₂ is not monotonic through the CF₄ night** (258 → 258 → 129 → 226 ppm). Within ±90 ppm per point, the existing
  data cannot separate a real overnight change from the ZS and mount systematics.
- **Y positions** do not enter this fit, so the residual Y junk on four CO₂-epoch rows (`rerun/sps_audit`) is irrelevant
  here. The Y stacks are strip sums around the seed, and the seed already excludes the oscillating channels.
- **No gas record** (flow, water) exists for the SPS week, so nothing independent can check these numbers
  (GAS_FLUSH_TIMELINE). No new data can be taken.
- **Not used by the reconstruction.** Gas-dependent constants are measured per row, so this is a description of the gas,
  not a reconstruction input.
