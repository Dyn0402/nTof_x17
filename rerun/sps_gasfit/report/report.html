SPS gas composition (H₂O + O₂) from the final pass

Verdict. Every SPS epoch carried both water and O2. With the drift gap from the RAW run71 stacks (27.7 ± 0.1 mm, quoted at 27.9 mm): CF4 epoch water 1.68-1.85 %, O2 129-258 ppm; CO2 epoch O2 322-443 ppm (per group: co2_run55 1.94 ± 0.51 % water, 443 ± 117 ppm O2; co2_run56 2.00 ± 0.42 % water, 363 ± 131 ppm O2; co2_run57 2.00 ± 0.23 % water, 322 ± 94 ppm O2; co2_run59 2.12 ± 0.49 % water, 322 ± 94 ppm O2; cf4_run62_ladder 1.85 ± 0.23 % water, 258 ± 98 ppm O2; cf4_run63_ladder 1.80 ± 0.20 % water, 258 ± 94 ppm O2; cf4_run63_flat 1.82 ± 0.16 % water, 129 ± 93 ppm O2; cf4_run71_raw 1.68 ± 0.17 % water, 226 ± 87 ppm O2). The O2 is required: removing it at the same water multiplies a stack's chi2 by 1.5-117 (median 33). It is higher in the CO2 epoch than in the CF4 epoch, and falls from co2_run55 to co2_run59 in the baseline and in all 10 arms; individual values carry ~±90-130 ppm, dominated by the systematic arms (largest: zswin, 81 ppm). The water is degenerate with the drift gap and the shaper and is quoted to ±0.15-0.5 %; the CO2-epoch water rests on a shaper fitted in the CF4 gas and is the least certain number.

Method

  • Stacks: the final SPS pass's own cache builder (sps_build_cache: seed fix, per-plateau hot probe, RAW common mode), 4000 events per row, trigger-placed (sample index), strip offsets from the seed strip, RAW drops missing, ZS zeros measured, saturated events (any sample >= 3550) kept apart (arm: kept).
  • Strip sums: head-on views only (X +-2, Y +-8 strips; on the 25.64 deg mount the narrower view); the inclined view is left out (its strip sum truncates with depth inside the +-16-strip window).
  • Model (cloud_basics FINDINGS §25): uniform ionisation, Magboltz v, eta, D_L on the (water, air) grid at 3e8 collisions (eta smoothed in log E; grid extended for this fit to beam water 1.2-2.0 %, CO2 water 1.0-2.2 %, CO2 fields 100-275 V/cm), a parametric shaper fitted on RAW run71 (three fields, X and Y) jointly with its composition, per stack t0 and amplitude.
  • ZS stacks: + the additive ZS distortion (RAW-emulated minus RAW) of run71 at the nearest field, same view and strip sum, at the row's ZS threshold.
  • Field = drift HV / gap; the gap is profiled (26.2, 27.0, 27.9, 28.8, 30.0 mm) and taken from the RAW group; every group is quoted at that gap.
  • Closure (closure.py): toy stacks from the same model are recovered exactly on the grid; a 30 mm truth fitted at 28.8 mm moves water by +0.13-0.15 % and leaves air unchanged.

Degeneracies

  • Water vs drift gap: both enter through v = gap / T. 1.2 mm of gap is worth ~0.13-0.15 % water (closure); air does not move.
  • Gap vs shaper: the shaper fitted at each gap differs (at 26.2-27.9 mm it rails at the CR-RC order bound, 60: a near-Gaussian; at 28.8-30 mm order 1.4-1.8 with a large fast undershoot); the RAW chi2 picks the gap to ~0.1 mm statistically, but the shaper freedom makes one profile step (0.9 mm) the honest systematic. The bench-template arm (no free shaper) moves the compositions by <= 0.06 % water and <= 40 ppm O2.
  • Air vs electronics droop: attachment loses charge at the same rate per time at every field, as a slow droop would. It is broken by the post-drift-end shape (undershoot) and by the RAW multi-field fit that fixes the shaper; on ZS stacks the post-end region rests on the RAW ZS emulation (arm: ZS rows fitted only to 2.9 us).
  • Water vs ZS distortion: ZS removes small and negative samples (the plateau slope and the tail); the ZS groups prefer a different gap and more water than RAW run71 in the same CF4 gas. The ZS correction scale (x0.5 / x1.5) is an arm.
  • Magboltz three-body O2 attachment (O2 + e + M, M = H2O / isobutane) sets the absolute ppm scale to a factor of a few; the epoch-to-epoch ordering does not depend on it.

Limits

  • run60 (the CO2 -> CF4 flush) is fitted in either pure base only: the grid has no mixed base gas, so its compositions are indicative (the trend, not the values).
  • The 32-sample rows (run61, run66, run70) are not used: the column overruns the 1.92 us window.
  • Single-field groups (run59, run63 flat) constrain water only through one v.
  • The shaper is fitted on the CF4 RAW run and applied to the CO2 epoch; the ion tail is gas-dependent, so the CO2 water is the least certain number.
  • No new data can be taken; a gas record (flow, water) does not exist for the SPS week (GAS_FLUSH_TIMELINE).
