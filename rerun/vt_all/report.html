What this does not rule out

  • The bench line is an interpolator: a knee inside a row (det6 6-26) shows as χ²/dof > 1, which inflates the error but is not modelled. Rows the gate flags with a large χ²/dof need the piecewise model of rerun/vt_within_rows.
  • Rows below 2 × 150 kw-window tracks (most 20-min HV-scan rows) have no time measurement; they inherit the night curve of their chamber if one exists.
  • SPS: the column duration is gain- and attachment-confounded (the beam gas carries O₂); the window ratio cancels the gap but not a change of attachment. Rows whose column does not end at an edge give no v.
  • n_TOF: the per-track time is the acquisition file's, interpolated; k is measured on arms A and C only (the is2_v2 stage-3 tables carry no B/D tracks), and k mixes v with any change of the source geometry.
