# Does K1 explain the unexplained angle remainder? (2026-11-01)

Simulation only. The pass and the reconstruction model are unchanged. The saturation decision is locked
(`rerun/calib/saturation/DECISION_2026-10-29.md`), and nothing here proposes new weights or a cap for the pass.

## Verdict: no. K1 at its measured size explains ≈ 0 % of the remainder, and ≤ 24 % even at the largest sizes tried

- **The test.** The §8 study (`README.md`) left 0.56–0.65° on clean det2/det3/det7 X events (1.42° on det4) unexplained by the toy. It
  reads as an event-varying waveform-shape error. K1 of the locked model is the obvious candidate: bright pulses scatter
  about the mean model by ~6 % of the peak, plane to plane, and the noise-only weights over-trust them.
- **What was injected.** Five forms of a per-event K1 scatter were put into the full FD toy (rung L8: every measured
  realism term, pass-1 bundles, interior placement, 600 events × 3 angles per point) and fitted with the production
  `fit_plane`, unchanged. Each one's size was **calibrated to the data's residual scatter, not to the angle**: T16's
  statistic, (data − model) / model peak on the brightest strip at its peak, which is 0.063 on det3 X.
- **The only form that reaches the measured scatter is per-sample proportional noise.** It does so at 5.7 / 7.3 / 8.3 %
  on det2 / det3 / det7 X, which is the 6 % K1 reading, and there it adds **0 % (−7 … +2 %) of U_cond²**.
- **The forms that do move the angle cannot reach the measured scatter** at physical sizes: per-strip gain jitter up to
  10 %, per-strip time jitter up to 20 ns. At those sizes they explain 9–14 % (gain) and 18–24 % (time) of U_cond² on
  det2/det3/det7.
  - Explaining all of the remainder would take **21–34 % per-strip, per-event gain jitter** or **41–47 ns per-strip
    time jitter**. Neither is physical.
  - The time form also grows with tan θ (b ≈ 1.4 at 20 ns), while the data remainder is present head-on.
- **The failing signatures are the angle size and the χ² level.**
  - At the calibrated size the toy's χ²/dof at the data's median charge is 20–33. The data reach 61 (det3 X) and
    118 (det2 X).
  - So the data misfit carries 3–5× more χ² per unit of centre-strip residual than any K1 form. Whatever the error is, it
    is **coherent across a plane's samples and strips**, not an amplitude-proportional scatter of the K1 kind.
- **Two of the §8 signatures turn out not to discriminate.**
  - **χ²/dof ∝ Q^1.4–2.0** is not unique to an unmodelled error. The full toy without K1 already gives a slope of 1.1–1.4
    on det2/det3/det4 X, against 1.4–1.9 in the data.
  - **The fit-visible fraction** is 13–30 % in the toy with or without K1, against 22–24 % in the data.
  - The §8 conclusion "an event-varying shape error, partly fit-visible" stands, but its Q-scaling evidence is weaker than
    stated.
- **The K1-aware weighting leaves nothing on the table** (toy only). Fitting with `MODEL_FRAC = 0.06`, the
  signal-proportional variance term the pass does not use, makes det2/det3/det6 X **worse by 0.04–0.06°** and det7 X
  by 0.29°. That holds with and without K1 injected.
  - Only det4 X improves (−0.06 … −0.11°), where the toy carries the 31 % strip-gain map that GA withdrew from the
    budget.
  - For the paper: the locked model leaves ≤ 0° on det2/det3 to a K1-aware weighting. This agrees with the data-side
    finding that a signal-proportional variance hurt by 0.10–0.33° (`ERROR_MODEL.md`).

## Method

- **Rungs.** `ladder.py` gains rungs K1G / K1T / K1W / K1S / K1L. Each is the full toy (L8 flags) plus one per-event
  injection, drawn fresh for every event, sized by `K1_AMP`:
  - G: per-strip gain, ×(1 + a·N);
  - T: per-strip time shift, a ns;
  - W: one pulse-width stretch per event, (1 + a·N);
  - S: per-sample noise a·W·N;
  - L: footprint σ0 per event, ×(1 + a·N).
- **K1-aware arm.** A trailing `w` (and rung L8w) fits with `MODEL_FRAC = K1_WT` (0.06).
- **New per-event columns:** q_sum and T16's centre-strip residual. The residual is computed with `wm.prep_plane` +
  `wm.model_waveforms` on the fit result.
- **Not changed:** existing rungs and the X/Y file names.
- **Campaign.** `condor/make_k1_jobs.py` built 660 jobs: 5 chambers × 2 views × 3 angles × (L8, L8w, each variant at three sizes,
  each variant's K1-aware arm at its middle size), 600 events each.
  - Code `741b3fce` (rebased as `553e4065`) plus the pass-1 bundles.
  - The DAG exited 0.
  - Raw rows: `/eos/experiment/ntof/data/x17/cosmic_bench/x17rerun/k1_floor/raw/`.
- **Analysis.**
  - `k1_analyze.py` gives the per-point signatures: weighted s68, the angle form a/b, the residual scatter, χ²/dof
    at q50 and its slope over unsaturated events (as `chi2_scaling.py`), and the best-χ²-quarter share in angle × q-tercile cells.
  - `k1_verdict.py` calibrates each variant to the data scatter and gives the shares.

## Limitations

- **The Y view cannot be tested.** The Y toy (X physics fitted as Y) has a residual scatter of 0.064–0.096 and χ²/dof 53–153
  before any K1 is injected, already at or above the data. Its K1-aware arm diverges (scatter ~1). The verdict is X only.
- **The calibration fits differ.** T16's data statistic comes from held-out fits from the reference start with t0
  pinned. The toy's comes from the production fit with a t0 prior at the truth. Both are the brightest strip at its peak.
- **Only five K1 forms, each a single parameter.** A coherent, plane-correlated shape error (for example per-event
  structure in the footprint or the induction across neighbouring strips, consistent in time) was not built. The χ²
  level says that is where the remainder would have to be.
- **In-sample calibration rows only.** det6 and det7 have small clean data samples (231 and 177 events).
