# Paper I §8: the unexplained angle term, the xasym systematic, bench vs SPS position, the fleet figure (2026-10-31)

Pass 1 (`Analysis_pass1`, read-only). The calibration golden rows (o22_long_det2, sat_det3, g_det4, g_det6_long, g_det7_long)
and the det2/det3 headline rows. 03_angles' interior_good events, the v(t) arm, and FD's convention throughout: the global
d(tan_ref) line and the cross-validated 2D 40 mm tilt map are removed, and s68 is taken over |d| ≤ 5°. The frames
reproduce `budget_pass1.json`'s tilt_2d40 to the printed digit. Report: `report.html` (generated by `make_report.py`).

## Verdict

1. **The unexplained angle term is not the reference, not a per-event common cause, not fine position structure and
   not the X copy.**
   - **Reference:** s68 has no dependence on the M3 χ², ±10 % of U² of either sign.
   - **Common cause:** corr(dt_X, dt_Y) on the same events is |r| ≤ 0.09.
   - **Structure finer than the 40 mm tilt cells:** split-half rms ≤ 0.08° on every chamber, ≤ 0.16° on det2 Y. That is
     ≤ 3 % of U².
   - **Angle non-linearity:** ≤ 0.13°, ≤ 2 %.
   - **X copy:** Y, which has no copy, carries a remainder of the same size.
2. **Event conditions the toy does not generate are 12–37 % of U², and 65–92 % on det7.** These are saturated planes,
   the bright tail and topology (second particle, noise band, strip excess, multiplicity). Saturation is the largest
   of them.
3. **What is left is 0.56–0.65° on det2/det3/det7 X (1.42° on det4).** It sits on clean events (unsaturated,
   mid-amplitude, single, no band), and its character is measured:
   - **Present head-on and roughly flat in angle:** s68² = a² + (b tanθ)² with a = 0.55–0.71° on det2/det3, while the toy
     is flat at 0.33–0.40°.
   - **Fit-visible.** Keeping the best χ² quarter, at the same amplitude, angle and strip count, removes 10–31 % of the
     clean-event variance on det2/det3 (calibration and headline rows). The perfect-model toy loses 13–19 % of its own variance under the same
     selection. A χ²-blind error, the geometry the 10-27 study proposed, could only shrink by the toy-like part
     (Δ² ≈ 0.03 on det3 X), but the data shrink by Δ² ≈ 0.14.
   - **Model error proportional to the signal:** the data χ²/dof is 4–15× the toy's and grows as Q^1.4–2.0.
   - So the remainder behaves like an **event-varying waveform-shape error the model does not describe**, which the fit
     partly sees. That reverses FD's "not a waveform misfit" on pass 1: there, the resolution was flat across raw χ²
     quintiles on the audit tables.
   - **Not identified:** which part of the signal it is (per-event pulse or footprint shape, the resistive spread,
     correlated noise). It is bounded and characterised, not explained.
4. **xasym ± systematic: ≤ 5 µm on the bench X position, nothing on the angle.**
   - The K map moves X by 48 µm rms on det2 and 28 µm rms on det3 (map scale 0.44 for its session).
   - The re-aligned X core σ changes 460 → 455 µm (det2) and 466 → 466 µm (det3); K ± 1σ adds ≤ 2 µm.
   - The angle cost of the X copy is the toy's L3 rung, 0.06–0.19°.
   - det4/6/7 have no usable map (excluded in `xasym_maps.json`), so their systematic is unquantified, bounded by
     det2's ≤ 116 µm per cell.
5. **Bench and SPS positions agree once M3's own scattering is measured.**
   - The bench residual grows steeply with the M3 χ² of the matching coordinate, e.g. det3 Y 298 → 733 µm across χ²
     quintiles: a muon scattering between the M3 doublets moves the line off the true point.
   - Extrapolated to χ² → 0 (σ² linear in χ²), minus the pointing P (0.21–0.28 mm):

     | | bench quoted | M3 scattering | chamber, all angles | chamber, near normal (headline rows) | SPS det4 head-on |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | µm | 430–620 | 240–370 | 230–310 (det2/3/7) | det2 163/176 ± 25, det3 126/116 ± 35 | 190–210 |

   - The same kinks change the line's direction by < 0.04°, which is why the angle shows no M3-χ² dependence.

## What was done

- **`frame.py`** (lxplus, lock venv, `RES_CODE=code_res_18efcd0d`): one per-event parquet per row with:
  - the residual in both arms;
  - every fit column of the view and the other view's fit;
  - the effbudget ray;
  - the recipe-selected M3 track's χ²/NClus, from `june_tests/<run>/<subrun>/m3_tracking_root_v2`.

  Frames: `/eos/experiment/ntof/data/x17/cosmic_bench/x17rerun/paper1_floor/frames/`.
- **`analyze.py`**: the cumulative condition ladder (saturated → amplitude 10–80 % → topology → best-χ² quarter in
  amplitude × angle × strip-count cells), plus:
  - split-half covariances for fine structure (1.56 mm cells) and non-linearity (20 tan quantiles);
  - σ² against M3 χ²;
  - the angle form;
  - the X–Y correlation.

  Output: `results/floor_budget.json`.
- **`misfit_retest.py`, `toy_chi2_select.py`, `chi2_scaling.py`:** the misfit re-test with geometry control; the same
  χ² selection inside the X and Y toys (pure selection); and χ²/dof against amplitude.
- **The Y toy:** `ladder.py` takes `FD_VIEW=y`. It fits with the bundle's Y dead list, t0 prior and `fit_plane(..., 'y')`,
  without the one-sided X copy and without the X-numbered dead misses. It ran on condor as 45 jobs (L0/L1/L8 × 3 angles
  × 5 chambers, 1500 events each; `condor/ytoy_job.sh`), with outputs in `.../paper1_floor/ytoy_raw/`. X results are
  unchanged by this edit (`FD_VIEW` defaults to x, file names unchanged).
- **`xasym_sys.py`:** the K arms on det2/det3 X, each re-aligned (offset + rotation + scale, 3-MAD trimmed).
- **`position_ms.py`:** the position residual against M3 χ² per coordinate, with the χ² → 0 intercept (bootstrap) and the
  pointing P from `rerun/paper1_res/results/rows.csv`.
- **`hits_arm.py`:** H0 against W on the same events (the fleet figure).
  - H0 is the rr1 hits chain, `Analysis_rr1/<row>/cache/event_results_veto50.pkl`: the per-view hit-time line dt/dx and
    the earliest-hit strip. Its angle scale is calibrated on M3 per row (binned-median u = dx/dt against tan_ref, then
    inverted), and it gets the same slope and tilt removal as W.
  - Result: H0 2.0–2.6° against W 0.73–1.56°. H0's implied v is 46 against 36 µm/ns on det3, the compression in
    `RECONSTRUCTION_BASIS.md`. Position: H0 550–805 µm, W 430–620 µm. Efficiency within 5 mm is within 1–5 points
    (detection is hits-defined).

## Limitations

- **The remainder is characterised, not identified.** No candidate on hand explains it; a per-event pulse or footprint
  shape model would be the next test, and no new data can be taken.
- **The Y toy generates X physics** (X footprint tails, pulse) and fits it with the Y bundle. Its production-fit rung
  reads χ²/dof up to ~1700 at 3°, a generation/fit mismatch rather than Y physics. So the Y toy_all is an upper
  estimate, and the Y U and remainder are lower estimates. With the exact-model floor instead (0.32–0.34°), Y's
  U would be larger: det3 Y 0.64° instead of 0.54°.
- **The ladder's selections are cumulative and correlated,** so the shares depend on the order (conditions first).
- **The bench chamber position rests on P** (±10 % moves the near-normal value by ~±50 µm) and on σ² linear in χ².
- **det6 (92 % saturated) and det7 have small clean samples.**
- **All numbers are in-sample** on the calibration rows, except the det2/det3 headline rows, which agree.
