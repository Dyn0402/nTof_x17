SPS follow-ups on pass 1 (handoff step 4)

Verdict

  • (i) run71 RAW resolution vs the uRWELL telescope: det4 X (wft p0) = 193 ± 36 µm at 700 V (residual 359 ± 19 µm, telescope pointing 303 µm from run 53), against 579 µm for det4's own charge centroid on the same events. det4 Y is not measurable: it is dead over the telescope's footprint. 450 V and 275 V have 40–70 telescope events each: not measurable.
  • (ii) the 34 rows without v: 8 rows get a v from the column duration (run60 ov07–ov14 except ov10, plus ov01); rescaled with it their inclined angle matches the CF4 operating rows to 2–3 %. The other rows stay positions-only: CO2 rows and the early flush have no column end edge (the charge fades), the low-field rows overrun the window.
  • (iii) run60 ov07: the beam was off for the first 27 of its 30 minutes. The pass took the plateau's first 3000 triggers, so 1271 of them are beam-off (x quality 26%); the 1729 beam-on events read x quality 94%, like the other plateaus.

What this does not rule out

  • The det4 X number rests on the run-53 telescope model (back plane 0.301 × pitch). Here the uRWELL clusters come from RAW in-time strip maxima, not run 53's ZS hits; a different back-plane resolution moves det4 X between ~150 and ~250 µm. The residual itself (359 µm) is an upper bound with no model.
  • v from the column duration carries ws_spsv's unexplained −8 % tan scale, as every CF4 row does.
