## (i) det4 position resolution, RAW run71, against the telescope

**How telescope tracks are matched to det4 events.** The uRWELL front and back planes are both on FEU 1, channels 0–255
front and 256–511 back (`sps_beam_test_26/analysis/urw_mapping/mapping_urwell.csv`). det4 is on FEU 3. Both FEUs see the
same trigger, so the DREAM `eventId` matches. The pass keys an event as `eventId + 1e7 × (file index)`
(`sps_build_cache._fill`), so the job takes the key modulo 1e7. FEU files rotate by size, so the FEU 1 and FEU 3 file
boundaries differ by about one event; the job matches by eventId over the same file group. banco's
`decoded_root`/`combined_hits` for run_71 predate the RAW decoder fix and merge events, so all 36 FEU 1 files were decoded
again with the pinned decoder fa80c06 inside condor jobs.

**The telescope sees very little of run71.** The uRWELL pulse is positive and peaks at sample 12 of the RAW window, at
~230 ADC on the lead strip against 4 ADC of noise. Only about 5 % of triggers light a given plane-view. Front and back are
both lit in the same coordinate in 0.4–0.5 % of triggers, and all four plane-views in 0.08 %. Over the whole run that is
614,964 triggers, 2675 x pairs, 2987 y pairs and 466 four-fold tracks. The pass's first-3000-per-plateau rows overlap this
in only a few tens of events. So the selection is telescope-first:
- `urwell_scan.py` scans every FEU 1 event;
- `det4_reco_events.py` reconstructs, exactly as the pass did (code a72b889, the same bundle, H4 layer, RAW censoring and
  v/K per plateau), the 1380 events that have a front and back pair and pass det4's own peak ≥ 300 ADC in both views;
- quality_ok uses the pass row's own χ²/dof medians.

**det4 Y is dead over the telescope.** At H4 the telescope's x axis runs along det4's y and its y axis along det4's x.
The telescope y coordinate correlates with det4 X (r = −0.54 with ghosts included). The telescope x coordinate correlates
with neither det4 view (|r| ≤ 0.13). In the pass-1 raw700 table, **44 % of the Y fits sit in one 5 mm bin at 155–160 mm**,
the edge of det4's dead Y band (144–160 mm). That bin is an artefact, not a position. Only telescope-y against det4-X is
measured. The alignment is therefore one-dimensional (track = a · det4 X + b), because a 2-D affine would add the dead
view's artefact positions as noise.

**Numbers** (raw700, z = 1140 mm; the z-scan minimum is at 1120 mm):

| | n | core σ_res | telescope pointing | σ_det4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| det4 X, wft p0 | 415 (259 in core) | 359 ± 19 µm | 303 µm (zone mix) | **193 ± 36 µm** |
| same, back-plane 1.0 mm zone only | 177 | 315 ± 21 µm | 254 µm | 184 µm |
| det4 X, own charge centroid (`ref_x`) | same events | | | 579 µm |

The method is the one of `sps_beam_test_26/analysis/spatial_resolution/resolution.py` (run 53):
- the track at det4 is interpolated from front to back;
- the residual core comes from a fixed-window Gaussian fit;
- σ_det4² = σ_res² − (z/1370)² σ_back² − (1 − z/1370)² σ_front², with the back plane at 0.301 × its pitch (measured on
  run 53) and the front plane taken as binary.

193 µm is in line with run 53's 176 µm (ZS, flat Ar/CO2/iso). The waveform fit beats det4's own centroid by a factor of 3
on the same events.

**Limitations.**
- The det4 number is pointing-dominated (303 of 359 µm). It rests on run 53's telescope model. Here the uRWELL clusters
  are 5-strip centroids of RAW in-time strip maxima, not run 53's ZS hits. A back plane at binary resolution (0.289)
  instead of 0.301 gives 210 µm; one 15 % worse gives ~90 µm. **The residual, 359 ± 19 µm, is a model-free upper bound.**
- n = 259 in the core: the zone-by-zone decomposition that made run 53 self-calibrating cannot be done here.
- 450 V and 275 V cannot be measured (40–70 events each). det4 Y cannot be measured at all at H4 in run71, because that
  view is dead there. No more data can be taken.

## (ii) v for the rows without one (review P3)

**What those rows ran with.** When the registry has no v, `sps_reco` keeps the bundle v, and that is the **bench** det4
v of 33.0 µm/ns. Since tan = w / v, their angles read 2.4–3.3× too small. That is the review's −6…−10° against −20…−25°.
The time-domain fit itself does not depend on v, so a v can be applied afterwards: **tan_true = tan_pass × 33.0 / v**.
Only the depth grid K depended on it.

**Route: column duration** (ws_spsv, `rerun/model_audit/ws_spsv/REPORT.md`). The strip sum of a view is a box of length
T = gap / v, convolved with the electronics template, whatever the angle. `vbox.py` builds each row's events with the
pass's cache builder (1000 per row; ov07 beam-on only) and runs ws_spsv's `zsstack.py` (stacked box, with and without a
charge gradient). It ran as condor, one job per row.

**Controls reproduce ws_spsv:**

| control | this work (X / Y) | ws_spsv |
|---|---|---|
| run62 d700 | 2140 / 2040 ns | 2130 / 2040 |
| run63 flat | 1980 / 2070 ns | 2060 / 2050 |
| run71 raw700, X | 2010 ns | 1970 |

**Rule** (`v_table.py`). T is defined when, in both views, the box with gradient and the flat box agree to 8 %, the
gradient is not railed at −1, and T_box ≤ 2900 ns. Then v = 28.8 mm / mean(T_box), and the half-difference between the
views is its systematic. The per-row verdicts are in `results/v_no_v_rows.csv`.

| rows | verdict | why |
|---|---|---|
| run60 ov07, ov08, ov09, ov11–ov14 | **v = 12.2–13.6 µm/ns** (±0.3–0.5) | The column ends at an edge. The flush trend is monotonic, and ov14 matches the CF4 control at the same mount and field to 2 %. |
| run60 ov01 | v = 9.97 (passes the rule on its own) | Its neighbours ov00/ov02/ov03 fail. Use it with care. |
| run60 ov00, ov02–ov06, ov10; run57 ×4; run59 | fade: positions only | The box and flat box disagree by 9–25 % and the gradient is −0.6…−0.9. On run57 d550–d650 T_box reaches the window end. ov10 just misses the rule (8.05 %). |
| run55 ×4, run56 ×2 (flat CO2) | fade: positions only | The gradient is railed at −1.0 and box and flat disagree by 40–60 %. These are flat rows, so their angle is ~0 whatever v is. |
| run61 d070–d280, run70 d100/d200 (32 samples), run62 d200, run71 raw275, run55 d400 | overrun: positions only | Field < 170 V/cm or a 1.92 µs window: the column does not fit. run62 d200's 3280 ns is the window edge. |

**Validation by the known angle** (`tan_check.py`). With v_box, the inclined-view tan of ov07–ov14 becomes 0.442–0.462.
The CF4 rows at the same mount and 243 V/cm read 0.451 (run61 op25), 0.451 (run63 d675) and 0.465 (run62 d700). The two
groups agree to 2–3 %, all 4–8 % below tan 25.64° = 0.480 (ws_spsv's open −8 %).

**Recommendation.** Give run60 ov07–ov09 and ov11–ov14 (and ov01 if wanted) their v, either by rescaling tan
(× 33.0 / v) or by putting v_box into the registry and re-running those SPS nodes. Keep the other 26 rows for positions
and efficiency only. There is no new data to settle the CO2 rows.

## (iii) run60 ov07

`ov07_check.py` uses the trigger rate per minute from the FEU 3 timestamps:
- ov05 and ov08 hold 68–77 k triggers per minute throughout (5 files each);
- ov06 falls to ~10 per minute from minute 16 on;
- ov07 has 40–70 per minute until minute 28, then 42 k and 76 k. It is a single file of 119,731 triggers against 1–2 M
  for its neighbours.

The pass took each plateau's first 3000 events after the 20 s edge. For ov07 that is 1271 beam-off triggers (cosmic and
noise; x quality 26 %, median 26 X strips, q_sum 39 k) plus 1729 beam-on events (x quality 94 %, 6 strips, q_sum 4.0 k).
**No other SPS row is affected**: the 3000 events of every other row span ≤ 120 s with no gap over 26 s, against 1716 s
for ov07.

**To use ov07:** cut at t_wall ≥ 91170 s (1729 events), or rebuild it with a beam-on gate. The pass's cache builder has
no beam-on condition; a rate gate per spill would protect any future rebuild.

## Open risks found on the way (outside step 4)

- **The run71 Y view.** 44 % of the pass-1 raw700 Y fits pile up at 155–160 mm (the dead-band edge). The stacked Y strip
  sum gives T = 3040 ns against X 2010 ns, while ws_spsv's pre-CM caches gave 1980. The run71 head-on **Y** angle s68
  (1.4–3.8°, review P2) includes these artefact fits. X is clean.
- **The CF4 ladder rows below 243 V/cm, which do carry v.** They use the span-route v, which ws_spsv retired. Their
  inclined tan falls with field: run63 d325 reads −0.244, d475 −0.364 and d625 −0.439, against −0.451 at 243 V/cm (the
  same in run62). So the angle scale of every ladder row below ~220 V/cm is off by up to 2×. They are not angle-calibrated
  either.
