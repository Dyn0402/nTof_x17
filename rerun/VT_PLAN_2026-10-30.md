# v(t) in every data set: plan and first test (2026-10-30)

Dylan, 10-30: "we also generally need to be looking at v as a function of t in all data sets after doing initial
calibrations." This is the plan for that step and the test of it on pass 1. Code, tables and report:
`rerun/vt_all/` (`report.html`, `results/`). Builds on `rerun/vt_within_rows/` (det6/det7 6-26, 46a27ac9).

## 1 · Where it sits in the chain

```
S1 calibration (golden rows) → G1 → vrefit (per-row v / kw) → pass → post (01–04) → **VT** → anything quoted
```

- **After vrefit and the pass.** A row's v (and its kw) is a row average. v(t) is measured *relative to that average*,
  on the row's own reconstruction, so it can only come after the row exists.
- **Before anything is quoted.** An angle, a depth or a resolution is quoted only from a row whose VT verdict is
  `flat`, or after its correction is applied.
- **Table-level, no refit.** The fit measures w, which does not depend on v (GC). v enters only the angle mapping
  `tan = (w − w0) / (kw · v · r(t) · S) − a` and the depth `z = v · r(t) · u`. So the correction is applied to the
  tables afterwards. The reconstruction does not need to know about it (`wft.reco.v_rel_t` exists but is not used).
- **One VT pass per production.** When a chamber is re-calibrated (S4 / the det4 and det7 decisions), its VT step
  re-runs on the new tables. It costs minutes.

## 2 · Estimator per data type

| data | estimator | why | precision per block | time resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **bench, with M3** | **M3 ladder** (`set_w0kw`'s kw per time block, after the bow): v_rel(t) = kw(t)/kw(row) | the same estimator that gives every row its kw; gain-independent (vt_within_rows: v flat to 1 % while the charge moves ×55) | 1–4 % per view (300 kw-window tracks) | 300 kw-window tracks: **13–40 min** on long rows |
| **SPS, no M3** | **column duration** per wall-clock window (ws_spsv's stacked box T): v_rel = T(row)/T(t) | no reference; the strip sum is angle-free and map-free | **1.4 %** per 1000-event window (run63 flat700 control) | 1000 events: 3–10 min |
| **n_TOF, no reference** | **in-situ angle scale k** (k_arm's `track` estimator on the pointing-coincident tracks, from the stage-3 table): v_rel = k(run)/k(t) | the capsule geometry fixes tan = (u − foot)/234.6 mm; k is v_assumed/v_true; gain-independent | **0.7 %** per sub-run and arm (A, C) | **one sub-run (~1 h)**; half sub-runs at 1.4 % |

Not used as the gate estimator:
- **The bench column duration**, because it is gain- and attachment-confounded (GC: the det6 ladder moved 1.68× faster
  than 1/T).
- **The n_TOF column end** (`x_q_uend`), because it rails at the depth-grid edge on ~40 % of tracks (1080 ns) and
  carries the beam's O₂ attachment.

Both are kept as diagnostics.

## 3 · The gate (when a row needs a correction)

**Bench** (`vt_bench.gate`, one definition used by the condor node and the report):
- **`correct`:** a joint line (one slope, an intercept per view) over the row's blocks gives |Δv| ≥ **2 %** start to
  end, at ≥ **3σ** (error inflated by √(χ²/dof) where above 1). The row has ≥ 3 blocks, and the two views' own lines
  agree within 3σ.
  - Why 2 %: a ±1 % angle scale about the row mean is ±0.25° at 30°. That is the size of a row's own kw error and of
    the resolutions being quoted.
- **`candidate (2 blocks)`:** passes on two blocks only. A line through two points has no check of its own, so the
  row needs its chamber's night curve or its neighbours before it is corrected.
- **`unstable`:** the line's χ²/dof > 3 (dof ≥ 5). v jumps from block to block rather than drifting. This calls for a
  per-row decision (per-block v, or exclusion), not a line.
- **`views disagree`:** passes, but x and y disagree. This is a ladder problem, not v.
- **`flat`** and **`significant, < 2 %`:** no correction.
- **`too few tracks`:** fewer than 2 × 150 kw-window tracks. No time measurement. The row takes its chamber's night
  curve where one exists (interpolated from the neighbouring rows' blocks on the clock), otherwise it stays as is and
  the limitation is stated.

**SPS:** first-to-last window change ≥ max(2 %, 3·√2·σ_win) in both views, with the same sign. σ_win is measured on
the control (run63 flat700, stable gas). Only rows whose column ends at an edge (vbox's rule) are measured.

**n_TOF:** k is measured per sub-run anyway. The gate is whether one k per run is enough, i.e. whether the sub-run
scatter exceeds its errors (χ² of a constant, p < 10⁻³) or the line moves > 2 % at 3σ over the run. Where it fails,
use **k per sub-run** in place of k per run. This is a lookup change in `k_arm`; nothing is refitted.

## 4 · Storage

- **Per corrected row:** `vt_map.json` = the `v_map['t']` block of `wft.reco.v_rel_t`:
  - `model` (`line`, or `night` for vt_within_rows' chamber curve);
  - `clock` (stated in the file);
  - `t_h`, `v_rel` (piecewise-linear, clamped);
  - `v_drift`, `source`, `pass1_key`.
- **Clocks:**
  - Bench vt_all uses **hours since 2026-06-01 00:00 CERN local** (acq_tag stem + DREAM timestamp × 10 ns).
  - vt_within_rows used hours since 2026-06-26. Each file states its own.
  - SPS uses the wall clock of `sps_build_cache` (s since the day's 00:00).
  - n_TOF uses the acquisition file's interpolated time (stage 3 carries no per-event timestamp).
- **Test location:** `/eos/user/d/dneff/x17rerun/vt_all/{bench,sps,ntof}/`.
- **In production:** next to each row's tables, `Analysis_passN/<run>/<subrun>/<det>/wft/vt/vt_map.json`, plus
  `events_vt.parquet` (event_id, t_h, v_rel and the corrected tan/θ/mid/z, as vt_within_rows writes them). Never over
  the pass tables.
- **Docs:** the gate constants and clocks are in `docs/PARAMETERS.md` (§3.1, `v_map`).

## 7 · What this does not cover / limitations

- **The bench line is an interpolator.** det6's knee shows as χ²/dof 1.4. The piecewise or night model of
  vt_within_rows is the better shape where the night curve is known.
- **What changes the gas is not identified** on any night (no gas log). Water drying out is the plausible common
  cause on the bench. det4 6-24's fall is the only decreasing case.
- **SPS:** the window ratio cancels the gap but not a change of attachment (the beam gas carries O₂ that falls
  overnight, run63). A column shortened by attachment would read as faster v. Below 5 % nothing can be separated.
- **n_TOF:**
  - k mixes v with anything that changes the pointing geometry (capsule position, an arm moving). A k step that is
    common to A and C at one time would point there.
  - The per-track time is the acquisition file's, interpolated between sub-run starts.
