Generated by make_gaps.py. The gap is measured as g = v_ang × T, the method of Paper I figure two_velocities (c): T is the duration of the drift column in the head-on stack, and v_ang is the angle-scale velocity of the same row (M3 ladder). Neither uses the design value. The geometric gap (charge extent of inclined tracks) is an independent second method. Rows taken in the first hours after a chamber was installed (C before 06:00 on 6-26, D's first row, E's first row on 6-23) are left out of the mean, because attachment shortens the visible column there. Rows whose column runs past the end of the recorded stack (chamber A at 500 V) are left out too, since their T is only a lower limit.
