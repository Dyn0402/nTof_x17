The drift gap of chamber A across its surface (pass 1)

Paper I Sec. 5.1. Code: rerun/paper1_gap/. Inputs: the pass-1 tables and the rr1 waveforms of the two det3 golden rows (Run 1 = sat_det3, 6-27; Run 2 = the 6-28 p2 overnight), 490 V mesh, 1000 V drift, top slot.

Verdict. The gap of chamber A is not uniform. Three independent measurements (geometric with the X strips, geometric with the Y strips, and angle-scale velocity × drift time) show the same pattern in both runs. The gap rises along x by 0.5–0.8 mm per 100 mm (a cathode tilt of about 0.3–0.5° relative to the mesh). Along y it is largest near the centre and falls toward both ends, with the shape of the measured dish of the readout board under a flat cathode (0.5–0.8 of the dish). Over the interior the gap runs from about 27 to about 29 mm around the mean of 28.2 mm, with a cell scatter of 0.5–0.7 mm. The timing maps carry the signature of a gap change, not a gas change (d ln v / d ln T = -0.50 ± 0.02; gap -0.40, gas −1).

Methods, per cell (track position at mid-gap, ≥ 30 mm inside the active area)

  • G, geometric, X strips (no time): the half-height edges of the X charge of inclined tracks (|tan θ| 0.2–0.8) against z = (x − x_mesh)/tan θ_ref; mask = pass dead list ∪ audit flags; mean of the unsaturated and saturation-masked selections (the S4 recipe); the +tan and −tan halves averaged per cell, which removes a local drift tilt to first order. Bootstrap errors (40).
  • GY, geometric, Y strips: the same on the Y view with the peak sample (the Y integral drains through the RC spreading). The peak version reads high by a blur-dependent amount, so GY is used relative to its own chamber value.
  • T, column duration: colsum.py's per-trigger box fit of the strip sum of the track's cluster (valid for any angle), matched to the M3 table; unsaturated single clusters; amplitude walk removed globally (it moves the map by ≤ 0.06 mm); 20 % trimmed mean.
  • V, angle-scale velocity: per cell and view, a robust line w = a + v tan θ_ref (|tan θ_ref| ≤ 0.6); no gap enters.
  • VT: g = v × T per cell, the paper's second gap measurement made locally. The field dependence cancels: T ∝ g1+β, v ∝ g−β.
  • bow: a flat cathode above the measured dish of the board, −(curvature part of the pass-1 bow surface). The linear part of the bow surface is the rigid tilt of the whole chamber, which the cathode shares, so it is left out.

Coverage. Inclined tracks reach only the centre of the chamber through the M3 telescope: the X-strip geometric method covers x ≈ 100–320 mm (all y), the Y-strip one y ≈ 60–290 mm (all x). The timing methods use every angle and cover the whole interior.

Checks

  • Staging: the Run 1 inclined-track cache rebuilt here is identical, event by event and sample by sample, to S4's pass-1 cache.
  • Gain: removing the amplitude walk of T changes the T map by ≤ 0.06 mm.
  • Run 1 and Run 2 were taken in the same mounting, hours apart; the pattern is not a time effect within a run.

What this does not rule out

  • The timing product reproduces the pattern with about 0.7 of the geometric amplitude (0.72 ± 0.13), and the v-only map varies more than a pure gap change predicts (k = -0.50 against -0.40). So the amplitude of the variation is known to about 30 %, and part of the velocity map may come from something other than the gap (β itself is uncertain: 0.68 from Magboltz, 0.87 from the drift-time scan alone).
  • Structure beyond tilt + dish remains (dish + tilt χ² above ndf on the timing maps); its origin is not identified.
  • The geometric methods do not see the outer x columns (X strips) or the high-y row (Y strips); there the map rests on timing.
  • The mean gap (28.2 mm, 1.8 mm short of the 30 mm design) is not explained by this map; we have no measurement of the cathode position.
  • Only one mounting of chamber A at the operating point is usable; the earlier bottom-slot runs are excluded (pathological drift).
