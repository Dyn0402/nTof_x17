# Bright samples, saturation and the footprint: the simple model is locked (Dylan, 2026-10-29)

**Decision.** The from-scratch pass runs the reconstruction as it stands on main: noise-only χ² weights, clipped
samples censored as lower bounds at the 3550 rule, one static Gaussian footprint (σ0 = 0.30 mm ⊕ Dp·√u) per view,
one X-derived template for both views, the physical RC kernel. **No cap on bright samples, no per-event width, no
extra footprint/shape parameter, no variance model, no per-strip nuisance, no per-sample rails.** Dylan's reasons:
the capping strategies and a width per event are not physically convincing, and the measured cost of leaving them
out is small. Everything below is a *known simplification* of that model, carried into the pass and into the papers
as a stated limitation. The study switches stay in `wft/model.py` (all off = bit-identical) for a possible second
pass; none of them is part of the algorithm.

This closes the saturation and error-model studies (`SATURATION_STUDY.md`, `ERROR_MODEL.md`), the footprint-shape
part of the template study (`../templates/TEMPLATE_STUDY.md` T9/T13/T15/T16), and the model-audit items F17/F18/F21
on weights and the per-event footprint. It supersedes the "Next" lists of those files.

## What leaving them out costs (bench, held-out, event-paired against the current model)

Δs68 of the best candidate (cap 1200 + plain charge re-solve) relative to the model as locked. Negative = the
candidate was better. The candidate was then refused by the full-reconstruction gate (below), so these are the
upper bound of what is given up.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| det3 (4818 events, second sample) | −0.052 ± 0.010° | −0.037 ± 0.010° | −0.3 pp | ERROR_MODEL.md held-out table |
| det3 500 V / 700 V / 6-25 / 6-28 | −0.03…−0.06° | −0.02…−0.05° | ≤ ±1 pp | same |
| det2 | −0.05° | −0.06° | ~0 | same |
| det4 | −0.06° | +0.01° | ~0 | same |
| det6 | 0.00° | −0.04° | ~0 | same |
| det7 | −0.12° (−0.08 of it is the rail, not the cap) | −0.01° | +2 pp | same, SATURATION_STUDY cap table |

Against det3's 1.0–1.1° that is about 5 % of the resolution, or ~7 % of the excess over the exact-model toy floor
(0.30–0.35°, `rerun/model_audit/ws_floor`). The static shape terms (narrow-core fraction, Dp scale, Voigt-like
tails, template stretch) buy no angle on det3 at all (T15/T16), only χ² (−6…−11 %) and charge. The per-event
footprint choice (`sigma_p0_prof`) was already retired on 2026-10-27 (reco.py, FE item 12 / F18): it chose the wide
footprint on 5–36 % of fits and those read 6–12 % shallow.

The locked model keeps what the candidates lost: the charge (x-y log-charge σ 0.054–0.060 on det3, pairing power
0.90), the interior detection efficiency (the cap cost det6 −0.7…−0.8 pt and det7 −1.4…−1.6 pt in the full
reconstruction, runaway steep fits on clipped near-vertical columns), and the two-track splits.

## Known simplifications and problems carried into the pass (all measured; nothing here is new)

| # | simplification | what it does to the results | where measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | **Noise-only weights.** Every sample is weighted by the electronics noise (~10 ADC) although a bright pulse scatters around any mean model by ~6 % of its peak (50–200 ADC), plane to plane. | The brightest samples are over-trusted 10–20×. s68 rises with amplitude (det3 Y 0.84 → 1.18° lowest to highest charge quartile); the ~0.05° the cap recovered is this. χ²/dof 15 (800–1500 ADC) → 60 (2500–3550) on unsaturated det3 planes, so **absolute χ² is not a usable cut**; the quality flag is relative (5 × the table median per view). | TEMPLATE_STUDY T9/T16, ERROR_MODEL.md |

| K8 | **Template carries the strip's drift segment** (4–7 % too wide in time, T1); Y uses the X template through the RC kernel and wants more late spread than it gets (T14: own Y template worse). det4 Y would gain 0.12° from a 0.84 time stretch; not adopted (does not win on det3). | χ² and the temporal part of K2; angle-neutral on det3. | TEMPLATE_STUDY T1, T14, T15 |

What is **not** a simplification: the LM fitter, pinned bench t0, the bow block, the dead/hot masks, the two-track fit,
the χ² flag, w0/kw per row. Those are the algorithm (`rerun/STOCKTAKE_2026-10-29.md` §1).

## What would reopen this

Nothing before the pass. A second pass would be justified only by a mean model that is right at the bright samples
for a physical reason (K2 with a mechanism, not a fitted shape); the toy says the correct Poisson variance then
gains another 7–10 %. The gates for any such change are fixed: held-out on det3 500/700 V + 6-25 + 6-28
(`make_satfin_dag.py`) **and** the full-reconstruction gate (`make_gate_dag.py`, `gate_compare.py`,
`satgate_pairs.py`), efficiency and steep-runaway count first.
