## The simulation

`MX17_Full_Geant` branch `b4c-slab` (`--slab B4C:2.5 --slab-radius R`, `--bias-slab`, cascade
entries in `--gamma-lines`). Built in `/afs/cern.ch/work/d/dneff/git/MX17_Full_Geant_b4c`.
Outputs `/eos/experiment/ntof/data/x17/full_sim/b4c_july/` (185 GB, mostly the IPC step trees),
reduced parts `/eos/user/d/dneff/scratch/b4c_sim/parts*` → `/media/dylan/data/x17/b4c_target/sim_parts`.

| set | what | events |
|---|---|---|
| `neutrons_r{50,25}` | analog EAR2 neutrons 2.2 meV–2 eV, photon evaporation on | 2×10⁷ each |
| `casc_r{50,25}` | EGAF ¹⁰B(n,γ) cascades from the slab capture vertices | 10⁷ each |
| `ipc_r{50,25}` | Born IPC, 7 lines (E1: 7.007, 4.711, 2.533, 11.448; M1: 4.444, 8.917, 6.740*) | 10⁶ per line |

\*6.740 is E2; the generator has no E2, so M1 kinematics with the E2 coefficient.

Why the cascade is generated by hand: Geant4 11.2 ParticleHP samples the ¹⁰B capture lines
independently (cascades summing to 20–50 MeV against Q = 11.45), and
`/process/had/particle_hp/use_photo_evaporation true` conserves energy but uses a statistical
branching (35 % via a 2.12 MeV level that the data do not show). Geant4 also emits the 478 keV
⁷Li line in ~51 % of (n,α) instead of 94 %.

Run: `python -m ntof_b4c_target.expect` then `python -m ntof_b4c_target.make_report`.

## Results (per nominal 7×10¹² pulse; exposure = 3107 nominal pulses)

- ¹⁰B(n,γ) captures 396/pulse → internal pairs produced 1.6/pulse.
- Trigger legs from the target physics ~2/pulse; the data recorded 35 triggers/pulse.
- Doubles: ~1.3 in the whole exposure (cascade γs); IPC pairs with two legs and both
  chambers lit: **0.03 in the whole exposure** (1.7 even at a 0.1 MIP plastic threshold).
  The leptons (≤ 11.45 MeV transitions) do not reach the plastics behind the LS vessels.
- Disc diameter (Ø100 vs Ø50) barely matters: the beam ends at r ≈ 22 mm.

## Open

- Simulated chamber occupancy (~1900 lit per arm per pulse, 478 keV Compton electrons)
  implies ≥ 2 chambers active in nearly every readout; the data show 2.5 %, as in production.
  Criteria not matched — unresolved. Could also mean the disc was small or off the beam
  (the May test found the B4C off axis).
- Target diameter, density and exact thickness were never recorded.
