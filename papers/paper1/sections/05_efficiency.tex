\subsection{What ``detected'' means}\label{sec:detected}
\tbw{Denominator: one clean M3 ray ($\chi^2<1$, four clusters) whose footprint lies inside the measured active area
(the whole chamber: the edge cut changes the efficiency by ${\le}0.9$\,pt, so the headline numbers use the full area
and \cref{sec:edge} studies the edges). Detected: both views fitted, ${\le}2$ competing strips per view, and the
reconstructed track within $R$ of the M3 ray at the mesh. When the chamber reconstructs several tracks, the event is
detected if one of them is the M3 track (multi-track credit; this is the default number). Nothing is removed from the denominator: tracks far from M3 with the right angle (0.20\,\%) are only half
explained by muons scattering between the M3 doublets, so they count as losses with everything else we cannot pin on
the reference.}
\tbw{\textbf{The radius.} $\varepsilon(R)$ has two regimes (\cref{fig:effradius}): the resolution core (core
$\sigma=0.55$\,mm per view at the mesh, dominated by the M3 pointing and scattering), steep up to ${\sim}4$\,mm; then a
slow rise of 0.1--0.2\,pt per mm out to 40\,mm, made of mis-measured tracks ($\delta$-pulled fits, saturated
blobs, bent reference tracks) and accidentals. We use $R=5$\,mm, the knee: 9 core $\sigma$, where the Gaussian core
is exhausted and the accidental rate (event mixing) is 0.07\,\%, below the statistical error and 70$\times$ below
the inefficiency. $R\in[4,8]$\,mm moves the efficiency by $-0.5/{+0.6}$\,pt (systematic).}

\subsection{det3: single-track efficiency}\label{sec:single}
\tbw{\textbf{The selection.} A ``single-track'' sample selected by det3 itself is biased: a failure that looks like two
tracks would leave the sample by its own failure. An external tag does not help on the bench: M3 sees a second straight
track in 2 of the 276 events where det3 sees two particles, because those particles are local (soft electrons and
secondaries at large angles). We use the two views instead: a real second particle is ionisation in the gas, seen in both
views with the same charge (the X/Y charge ratio of one particle is known to 6\,\%). An event leaves the single-track
sample only if it carries such a second particle, in one of three forms: (i) a separate, charge-matched segment in each
view, not attached to the muon; (ii) two segments in one view and, in the other, a single segment that is the two merged
in projection: wider than a single track (median 15--25 strips against 12), carrying the charge of both, or placed
between them within the 12\,mm seed gap; (iii) a clean single-track muon in both views plus a separate deposit, which
together trip the discharge veto (${>}50$ strips). Everything else stays in the sample and counts against the chamber.}
\tbw{\textbf{Result.} Over the full active area (two golden rows, 28\,453 tracks):
$\varepsilon_\mathrm{single}=94.8\pm0.1$\,(stat)$^{+0.6}_{-0.5}$\,($R$)\,\%; in the interior 95.7\,\%. Interior
losses (4.3\,\%): $\delta$-rays 2.5 (below), discharges 0.5, noise bands 0.2, fits off with no flag 0.3, second deposits
not particle-like 0.2, fits right in angle but far from M3 0.2, edge of the other coordinate 0.15. The chamber hardware itself (discharges, noise bands) accounts for 0.7\,\%; the rest is the
reconstruction of a muon accompanied by its own $\delta$-ray. The extra 0.9\,pt lost over the full area is the edge
response (\cref{sec:edge}).}
\tbw{\textbf{$\delta$-rays: one population, three outcomes by size.} Median strips over threshold: a clean muon 12; a
compact saturated blob 19 (the fit is pulled off, 0.9\,\%); a $\delta$ along the track 34 (the fit is pulled beyond
5\,mm, 0.9\,\%); a large branching deposit 67 (0.7--0.9\,\%), which trips the discharge veto (${>}50$ strips) so that no
fit is attempted. None of them shows extra activity in the M3 planes above or below det3 (0.49/0.47 extra clusters per event for the largest
deposits, 0.56/0.72 for detected muons, 1.55/1.46 for events with an independent second particle): the deposit is
made in det3's own gas, cathode or window, by the muon. Their rate is consistent in order of magnitude with knock-on
electrons ${\gtrsim}0.1$\,MeV (${\sim}1$--1.5\,\% of muons). They are counted as losses: a micro-TPC that measures a
straight track has to cope with the $\delta$-rays it makes. Real discharges are different: 113 strips over threshold
and 220--230 at SNR\,$>$\,6, with chip-wide ringing.}

\subsection{det3: two particles in the chamber}\label{sec:pairing}
\tbw{\textbf{Efficiency.} 3.2\,\% of tracks (945 of 29\,298, full area) come with a second particle in det3. The muon is
reconstructed in 73.7\,\% of them, and the losses are all in the two harder configurations
(\texttt{rerun/effbudget/single/single\_track\_area.py}):
(i) two separate segments in both views (620 events): 95.5\,\%, as efficient as with one particle;
(ii) two segments in one view, merged into one in the other projection (232): 44\,\%;
(iii) a clean muon plus a separate deposit elsewhere in the plane, which together exceed the discharge veto (93): 1\,\%.
These are fair two-particle events and count as losses where the muon is not recovered.}

\tbw{\textbf{Overlap in one projection.} The reconstruction is built for it: a joint two-track fit offers a merged
candidate a second track, with a lowered threshold when the other view resolves more candidates. In its first version
it rarely fired on the bench: both its trigger and its acceptance required candidates to pass a plausibility test tuned
on beam tracks (full drift column, raw $|\tan|<0.6$) that short, steep secondaries fail, and a short secondary drifts from
only part of the gap, so its column time misses the coincidence. A charge-corroborated trigger and acceptance (the
merged view's charge must match the sum of the other view's two segments, and each child its partner; only the muon
child need be plausible; the 4-pitch separation floor kept) recovers the muon in 53.8\,\% of the 195 interior merged
events (from 41.0\,\%), with correct X/Y pairing in 36.4\,\% (from 20.5\,\%), and splits no clean single muon on any of the
five chambers (\texttt{rerun/effbudget/single/MERGED\_PROJECTION\_2026-11-02.md}). The rest fail the geometric guards:
the two children share almost no depth (one track cut in half) or lie within the floor.}
\analysis{MERGED}{charge-corroborated split on main (28ac36a0), bench switch xy\_pairing.split\_corrob = charge; needs waveforms, so the 44\,\% in \cref{fig:twoparticle} stays until the next bench re-pass}
\tbw{\textbf{The discharge veto.} Configuration (iii), the large $\delta$-rays and the edge-zone losses all come from
dropping every event with more than 50 strips over threshold. A rescue that re-seeds such events from their clean
clusters (track-sized clusters only, rail samples censored, a one-particle gate on the result) recovers muons, but they
are right far less often than ordinary tracks: 57--75\,\% on det2/det3 (ordinary 95\,\%), 11--26\,\% on det4/det6/det7, where a
discharge rings every chip within a sample of the muon's own signal. It therefore stays off, and vetoed muons count as
losses (\texttt{rerun/effbudget/single/DISCHARGE\_VETO\_2026-11-02.md}). The clean case, a muon plus a separate deposit,
would be worth rescuing alone (29 right, 4 wrong on det2/det3) if it could be told from a shower without the reference.}
\tbw{\textbf{Pairing the views.} Two particles give two segments per view and two possible X/Y pairings; the wrong
one makes two ghost tracks. The views share no coordinate, but they read the same avalanche charge at the same drift
times, so the reconstruction pairs them by the charge balance and the charge-arrival time (Paper~II). On 552 real
two-particle cosmic events where M3 marks one particle in both views, the pairing is right in
$98.0\pm0.6$\,\% (charge alone: 95.7\,\%; 100\,\% when the two charges differ by more than a factor 1.65, 96\,\% when they
are similar). This is the feasibility demonstration for pairs at n\_TOF. The production reconstruction makes the same choice (the
matching with the lowest summed pairing cost, then the coincidence gate per pair) and pairs the two-particle events
correctly on every chamber: det3 95.9\,\%, det2 96.8\,\%, det6 92.2\,\%, det7 90.1\,\%, det4 94.0\,\%
(\texttt{rerun/effbudget/single/PAIRING\_FIX\_2026-11-02.md}).}
