n\_TOF, so the one chamber not needed there, with its known non-amplifying stripes (\cref{sec:det4}), was the one
available. Beam, uRWELL references, chamber~E position, runs used (HV, drift field, gas, rotation).}
\tbw{\textbf{Role in this paper: a cross-check.} Every headline number comes from the cosmic bench (efficiency, angles,
the intrinsic position resolution with the reference subtracted, the drift velocity and the gas). The SPS data confirm
the position resolution in a beam (chamber~E's working region, 138/120\,\textmu m) and the angle scale at a known angle
(column $v$ 0.981 $\pm$ 0.03 of tan 25.4\textdegree), and measure the charge footprint at normal incidence.}

\tbw{Five chambers were tested on the bench. We call them A--E. Chambers A--D later ran together at n\_TOF and keep
these names in the companion paper; chamber E, whose readout board has non-amplifying strips (\cref{sec:det4}), went
to the SPS instead. Chamber A is the subject of this paper; B--E show what varies from one board to the next.
Each chamber's bench numbers come from its \emph{golden run}, a long run at the operating point its HV scan selected
(\cref{tab:datasets}). Chamber A has two, taken back to back on the night of 27--28 June at the same HV and in the
same slot: \textbf{Run~1}, the long run that closed its HV and drift scans, and \textbf{Run~2}, the overnight run
that followed. They are summed unless a figure shows them separately.}
