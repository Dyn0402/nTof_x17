DREAM readout, sampling, window. Gas and flow.}

\subsection{SPS H4 beam}
\tbw{Only chamber~E went to the SPS: the test (late July -- early August 2026; exact dates to fill from the run log) ran while chambers A--D were installed at
n\_TOF, so the one chamber not needed there, with its known non-amplifying stripes (\cref{sec:det4}), was the one
available. Beam, uRWELL references, chamber~E position, runs used (HV, drift field, gas, rotation).}
\tbw{\textbf{Role in this paper: a cross-check.} Every headline number comes from the cosmic bench (efficiency, angles,
the intrinsic position resolution with the reference subtracted, the drift velocity and the gas). The SPS data confirm
the position resolution in a beam (chamber~E's working region, 138/120\,\textmu m) and the angle scale at a known angle
(column $v$ 0.981 $\pm$ 0.03 of tan 25.4\textdegree), and measure the charge footprint at normal incidence.}

\tbw{Five chambers were tested on the bench. We call them A--E. Chambers A--D later ran together at n\_TOF and keep
these names in the companion paper; chamber E, whose readout board has non-amplifying strips (\cref{sec:det4}), went
to the SPS instead. Chamber A is the subject of this paper; B--E show what varies from one board to the next.
Each chamber's bench numbers come from its \emph{golden run}, its long run at fixed HV (\cref{tab:datasets}); these
ran at 490--495\,V, 10--65\,V above the HV the scans later showed to be best (\cref{sec:operation}). Chamber A has two, taken back to back on the night of 27--28 June at the same HV and in the
same slot: \textbf{Run~1}, the long run that closed its HV and drift scans, and \textbf{Run~2}, the overnight run
that followed. We treat them as one data set (10\,h, 28\,453 tracks over the full active area): taken apart they
agree on everything this paper measures (interior efficiency $95.43\pm0.26$ / $95.15\pm0.15$\,\%, angle $s_{68}$
0.975 / 0.991\textdegree{} in X and 0.896 / 0.901\textdegree{} in Y, position core at the mesh 0.50 / 0.51\,mm in X,
uncorrelated time 22.4 / 22.2\,ns). The one difference is the drift velocity, 35.8 against 37.2\,\textmu m/ns, as
the gas dried through the night (\cref{sec:gas}); the calibration was made on Run~1 and applied to Run~2 with only
$v$ refitted. Where a figure needs a single event it is from Run~1.}
