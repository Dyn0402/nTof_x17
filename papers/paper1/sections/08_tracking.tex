\intent{What the chambers deliver with the reconstruction of the companion paper~\cite{paperII}: angle and position

\subsection{Cosmic rays}
\tbw{det3 angular resolution ($s_{68}$, interior, golden rows): 0.99\textdegree{} (X) and 0.90\textdegree{} (Y); det2
1.05/1.13\textdegree, det6 1.26/1.51\textdegree, det7 1.23/1.46\textdegree, det4 2.41/2.08\textdegree{} (\cref{fig:trackperf}).
Calibration: the physical RC kernel of~\cite{paperII}; numbers from the from-scratch pass 1. An unexplained remainder of
0.56--0.65\textdegree{} on clean events is measured and bounded but not identified.}

\subsection{Intrinsic position resolution on the bench}\label{sec:posres}
\tbw{The residual to M3 (core 430--620\,\textmu m) holds three terms: the chamber, the M3 pointing $P$ (0.21--0.28\,mm), and
scattering between the M3 doublets, which grows with the M3 $\chi^2$ of the matching coordinate. Extrapolating the
near-normal core width to $\chi^2\to0$ removes the scattering (det3 $\sigma_0=274\pm16$ / $237\pm18$\,\textmu m, X/Y), and
subtracting $P$ in quadrature leaves the chamber: det3 $126\pm34$ (X) and $116\pm36$\,\textmu m (Y), det2 $163\pm29$ and
$176\pm21$\,\textmu m (\cref{fig:posres}). The subtraction dominates the uncertainty: $\pm10$\,\% on $P$ moves det3 X to
58--164 and Y to 67--147\,\textmu m. Quoted: ${\sim}120\pm60$\,\textmu m for det3 near normal incidence.}

\subsection{A measured X neighbour asymmetry}\label{sec:xasym}
\intent{Report it as a property of the June boards, not a reconstruction artefact, with its size and what it does
and does not affect.}
\tbw{Every June resistive print shows an X-only field of unequal $\pm1$ neighbours: smooth over the board, fixed per
board, both signs, absent in Y. det3 head-on: $a_Q=(Q_{-1}-Q_{+1})/(Q_{-1}+Q_{+1})=+0.165\pm0.019$, the excess a copy of
the centre pulse $0.13\times$ its size, $\approx$110\,ns late (det2 $+0.08$, det4 $+0.08$, det7 $+0.19$, det6 $-0.02$).
Excluded: electronics and connectors (it follows the board, not the FEU), copper/bus coupling ($\times$10 too weak),
track angle and walk (measured at the time-symmetric angle), the 800\,\textmu m strip/pad beat (no 31.2\,mm period),
the bulk pillars. Leading hypothesis, unproven: resistive-strip registration against the pads; the strip mask was
never obtained and the photographed film (761\,\textmu m) does not make the size. Effect: the production X position
carries $K=-0.77\pm0.13$ times the raw centroid-bias map, $\le44$\,\textmu m (det3) and $\le116$\,\textmu m (det2) per
50\,mm cell after the board mean; resolution unchanged within 5\,\textmu m after correction and re-alignment. The
forward model of~\cite{paperII} is symmetric in X; the cost of that is quoted there.}

\subsection{Cross-check in the beam}
\tbw{det4 at SPS, in its working region R (X 150--160 $\times$ Y 222--246\,mm, run 63): $\sigma_X=138$\,\textmu m (telescope
$f$ range 112--153, stat $\pm7$) and $\sigma_Y=120$\,\textmu m (89--136, $\pm9$), model-free upper bounds 153 and
145\,\textmu m: consistent with the bench chamber term. det4 runs at reduced gain over most of its board (\cref{sec:det4}),
so this is a cross-check, not the headline.}
