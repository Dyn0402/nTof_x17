Paper II §6: what a hits-only readout achieves (tag ana1106)

Verdict. On det3, the best hits-only reconstruction (H2c, a forward fit of the emulated hit list) reaches X 1.20° / Y 1.28° against the waveform fit's 1.04° / 1.01° (naive hits 3.18° / 3.75°). The remaining gap sits in near-normal tracks. In the re-simulation the hits compression does not go away without the resistive layer: Y 1.34 at the measured RC, 1.23 with no RC, 1.19 in the metallic limit; the waveform fit reads 0.99–1.00 everywhere.

What this does not rule out

  • Arm D emulates the logic of VMM/TIGER/VFAT-like chips at DREAM shaping and 60 ns sampling, not their analogue front ends; shaping is not yet an Arm S axis.
  • H2c on SPS is not tuned for those rows (its model takes v and t_rise from the bench bundle); its SPS s68 (2.0–2.7°) is not a statement about hits at SPS.
  • On SPS the waveform fit is the calibration of v, so the SPS numbers are hits relative to W; W's own known-angle accuracy (0.98 ± 0.03) is Paper I's, on CF₄ rows.
  • Arm S resolutions are on the model with exact truth: use its ratios and biases, not its absolute s68.
  • The near-normal Y excess (far strips early in data) is not in the model; its cause is a hypothesis (prompt capacitive coupling), not shown.
