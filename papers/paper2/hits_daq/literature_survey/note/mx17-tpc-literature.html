ATLAS NSW: tracks read steep, blamed on strip coupling; production avoids the µTPC fit

ATLAS New Small Wheel resistive Micromegas · Strip micro-TPC

Used for

Muon tracking in the ATLAS forward region; high rate, large area. µTPC is a test-beam and inclined-track option.

Drift gap

5 mm, about 106 ns

Strip pitch

0.425-0.45 mm, resistive strips over copper

σT / pitch

0.57-0.61 (derived)

Gas

Ar:CO2 93:7, 47 µm/ns

Electronics

VMM3 peak time; APV25 in earlier tests

Reference

set chamber angle, SPS H4

What they did

• Fit a line through one time per strip (APV25 rise time, VMM peak)

• Measured neighbour sharing: 10 % (simulated) to 67 % (longest strips)

• Simulated reconstruction with charge sharing on and off, with diffusion on

• Fixes: drop edge strips, position correction in earliest time, angle filters

What they concluded

• Bias to larger angles from capacitive coupling and edge strips, shrinking with angle

• “Adding CS pulls the time measurements more towards the mean time”

• With sharing off, simulation still peaks +7° high (tracks above 25°); cause left open

• Test beam at 28° peaks at the true angle, in tension with that simulation

For us: Closest published relative. The +7° without sharing is the nearest analogue of our “a metallic readout keeps most of it”, but they did not separate diffusion from geometry.

LMU floating strips: +5° at 10°, and the drift velocity comes out too large, as ours does

LMU floating-strip Micromegas (Bortfeldt, Klitzner) · Strip micro-TPC

Used for

High-rate tracking R&D; low-energy proton and ion tracking in Ne:CF4 (Klitzner).

Drift gap

6 mm

Strip pitch

0.5 mm, metallic, capacitive readout

σT / pitch

0.57 (derived, DT assumed)

Gas

Ar:CO2; Ne:CF4 80:20 (Klitzner)

Electronics

APV25, Fermi rise-time fit

Reference

set angle (10 ± 1)°; doublet 25.8 ± 0.4°

What they did

• Fitted a Fermi function per strip and a line through the times

• Simulated capacitive chains in LTspice forward

• Scanned reconstructed angle against drift field at four angles

• Derived an angle-dependent rescale, per sample rather than per event

What they concluded

• Reconstructed angle always larger than true, approaching it with angle

• +5° at 10° measured; +9° in the LTspice model

• Slopes too small, so velocities too large: our hit-ladder symptom

• Klitzner: fewer strips means more bias; but most Ne:CF4 layers read slightly small

For us: Same sign and same velocity symptom on metallic strips, so the sign does not need a resistive layer. Not a controlled comparison.

LMU GEM: the closest published correction, a 20 % neighbour subtraction per time bin

LMU triple-GEM µTPC (Flierl) · Strip micro-TPC

Used for

GEM µTPC development, including ions from 10B neutron converters.

Drift gap

8 mm

Strip pitch

0.4 mm

σT / pitch

0.82 (derived, DT assumed)

Electronics

APV25

Reference

set chamber angle

What they did

• Line fit through per-strip times

• Subtracted 15 % (simulated) or 20 % (empirical) of each neighbour, per time bin

• Compared angle resolution against drift field

What they concluded

• 19° at a set 15° (most probable); 32° at 30° (mean)

• After correction: 16.99 ± 0.04° at 15°

• First electrons occupy the full cluster width, so signals appear simultaneous

• Best resolution at the drift voltage with minimal diffusion

For us: States our mechanism in words, in one passage about short ion tracks. The per-bin subtraction touches the waveform but is a fixed-fraction correction, not a fit.

Why others don’t show it: four explanations, each right for a different family

Reconstruction axis

Partly right

Large TPCs use a charge-weighted time per row, not time slices. LAr has both; MIMAC slices still show end deviations. Charge-weighted time is untested on MX17.

Lever arm Λ

Main separator

Orders the families by 1-2 decades. Necessary, not sufficient: GridPix has no aggregation; LAr short segments are untested.

Same effect, other name

True for strip µTPCs

Capacitive coupling, charge sharing, edge strips; BEAST canoe shape. LAr: width and charge only. Pad TPCs: variance terms, weak.

Few check an external angle

Supported

Drift scale is checked to 0.1 % but track calibration absorbs a compression. Strip µTPCs use a set angle, ±1°. Per-track telescope slope: not found.

What Paper II can safely write

Safe wording

“Single-time-per-strip µTPC reconstruction is known to read tracks too steep, an effect attributed to capacitive coupling and edge strips. We measure it per track against an independent telescope, show that it is dominated by same-time mixing from diffusion and the prompt footprint with a further strip-end term, that it would persist on a metallic readout, and that a per-event forward fit of the depth-resolved waveform removes it.”

Add: we did not find this decomposition or such a fit in a limited search.

Do not write

• “First” for the effect or the convolution model

• “Only MX17 sees this”

• “Whatever the estimator” (charge-weighted untested)

• Any resistive-vs-metallic statement about NSW

• A direct numerical comparison with Scholer’s test beam

Must cite

• Bortfeldt, Flierl, Scholer, Vanadia, Sessa, Alexeev

• Adams 2018; MicroBooNE 2021; ProtoDUNE-SP

• T2K ERAM (RC, waveform model)
