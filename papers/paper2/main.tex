\begin{abstract}
\tbw{Abstract written last. Key points: a forward model of the strip signals (physical RC spreading of the resistive
layer, prompt footprint, diffusion), calibrated per chamber and run condition from the data alone, fitted to the
sampled waveforms; track angle and drift depth to $\sim$1\textdegree{} on five chambers in two setups (cosmic bench,
SPS beam at a known angle), against 2--2.6\textdegree{} for the production hit times and 1.2--2.0\textdegree{} for the
same model fitted to the hit lists alone. Why single hit times fail is the companion paper (IIb).}
\end{abstract}
