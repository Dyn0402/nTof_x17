\section{How far from the physics floor}\label{sec:floor}
\sectionstate{partial}{\texttt{rerun/model\_audit/ws\_floor} (F3, 2026-10-27); pass-1 resolutions (\texttt{rerun/paper1\_res}); bootstrap (\texttt{rerun/paper1\_floor})}
\intent{What limits the angle once the bias is gone, and how far the measurement is from it. Replaces the withdrawn
``$\sim$1\textdegree{} physics floor''.}
An electron-by-electron toy (cluster sizes and $\delta$-ray ranges, Magboltz diffusion, Polya gain, the measured footprint,
template, sampling, noise and ADC ceiling), fitted by the production estimator with the toy's exact mean model, gives a floor
of 0.30--0.35\textdegree{} (s68) at every angle up to 45\textdegree, on all five bench chambers. Transverse diffusion sets it:
without diffusion the floor falls to 0.02--0.09\textdegree, while electronics noise alone contributes at most
0.015\textdegree. The closed form $\sigma_\theta\approx D_T\sqrt{6(1+f)/(NG)}$, with $N$ drift electrons, gap $G$ and
$f=1/(1+\theta_\mathrm{Polya})$, gives 0.29\textdegree{} for chamber A. \tbw{Define the chamber naming once with Paper I.}

The measured resolution of chamber A, 0.99\textdegree{} (X) and 0.90\textdegree{} (Y) on pass 1, is about three times this
floor. Most of the difference is not understood: \tbw{bring in the remainder from the residual bootstrap, $0.59\pm0.10$\textdegree{} on chamber A X,
which no reference, X-copy or position structure explains.}

\paragraph{The depth-bin bias.} The waveform fit describes the charge column in bins of one sample, 60\,ns. Even with a
perfect model this degrades steep tracks and reads them shallow: in the toy the bias is $-0.08$, $-0.23$ and $-0.51$\textdegree{} at
25, 30 and 40\textdegree, and the resolution grows from 0.34\textdegree{} at 20\textdegree{} to 1.35\textdegree{} at 40\textdegree. With 20\,ns
bins the bias stays within 0.05\textdegree{} up to 45\textdegree{} and the resolution within 0.27--0.41\textdegree. The cause is that
when $v\,\Delta t\tan\theta$ approaches the strip pitch, a charge uniform within a bin cannot represent clustered ionisation; the
effect vanishes in the toy without clustering. On the bench it is small ($\sim-0.05$\textdegree{} at 20\textdegree), but it is the main term for
pairs at 30--45\textdegree, as at n\_TOF. A finer or interpolated charge grid costs about three times more columns and has not been
tested on data.
