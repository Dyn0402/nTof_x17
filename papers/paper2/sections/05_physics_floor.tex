\section{The physics floor}\label{sec:floor}
\sectionstate{partial}{toy closure, WFT \S12 (\texttt{18\_toy\_closure.py}, R\&D model)}
\intent{What limits the angle once the bias is gone: electronics contribute 0.02\textdegree; $\sim$1\textdegree{} comes from
the 0.3\,mm jitter of the charge centroid (diffusion). The floor is physics, not readout.}
\analysis{R6}{re-run the toy on the production model and the r06 kernel}
\tbw{Unmodelled X asymmetry (\texttt{xasym\_toy.py}, cloud\_basics \S43): the measured one-sided $\pm1$ copy
($0.13\times$, 114\,ns late) injected into det3 toys on the production model and refitted symmetric. Closure floor
0.018\textdegree, no bias. With the copy: angle bias $-0.051\pm0.005$\textdegree{} over the bench angle mix,
$\mp0.19$\textdegree{} at $|\tan\theta|<0.05$, turning over by $|\tan\theta|\approx0.2$; spread $+0.13$\textdegree{}
($+0.007$\textdegree{} in quadrature on 1.15\textdegree); position pulled 60--70\,\textmu m toward the copy side.
$\sigma_{0,x}=0.25$ halves the overall angle cost against 0.42 and cuts $\chi^2$/dof 17.8 $\to$ 6.6. On that footprint the
injection reproduces the measured head-on $a_Q$ ($+0.160$ against $+0.165$).}
