Ne_CF4_C2H6_80_10_10_<pressure>.gas  (canonical name used by RUN_CONFIG)
  = copy of the nColl=1 rung, grid 18000-52000 V/cm, 10 log-spaced points.
  Generated 2026-08-01 by mm_gasgen_quick.sub (cluster 13324562).
  Magboltz wall time 15.4 min (CERN) / 17.1 min (Saclay).

  nColl=1 is LOW statistics for alpha (expect several %, vs ~1-2% at the
  production nColl=10). It is used here because the avalanche is run with
  AvalancheMicroscopic, which tracks on the cross sections rather than on this
  table -- the table carries composition/P/T and gives alpha for the voltage-
  window prediction. The nColl=3 and nColl=6 rungs are still running; when they
  land, compare alpha against this file before deciding whether to redo anything.

  DO NOT reuse for drift velocity or diffusion: the grid was cut to the
  amplification range and does not cover drift fields.

--- MEASURED 2026-08-01, replaces the caution above ---
The nColl=1 statistics turned out to be far better than the "several %" feared.
Two INDEPENDENT nColl=1 tables exist for CERN_450m -- the 10-point grid used
here and the 4-point nc1p4 rung -- and their alpha values agree to:

    V_mesh    nc1(10pt)   nc1(4pt)     diff
      400        436.3      437.5     +0.28 %
      450        521.1      524.1     +0.58 %
      500        604.9      608.7     +0.63 %
      550        690.2      687.5     -0.39 %
      600        773.6      774.3     +0.09 %

  rms difference 0.37 %  ->  per-table sigma(alpha) ~ 0.26 %

Propagated: at G = 1e4 that is 2.4 % on gain, and with dlnG/dV = 0.018 /V
(measured on the prescan, 500->520 V) it is 1.3 V on the equal-gain map.

1.3 V is negligible next to the Penning bracket (r = 0.40-0.60), which moves the
map by tens of volts. Regenerating at nColl=10 would NOT meaningfully improve
this map. The grid restriction still stands: do not reuse for drift or diffusion.
