The bunches that get no per-bunch clock correction

n_TOF → DREAM slim campaign, 116 segments, 96,356 bunches · generated by slim_study/make_unfitted_report.py

They are empty PS pulses. 1,658 of the 1,660 bunches that never get their own (dab, dkb) delivered no protons — PKUP intensity below 10e10 and, independently, tflash = 0 on 100.0% of them, i.e. the n_TOF flash finder saw no gamma flash either. They are not parasitic pulses: the parasitic family is fitted 100.0000% of the time. Nothing is lost and nothing is recoverable, because there was no beam: the 4,424 DREAM triggers inside them (0.0517% of the campaign) are detector background, and 0 of them match an n_TOF candidate.

How a bunch loses its correction

clockfit.fit_perbunch fits a straight line dab + dkb·t per bunch and needs 20 DREAM triggers whose nearest n_TOF candidate already sits inside ±200 ns of the global map. Below that the bunch keeps the global (K, T0, arm) map only, and efficiency() drops its events from the quoted denominator.

The measurement below says the threshold is never the marginal thing it looks like. The two populations do not overlap: unfitted bunches hold 0–19 triggers, fitted ones 46–139. No bunch fails on match quality — every single one fails for want of triggers, and it has no triggers because the PS sent it no protons. DREAM's gate opens on the PS timing whether or not beam arrives, so an empty pulse still produces a burst: a couple of dark-count coincidences at ~22 Hz against ~1.2 kHz with beam.

What is inside an empty pulse

Measured on run_116/stat090_0014 × 224636, the worst segment (22.3 % of its bunches empty): its 418 empty-pulse triggers carry 2.82 slim hits each, against 16.8 for beam triggers, and the composition is the giveaway — WAL 2.80, PSS 0.017, LIQ 0.000 per trigger. The walls' apparent activity is SiPM dark counts in the ±1 µs window; the plastics and the liquids, which have no dark rate to speak of, see nothing at all. Match efficiency in those bunches is 0.0000 against 0.946 for the beam bunches of the same segment.

In 114 of 116 segments the unfitted count equals the empty-pulse count exactly. So clock_qa's 'bunches fitted' check is not measuring the clock fit at all — it is measuring beam availability, and its 4 WARNs are hours when the PS delivered 86–92 % of the pulses n_TOF was scheduled. Every PKUP bunch in a segment's range reaches the slim, empty ones included (verified on 224636 and 224603: 0 missing), so the fitted fraction is exactly the delivered fraction.

By-product: the fleet efficiency spread is the beam, not the clock

The campaign's 93.6–97.3 % efficiency range was previously unexplained segment-to-segment scatter. Most of it is the dedicated/parasitic mix, which runs from 9 % to 61 % across segments.

What was changed, 2026-08-10

Empty pulses are now dropped at the join, before the candidate pass reads anything, and the bunches tree carries has_beam and intensity_e10 for every bunch the sub-run touched — so the table is both the beam record and the record of what was filtered. An analysis selects a clean sample with has_beam and splits dedicated/parasitic with intensity_e10. clock_qa now asks 'bunches fitted' of the bunches that had beam, adds a check that no no-beam trigger survived, and reports availability and beam mix without judging them. It takes effect on a re-slim: files written before this date still carry their 0.0517% of no-beam triggers, and their frac_fitted is beam availability rather than a quality number.

What this does not rule out

  • Why parasitic pulses match ~6 points worse is not established here. Fewer protons means fewer n_TOF candidates per trigger, so a DREAM trigger is more often left without a partner above the singles threshold — plausible, unmeasured.
  • The two unfitted bunches that did have beam (run_77/stat090_0003 bunch 44, run_118/stat090_0019 bunch 1758) are the first bunch of their sub-run — a burst DREAM joined part-way through. 33 events; no evidence they generalise beyond sub-run starts.
  • The empty pulses themselves are a beam question, not a DAQ one as far as this study goes: intensity and tflash both come from the n_TOF beam record. Whether the PS skipped them, or n_TOF was scheduled a pulse it never received, needs the accelerator logs.
  • This says nothing about the 54 sub-runs that could not be slimmed at all (the ~0.982 ms association) — a separate, still-open question.
