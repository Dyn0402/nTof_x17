How far does the charge spread — and is X really different from Y?

det4 (mx17_E) in the SPS H4 test beam, run 53, flat mount · 17,809 (X) and 34,489 (Y) head-on tracks clear of dead strips · companion to the spatial-resolution note

A head-on track lights 2.36 strips in X and 1.79 strips in Y at the 5σ zero-suppression this run was taken with — a striking asymmetry, and almost all of it is the threshold, not the detector.

X clusters carry 1.4× the charge of Y clusters here, and multiplicity is steeply charge-dependent. Compare the two views at the same cluster charge and they land on top of each other: 2.98 vs 2.78 strips, kernel widths 608 µm vs 575 µm.

det4's charge spreading is isotropic to about 5 %, and the residual X excess is consistent with the chamber's known 0.45 mm X tilt, which also skews the X kernel (1.22 left/right, against 1.02 in Y).

What is being measured, and against what

Same run and same tracks as the resolution note. At normal incidence every drift slice arrives at the same transverse position, so the lateral profile is diffusion plus resistive spreading and nothing else — there is no drift-time ladder smearing it out. And the uRWELL telescope gives an external, sub-strip impact point (242 µm against a 0.78 mm pitch), so the profile is built against the true track rather than against det4's own centroid, which would be circular.

The headline number, and why it is not the one to quote

Multiplicity is not a property of the chamber — it is a property of the chamber at a threshold. A strip enters the cluster only if it clears zero-suppression, so a view with more charge automatically shows more strips. That is the entire X/Y difference here.

The kernels

The selection that makes X measurable at all

det4 only amplifies in bands. In the X view — the striped coordinate — a track near a band edge has dead neighbours, so its kernel is truncated and it would report a falsely narrow one. Every track here is required to sit 3.5 strips clear of any dead strip, which leaves two usable bands and 17,809 tracks. In Y the live run is 95 mm wide and the same cut costs nothing.

What this does not establish

  • The tails are cut off. Run 53 is zero-suppressed at 5σ, so everything below ~60 ADC is simply absent and the kernel beyond ±2 strips is a lower bound. The few-percent µs-slow surface tails measured in RAW (RAW_RUN71_REANALYSIS_2026-08-04.md) are not visible here. This is a lateral kernel at threshold, not the full response.
  • X and Y sample different parts of the chamber. The X bands are two ~10 mm strips of detector; Y spans ~45 mm of illuminated area. With a ~15 % in-band gain gradient, the 5 % residual X/Y difference is comfortably inside that systematic — the honest statement is "isotropic to within what this measurement can resolve", not "isotropic to 5 %".
  • The tilt correction is a subtraction, not a measurement. tan θX is taken from the earlier drift study; it is used to remove 130 µm in quadrature from X. It explains the X kernel's left/right skew (1.22) but has not been fitted here.
  • One gas, one gain point. Ar/CO₂/iso 95/3/2 at the run-53 operating point. Charge sharing is set by the resistive layer and diffusion, both of which move with gas and field; the campaign's own kernel work found the time-domain kernel gain- and drift-invariant, but that is a different quantity from this lateral profile.
  • These are hits, not waveforms. Legitimate here only because the mount is flat — at θ ≈ 0 there is no ladder to reconstruct. Nothing here contradicts RECONSTRUCTION_BASIS.md.
