MPGD conference visuals — setup renderings

Publication-grade 3-D views of the two test setups: the SPS H4 beam telescope in P2, and the Saclay cosmic bench.

What this is. Two rebuildable 3-D scenes, driven entirely from the run configs and the measured records — not from a drawing package. Every detector position, the P2 fan's exact shape and all 1280 of its pads, the measured beam spot, the muon angular acceptance and the trigger aperture come from files in this repository or on lxplus. Anything not measured is listed under “What is drawn but not measured” below and is stated in each figure's caption.

The P2 BASKET chambers are not drawn as rectangles: the fan is the annulus sector back-solved from the group's own Gerber-derived pad map (apex at pad (-33.69, -20.58) mm, bisector 30.074°), mounted bisector-vertical with the apex 765 mm above the table, and all 1280 pads are placed as their true rotated rectangles. Only sectors 3–6 of 10 are read out, and they are drawn brighter than the rest.

On P2 MID the pads carry the measured beam illumination (stage-22 n_tag, 15.1 M tagged tracks summed over the ten eff_nominal_1 sub-runs). Pushed through the mounting transform it lands at 250 mm above the table with σh = 28.6 mm — the documented numbers, reproduced by the scene rather than asserted by it.

Beam particles are drawn parallel (measured divergence < 0.5 mrad over 620 mm), uniform in height across the 186–311 mm trigger slab (that hard-edged 125 mm band is the external scintillator aperture, not the beam) and Gaussian horizontally.

Either slot takes an MX17 chamber (drawn with its 30 mm drift volume, the one real thickness in the scene) or a P2 BASKET fan lying flat; --slots p2,mx17 reproduces the mixed configuration that mx17_det3_p2_det1_overnight_6-27-26 ran. The uprights carry brackets at every rail level (bottom_level_z = 82 mm, then every 97 mm), which is what makes P1 and P2 read as levels rather than free heights.

Muons are sampled from the sea-level cos2θ distribution and kept only if they cross both 60 × 60 cm paddles — the bench's actual trigger. Over the 1.53 m paddle separation that leaves nothing steeper than about 15°, so the drawn tracks are near-vertical because the acceptance says so, not because they were drawn that way.

One chamber, exploded

The layer stack of a single MX17: 512 readout strips per view at the strip map's own 0.7785 mm pitch, crossed X and Y, the resistive strips over them, the micromesh 150 µm above (garfield_sim/mm_config.py), and the 30 mm drift volume with a muon in it. Each primary cluster drifts straight down to the mesh, so its arrival time measures its depth — which is the micro-TPC measurement the whole reconstruction rests on.

Animations

Turntables for the talk, and build-up sequences whose numbered stills can be dropped on successive slides so the setup assembles itself as you speak. Every frame comes from the same scene code as the stills above.

What is drawn but not measured

  • sps transverse alignment — Every SPS run_config carries det_center_coords x = y = 0 -- nominal, not surveyed. The uRWELLs and MX17 are drawn centred on the beam axis (250 mm above the table), which is how they were mounted, but no transverse survey exists to confirm it.
  • sps mx17 z — MX17 "Detector E" at z = 1155 mm is flagged PLACEHOLDER in the run config itself.
  • p2 mounting — Fan bisector vertical, apex up, centred, 130 mm from the lowest active point to the table top -- supplied by the P2 group, corroborated by the trigger aperture coming out horizontal, but not surveyed.
  • bench scintillator z — The 60 x 60 cm trigger paddles are not in any run config (they are hardware outside the DAQ geometry). They are drawn just outside the stack at z = -110 / +1420 mm, matching the engineer-package schematic.
  • bench structure — The bench uprights and rails are drawn for context and are not surveyed dimensions.
  • drawn thicknesses — Chamber/frame thicknesses are chosen for legibility; only the 30 mm MX17 drift gap is a real dimension.

Reproduce

cd mpgd26
../.venv/bin/python make_figures.py                # the whole still set
../.venv/bin/python make_chamber.py                # the exploded chamber
../.venv/bin/python make_anim.py                   # turntables + build-ups
../.venv/bin/python make_report.py                 # this page

../.venv/bin/python make_figures.py --draft        # fast, for framing checks
../.venv/bin/python make_figures.py --theme both   # + dark theme

# individual scenes, with all the switches
../.venv/bin/python make_sps.py   --views hero,side,beam [--mx17] [--envelope]
../.venv/bin/python make_bench.py --views hero,side --slots p2,mx17
../.venv/bin/python make_anim.py  --only turn_bench --frames 120

Layout: geometry.py holds every number and its provenance; meshes.py turns geometry into meshes; style.py holds the palette, materials, light rig and render harness; scenes_sps.py / scenes_bench.py assemble each setup; annotate.py projects 3-D anchors to pixels and sets the type; make_figures.py drives the deliverable set.
