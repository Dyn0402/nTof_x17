n_TOF: CERN's neutron time-of-flight facility
EAR1's horizontal line (left) and EAR2's vertical line (right)
  • 20 GeV/c protons from the CERN PS strike a lead spallation target — ~300 neutrons per proton, 7 ns pulses, up to 0.8 Hz

Motivation
n + ³He → the n_TOF search channel
Discovery channel: e⁺e⁻ tracks and their opening angle. Neutron capture is overwhelmingly a γ-ray process — a detector that only responds to a charged, back-to-back pair rejects that background by construction, instead of trying to out-shield it.
n_TOF's neutron beam onto a ³He target — an independent nucleus, an independent facility, from the same simplified picture: capture → ⁴He* at 20.58 MeV → γ / IPC / X17. Measuring that angle is what the Micromegas micro-TPC is built to do, next.

Micromegas micro-TPC · Reconstruction
Resistive strips share charge — the fit has to know it
~50% of the avalanche charge is shared to the first neighbour (45% X / 52% Y, ~70 ns delay)
Uncorrected, this compresses the drift ladder 20–30% (~4° too steep); "unsharing" removes the depth trend
Current basis: a waveform-first forward fit reaching σθ ≈ 1.0–1.1° — the physics floor.

Backup · ATOMKI
How ATOMKI measured it
  • A thin solid target (Li, tritiated Ti, or B) at the centre of a fixed five-arm array of plastic ΔE–E scintillator telescopes
  • Multiwire proportional chambers in front of each arm give a hit position → pair opening angle, not a 3-D track
  • ~7° angular resolution (FWHM), no charge/vertex reconstruction, fixed acceptance set by the five arms
A wider-acceptance, tracking detector — like the Micromegas micro-TPCs in this talk — is the natural next step. J. Gulyás et al., Nucl. Instrum. Methods Phys. Res. A 808, 21 (2016)

ATOMKI: detector and target specs
Target chamber24 cm long conductive carbon-fibre tube, 3.5 cm radius, 0.8 mm wall — deliberately minimal material
MWPC anode10 µm gold-plated tungsten wires, 2 mm pitch
MWPC cathodes0.1 mm Ag-plated Cu wires, 1.27 mm pitch, crossed for x/y; 3.5 mm anode–cathode gap; delay-line readout, 10 ns/tap; Ar(80%)/CO2(20%) at atmospheric pressure
MWPC resolutionΔΘ = 2° FWHM (position measurement alone) — the ~7° FWHM quoted for the full five-telescope system also includes target/chamber-wall scattering; the two numbers answer different questions
Solid targets (Li, B)evaporated onto a 10 µm thick, 50 mm × 5 mm Al strip, stretched between two 3 mm rods
The ³H target — our channel's mirror reaction: not evaporated like the others. ³H gas is absorbed into a 4.2 mg/cm² Ti layer evaporated onto a Mo disc — a solid, µm-scale, low-mass target. Their original 0.5 mm Al target holders were replaced after GEANT + data showed they caused shadowing/scattering background: they fixed it by cutting material, not adding shielding. Worth keeping in mind for the ³He capsule.
J. Gulyás et al., Nucl. Instrum. Methods Phys. Res. A 808, 21 (2016) (spectrometer) · A.J. Krasznahorkay et al., Phys. Rev. C 104, 044003 (2021), arXiv:2104.10075 (³H target)
